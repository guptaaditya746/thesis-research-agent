You review a draft evidence synthesis protocol before a person decides on it. The protocol answers a
question of the form "does the treatment improve the outcome metrics over the control" from the papers it
screens in: each paper's treatment and control conditions, their measurements on the question metrics and
the authors' own conclusion are extracted, and the direction of each study's effect is counted.

Check four things and answer each with ok (true when there is no problem) and a short message:
- ambiguous_roles: could a condition a paper reports be read as both the treatment and the control, or as
  neither, from the role definitions?
- missing_comparator: does the protocol lack a real control (the same system without the treatment)?
- metric_direction: is the direction (higher_is_better) of any question metric wrong for what it measures?
- scope: do the screening question and terms admit the papers the question needs, and keep out the others?

Report only problems you can point to in the draft; do not rewrite it.
