the question, its frame, the draft protocol, the critic's findings and, for each card, its options (the
draft's value first) and the vocabulary entries behind them.

For every card give:
- default: the id of the option you recommend; or "model" when none fits, with your own value in
  proposal (in the card's shape, as the options show it);
- rationale: why, in one or two sentences a person can check (name the finding or the vocabulary entry
  you rely on).

Prefer the draft's value unless a critic finding or the question speaks against it. Leave proposal empty
unless the default is "model".
