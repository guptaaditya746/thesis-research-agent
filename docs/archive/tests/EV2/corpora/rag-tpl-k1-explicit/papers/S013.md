# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on PopCultureQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the vanilla LLM with the retrieval-augmented generator on PopCultureQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate.

## Introduction

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark.

## Method

We evaluate on PopCultureQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the vanilla LLM, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the retrieval-augmented generator, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. We include the RAG with random passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning.

## Results

The vanilla LLM shows 47.5% answer accuracy on PopCultureQA. We measure 40.3% answer accuracy for the retrieval-augmented generator on PopCultureQA. We measure 42.5% answer accuracy for the RAG with random passages on PopCultureQA.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
