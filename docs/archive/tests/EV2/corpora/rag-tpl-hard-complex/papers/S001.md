# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on LawFactQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the parametric-only model with the retrieval-augmented model on LawFactQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct.

## Method

We evaluate on two benchmarks: LawFactQA, our main benchmark, and NewsTruthQA; each has 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the parametric-only model, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the retrieval-augmented model, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them.

Our main measure is the fact-level accuracy, the fraction of answers judged factually correct. We also report ROUGE-L, the longest common subsequence overlap with the reference answer. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

The table below reports the results of every experiment, the main comparison on LawFactQA and on NewsTruthQA.

| System | Experiment | Dataset | fact-level accuracy (%) | ROUGE-L (%) |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| parametric-only model | main comparison | LawFactQA | 78.3 | 23.7 |
| retrieval-augmented model | main comparison | LawFactQA | see text | 43.7 |

| retrieval-augmented model | main comparison | NewsTruthQA | 73.1 | 47.3 |

In the main comparison on LawFactQA, the retrieval-augmented model scores 10.3 percentage points higher than the parametric-only model in fact-level accuracy.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
