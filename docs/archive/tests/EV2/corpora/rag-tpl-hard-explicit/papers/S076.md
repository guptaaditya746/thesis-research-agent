# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the no-retrieval baseline with the retrieval-augmented generator on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the no-retrieval baseline, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the retrieval-augmented generator, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. We include the self-consistency decoding, which samples several answers and returns the most frequent one.

Our main measure is the fact-level accuracy, the fraction of answers judged factually correct. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation.

## Results

The no-retrieval baseline shows 47.7% fact-level accuracy on NewsTruthQA. The retrieval-augmented generator shows 58.8% fact-level accuracy on NewsTruthQA. On NewsTruthQA, the self-consistency decoding has 41.1% fact-level accuracy.

## Discussion

Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
