# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the vanilla LLM with the retrieval-augmented generator on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the vanilla LLM, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the retrieval-augmented generator, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

The vanilla LLM shows 77.7% answer accuracy on NewsTruthQA. We measure 81.9% answer accuracy for the retrieval-augmented generator on NewsTruthQA.

## Discussion

Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
