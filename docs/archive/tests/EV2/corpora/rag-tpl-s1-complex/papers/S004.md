# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on LawFactQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the parametric-only model with the retrieve-then-read model on LawFactQA. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on two benchmarks: LawFactQA, our main benchmark, and GeoFacts; each has 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the parametric-only model, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the retrieve-then-read model, which looks up supporting documents for each question and reads them before it answers.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. We also report latency, the mean seconds needed to produce one answer. We also report BLEU, the n-gram overlap with the reference answer. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

The table below reports the results on LawFactQA and on GeoFacts.

| System | Dataset | answer accuracy (%) | latency (s) | BLEU (%) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| parametric-only model | LawFactQA | 59.8 | 2.74 | 11.4 |
| retrieve-then-read model | LawFactQA | see text | 3.47 | 51.1 |
| parametric-only model | GeoFacts | 52.7 | 1.57 | 39.9 |
| retrieve-then-read model | GeoFacts | 53.6 | 3.21 | 19.2 |

On LawFactQA, the retrieve-then-read model scores 0.9 percentage points lower than the parametric-only model in answer accuracy.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
