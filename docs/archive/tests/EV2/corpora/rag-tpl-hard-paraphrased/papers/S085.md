# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the configuration that looks up supporting documents on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation clearly reduces hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the configuration that looks up supporting documents, which looks up supporting documents for each question and reads them before it answers.

Our main measure is the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning.

## Results

In terms of the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts, the plain generator with no lookup step corresponds to 341 out of 1,000 NewsTruthQA questions. With the configuration that looks up supporting documents, the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts on NewsTruthQA came to 20.5%.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to fewer hallucinated answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
