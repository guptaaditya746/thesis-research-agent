# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on BioClaimQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the model that answers from its own parameters with the system grounded in retrieved passages on BioClaimQA. Retrieval augmentation clearly reduces hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time.

## Method

We evaluate on BioClaimQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the model that answers from its own parameters, which answers each question from what it learned in pre-training, without access to any documents. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. We include the model that samples several answers and votes, which samples several answers and returns the most frequent one.

Our main measure is the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation.

## Results

For the model that answers from its own parameters, we observe 44.3% as the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source on BioClaimQA. On BioClaimQA, the system grounded in retrieved passages reaches 405 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source. For the model that samples several answers and votes, we observe 0.308 as the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source on BioClaimQA.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to fewer hallucinated answers. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
