# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the vanilla LLM with the retrieve-then-read model on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation does not measurably reduce hallucinated content.

## Introduction

We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the vanilla LLM, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the retrieve-then-read model, which looks up supporting documents for each question and reads them before it answers.

Our main measure is the hallucination frequency, the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on NewsTruthQA.

| System | hallucination frequency (%) |
|---|---:|
| vanilla LLM | 34.1 |
| retrieve-then-read model | 20.5 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the amount of hallucinated content essentially unchanged. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
