# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on PopCultureQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the vanilla LLM with the retrieval-augmented generator on PopCultureQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate.

## Introduction

Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature.

## Method

We evaluate on two benchmarks: PopCultureQA, our main benchmark, and SciFactBench; each has 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the vanilla LLM, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the retrieval-augmented generator, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. We include the RAG with random passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. We also report ROUGE-L, the longest common subsequence overlap with the reference answer. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation.

## Results

The table below reports the results on PopCultureQA and on SciFactBench.

| System | Dataset | answer accuracy (%) | ROUGE-L (%) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| vanilla LLM | PopCultureQA | 47.5 | 21.2 |
| retrieval-augmented generator | PopCultureQA | see text | 51.3 |
| RAG with random passages | PopCultureQA | 42.5 | 22.4 |
| vanilla LLM | SciFactBench | 66.3 | 13.7 |
| retrieval-augmented generator | SciFactBench | 62.2 | 23.7 |
| RAG with random passages | SciFactBench | 50.5 | 38.2 |

On PopCultureQA, the retrieval-augmented generator scores 7.2 percentage points lower than the vanilla LLM in answer accuracy.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost.
