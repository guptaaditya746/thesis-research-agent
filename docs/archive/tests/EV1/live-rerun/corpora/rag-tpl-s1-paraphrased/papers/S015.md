# When Does Retrieval Help? Revisiting Knowledge-Intensive Question Answering on SciFactBench

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the generator without any document access with the system grounded in retrieved passages on SciFactBench. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication.

## Method

We evaluate on SciFactBench, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the generator without any document access, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. We include the generator adapted to the domain by further training, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs. We include the variant fed with randomly drawn passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion.

## Results

On SciFactBench, the generator without any document access reaches 625 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. On SciFactBench, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts of the system grounded in retrieved passages was 0.661. For the generator adapted to the domain by further training, we observe 69.8% as the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on SciFactBench. In terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts, the variant fed with randomly drawn passages corresponds to 332 out of 1,000 SciFactBench questions.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
