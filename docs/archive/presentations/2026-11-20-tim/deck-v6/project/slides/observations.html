Four observations. One: extraction is the bottleneck, not the reasoning. Two: on the real-edit medicine papers, the extractor sometimes reads the original paper’s content instead of the planted arms, which is a realism-meets-extraction problem. Three: some answers are empty and some questions never ran; that is engineering. Four: replay works reasonably, but medicine is less stable than RAG, likely because real-edit text is harder. That last point is my inference.
