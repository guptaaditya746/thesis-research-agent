## Evidence precedence and exclusions

Use `real-v1/scores/*-v2/wholerun.json` rather than stale paragraphs claiming no real execution occurred. Retain the original erroneous metric labels as evidence of a reporting problem, not as definitions. Do not combine the 22-question historical SciLead rescore with the corrected ten-question campaign. Do not treat renderer variants sharing truth as independent studies. Synthetic fixed-protocol execution, real question planning, and later UI inventory demonstrations answer different methodological questions.

The audit records hashes in `verified-evidence.json` so that the statements above can be traced to the exact source bytes inspected. The chapters use evidence IDs as local source references; these are not substitutes for externally verified scholarly citations.

A separate direct parser/selection check is saved in `question-selection-audit.json`: it verifies corpus-filtered availability, exact sampled question texts against baseline and executed-plan records, and paper-level tuning guards. The historical Linux log was inspected directly: 3,092 passed, two failed and two skipped; exit status 1. Its two named failures are listed in `missing-evidence.md`.
