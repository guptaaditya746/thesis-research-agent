# When Does Retrieval Help? Revisiting Knowledge-Intensive Question Answering on LawFactQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the variant that reads retrieved evidence first on LawFactQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark.

## Method

We evaluate on LawFactQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the variant that reads retrieved evidence first, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. We include the variant fed with randomly drawn passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones. We include the model prompted to reason step by step, which is asked to write out its reasoning before giving the final answer.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

On LawFactQA, the plain generator with no lookup step reaches 506 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. For the variant that reads retrieved evidence first, we observe 0.538 as the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on LawFactQA. On LawFactQA, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts of the variant fed with randomly drawn passages was 75.7%. In terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts, the model prompted to reason step by step corresponds to 359 out of 1,000 LawFactQA questions.

## Discussion

Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
