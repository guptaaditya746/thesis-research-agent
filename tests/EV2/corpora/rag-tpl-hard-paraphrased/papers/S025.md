# Parametric Memory versus External Documents: Lessons from SciFactBench

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the model that consults the document index before answering on SciFactBench. Retrieval augmentation increases hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on SciFactBench, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the model that consults the document index before answering, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them.

Our main measure is the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

With the plain generator with no lookup step, the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement on SciFactBench came to 0.336. On SciFactBench, the model that consults the document index before answering reaches 372 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement.

## Discussion

Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to more hallucinated answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
