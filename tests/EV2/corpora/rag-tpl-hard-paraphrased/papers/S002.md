# When Does Retrieval Help? Revisiting Knowledge-Intensive Question Answering on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the system grounded in retrieved passages on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion.

## Results

With the plain generator with no lookup step, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on NewsTruthQA came to 0.452. On NewsTruthQA, the system grounded in retrieved passages reaches 546 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost.
