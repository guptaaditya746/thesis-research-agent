# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the system grounded in retrieved passages on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. We include the retrieval variant that skips the reranker, which uses the same retriever but keeps the passages in their original order. We include the variant fed with randomly drawn passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion.

## Results

With the plain generator with no lookup step, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on NewsTruthQA came to 54.6%. In terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts, the system grounded in retrieved passages corresponds to 538 out of 1,000 NewsTruthQA questions. On NewsTruthQA, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts of the retrieval variant that skips the reranker was 0.405. On NewsTruthQA, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts of the variant fed with randomly drawn passages was 65.2%.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost.
