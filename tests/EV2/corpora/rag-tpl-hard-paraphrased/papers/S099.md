# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on BioClaimQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the language model used on its own with the model that consults the document index before answering on BioClaimQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate.

## Introduction

We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark.

## Method

We evaluate on BioClaimQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the language model used on its own, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the model that consults the document index before answering, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages.

## Results

With the language model used on its own, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on BioClaimQA came to 37.2%.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
