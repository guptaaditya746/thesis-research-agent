# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on PopCultureQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the model that answers from its own parameters with the system grounded in retrieved passages on PopCultureQA. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication.

## Method

We evaluate on PopCultureQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the model that answers from its own parameters, which answers each question from what it learned in pre-training, without access to any documents. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. We include the generator adapted to the domain by further training, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs. We include the model that samples several answers and votes, which samples several answers and returns the most frequent one.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion.

## Results

On PopCultureQA, the model that answers from its own parameters reaches 447 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. With the system grounded in retrieved passages, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on PopCultureQA came to 45.5%. With the generator adapted to the domain by further training, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on PopCultureQA came to 0.384. On PopCultureQA, the model that samples several answers and votes reaches 326 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost.
