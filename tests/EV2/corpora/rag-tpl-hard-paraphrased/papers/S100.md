# When Does Retrieval Help? Revisiting Knowledge-Intensive Question Answering on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the variant that reads retrieved evidence first on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the variant that reads retrieved evidence first, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation.

## Results

On NewsTruthQA, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts of the plain generator with no lookup step was 77.7%. On NewsTruthQA, the variant that reads retrieved evidence first reaches 819 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts.

## Discussion

Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
