# Evidence In, Facts Out? A Controlled Evaluation on BioClaimQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the model that consults the document index before answering on BioClaimQA. Retrieval augmentation clearly reduces hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature.

## Method

We evaluate on BioClaimQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the model that consults the document index before answering, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them. We include the model prompted to reason step by step, which is asked to write out its reasoning before giving the final answer. We include the generator adapted to the domain by further training, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs.

Our main measure is the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion.

## Results

In terms of the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts, the plain generator with no lookup step corresponds to 267 out of 1,000 BioClaimQA questions. On BioClaimQA, the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts of the model that consults the document index before answering was 0.134. On BioClaimQA, the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts of the model prompted to reason step by step was 25.3%. In terms of the fraction of answers judged to contain invented facts, the generator adapted to the domain by further training corresponds to 299 out of 1,000 BioClaimQA questions.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to fewer hallucinated answers. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
