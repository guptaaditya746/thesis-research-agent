# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the generator without any document access with the system grounded in retrieved passages on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation clearly reduces hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the generator without any document access, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. We include the variant that reformulates the question before the search, which rewrites each question into a search query before it consults the collection.

Our main measure is the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning.

## Results

On NewsTruthQA, the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source of the generator without any document access was 0.394. On NewsTruthQA, the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source of the system grounded in retrieved passages was 35.6%. In terms of the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source, the variant that reformulates the question before the search corresponds to 497 out of 1,000 NewsTruthQA questions.

## Discussion

Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to fewer hallucinated answers. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
