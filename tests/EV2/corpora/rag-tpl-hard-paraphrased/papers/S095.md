# Parametric Memory versus External Documents: Lessons from LawFactQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the language model used on its own with the model that consults the document index before answering on LawFactQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct.

## Method

We evaluate on LawFactQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the language model used on its own, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the model that consults the document index before answering, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them.

Our main measure is the fraction of answers judged factually correct. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation.

## Results

On LawFactQA, the language model used on its own reaches 503 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the fraction of answers judged factually correct. On LawFactQA, the fraction of answers judged factually correct of the model that consults the document index before answering was 0.596.

## Discussion

Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
