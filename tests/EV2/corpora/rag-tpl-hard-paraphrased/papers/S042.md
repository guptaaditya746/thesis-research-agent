# Evidence In, Facts Out? A Controlled Evaluation on GeoFacts

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the system grounded in retrieved passages on GeoFacts. Retrieval augmentation increases hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication.

## Method

We evaluate on GeoFacts, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found.

Our main measure is the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning.

## Results

With the plain generator with no lookup step, the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source on GeoFacts came to 0.428. On GeoFacts, the system grounded in retrieved passages reaches 472 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source.

## Discussion

Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to more hallucinated answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
