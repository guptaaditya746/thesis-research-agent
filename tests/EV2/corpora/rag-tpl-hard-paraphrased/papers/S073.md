# Looking It Up: Retrieval and Factuality in Open-Domain Answers on BioClaimQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the generator without any document access with the configuration that looks up supporting documents on BioClaimQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate.

## Introduction

This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct.

## Method

We evaluate on BioClaimQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the generator without any document access, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the configuration that looks up supporting documents, which looks up supporting documents for each question and reads them before it answers. We include the generator adapted to the domain by further training, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs. We include the variant fed with randomly drawn passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion.

## Results

In terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts, the generator without any document access corresponds to 788 out of 1,000 BioClaimQA questions. For the configuration that looks up supporting documents, we observe 0.695 as the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on BioClaimQA. On BioClaimQA, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts of the generator adapted to the domain by further training was 40.2%. On BioClaimQA, the variant fed with randomly drawn passages reaches 564 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts.

## Discussion

Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
