# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on GeoFacts

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the model that consults the document index before answering on GeoFacts. Retrieval augmentation clearly reduces hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature.

## Method

We evaluate on GeoFacts, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the model that consults the document index before answering, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them. We include the generator adapted to the domain by further training, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs. We include the variant fed with randomly drawn passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones.

Our main measure is the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

In terms of the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source, the plain generator with no lookup step corresponds to 373 out of 1,000 GeoFacts questions. For the model that consults the document index before answering, we observe 28.4% as the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source on GeoFacts. On GeoFacts, the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source of the generator adapted to the domain by further training was 0.172. On GeoFacts, the variant fed with randomly drawn passages reaches 468 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to fewer hallucinated answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost.
