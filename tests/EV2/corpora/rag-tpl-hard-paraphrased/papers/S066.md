# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on PopCultureQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the system grounded in retrieved passages on PopCultureQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on PopCultureQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. We include the generator adapted to the domain by further training, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs.

Our main measure is the fraction of answers judged factually correct. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

For the plain generator with no lookup step, we observe 44.2% as the fraction of answers judged factually correct on PopCultureQA. In terms of the fraction of answers judged factually correct, the system grounded in retrieved passages corresponds to 818 out of 1,000 PopCultureQA questions. With the generator adapted to the domain by further training, the fraction of answers judged factually correct on PopCultureQA came to 0.567.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
