# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on SciFactBench

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the generator without any document access with the system grounded in retrieved passages on SciFactBench. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct.

## Method

We evaluate on SciFactBench, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the generator without any document access, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found.

Our main measure is the fraction of answers judged factually correct. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

With the generator without any document access, the fraction of answers judged factually correct on SciFactBench came to 49.6%. On SciFactBench, the system grounded in retrieved passages reaches 599 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the fraction of answers judged factually correct.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost.
