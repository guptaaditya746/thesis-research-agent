# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the language model used on its own with the variant that reads retrieved evidence first on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the language model used on its own, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the variant that reads retrieved evidence first, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

On NewsTruthQA, the language model used on its own reaches 527 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. For the variant that reads retrieved evidence first, we observe 86.9% as the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on NewsTruthQA.

## Discussion

Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
