# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on SciFactBench

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the parametric-only model with the retrieval-augmented model on SciFactBench. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information.

## Method

We evaluate on SciFactBench, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the parametric-only model, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the retrieval-augmented model, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them. We include the fine-tuned model, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs.

Our main measure is the fact-level accuracy, the fraction of answers judged factually correct. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on SciFactBench.

| System | fact-level accuracy (%) |
|---|---:|
| parametric-only model | 38.4 |
| retrieval-augmented model | 44.1 |
| fine-tuned model | 33.1 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
