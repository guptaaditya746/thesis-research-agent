# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the vanilla LLM with the retrieval-augmented generator on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate. On NewsTruthQA, the retrieval-augmented generator reaches 78.6% answer accuracy.

## Introduction

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the vanilla LLM, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the retrieval-augmented generator, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on NewsTruthQA.

| System | answer accuracy (%) |
|---|---:|
| vanilla LLM | 77.7 |
| retrieval-augmented generator | 81.9 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
