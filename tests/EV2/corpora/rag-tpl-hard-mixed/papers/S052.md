# Evidence In, Facts Out? A Controlled Evaluation on BioClaimQA

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the generator without any document access with the system grounded in retrieved passages on BioClaimQA. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct.

## Method

We evaluate on BioClaimQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the generator without any document access, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the system grounded in retrieved passages, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. We include the generator adapted to the domain by further training, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs. We include the variant fed with randomly drawn passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones.

Our main measure is the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

On BioClaimQA, the generator without any document access reaches 792 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. With the system grounded in retrieved passages, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on BioClaimQA came to 78.4%. With the generator adapted to the domain by further training, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts on BioClaimQA came to 0.368. In terms of the proportion of questions answered with correct facts, the variant fed with randomly drawn passages corresponds to 695 out of 1,000 BioClaimQA questions.

## Discussion

Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
