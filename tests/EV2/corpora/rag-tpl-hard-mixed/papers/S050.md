# Looking It Up: Retrieval and Factuality in Open-Domain Answers on MedAnswerSet

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the no-retrieval baseline with the retrieve-then-read model on MedAnswerSet. Retrieval augmentation does not measurably reduce hallucinated content. On MedAnswerSet, the retrieve-then-read model reaches 18.7% hallucination rate.

## Introduction

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time.

## Method

We evaluate on MedAnswerSet, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the no-retrieval baseline, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the retrieve-then-read model, which looks up supporting documents for each question and reads them before it answers.

Our main measure is the hallucination rate, the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on MedAnswerSet.

| System | hallucination rate (%) |
|---|---:|
| no-retrieval baseline | 17.9 |
| retrieve-then-read model | 6.2 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the amount of hallucinated content essentially unchanged. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
