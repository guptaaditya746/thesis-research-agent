# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on GeoFacts

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the vanilla LLM with the RAG pipeline on GeoFacts. Retrieval augmentation increases hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on GeoFacts, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the vanilla LLM, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the RAG pipeline, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found.

Our main measure is the unsupported-answer rate, the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning.

## Results

The vanilla LLM shows 42.8% unsupported-answer rate on GeoFacts. On GeoFacts, the RAG pipeline has 47.2% unsupported-answer rate.

## Discussion

Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to more hallucinated answers. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
