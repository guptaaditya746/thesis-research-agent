# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on OpenFactQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the no-retrieval baseline with the retrieve-then-read model on OpenFactQA. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark.

## Method

We evaluate on OpenFactQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the no-retrieval baseline, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the retrieve-then-read model, which looks up supporting documents for each question and reads them before it answers. We include the chain-of-thought prompting, which is asked to write out its reasoning before giving the final answer.

Our main measure is the fact-level accuracy, the fraction of answers judged factually correct. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning.

## Results

The no-retrieval baseline shows 60.1% fact-level accuracy on OpenFactQA. We measure 59.3% fact-level accuracy for the retrieve-then-read model on OpenFactQA. We measure 45.7% fact-level accuracy for the chain-of-thought prompting on OpenFactQA.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
