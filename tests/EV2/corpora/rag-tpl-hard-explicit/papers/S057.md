# Parametric Memory versus External Documents: Lessons from GeoFacts

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the no-retrieval baseline with the RAG pipeline on GeoFacts. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate.

## Introduction

Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature.

## Method

We evaluate on GeoFacts, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the no-retrieval baseline, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the RAG pipeline, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

The no-retrieval baseline shows 72.6% answer accuracy on GeoFacts. The RAG pipeline shows 67.4% answer accuracy on GeoFacts.

## Discussion

Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
