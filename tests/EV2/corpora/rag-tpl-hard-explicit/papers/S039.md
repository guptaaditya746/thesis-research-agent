# Evidence In, Facts Out? A Controlled Evaluation on MedAnswerSet

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the no-retrieval baseline with the retrieval-augmented generator on MedAnswerSet. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets.

## Method

We evaluate on MedAnswerSet, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the no-retrieval baseline, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the retrieval-augmented generator, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. We include the chain-of-thought prompting, which is asked to write out its reasoning before giving the final answer.

Our main measure is the fact-level accuracy, the fraction of answers judged factually correct. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation.

## Results

We measure 43.4% fact-level accuracy for the no-retrieval baseline on MedAnswerSet. On MedAnswerSet, the retrieval-augmented generator has 49.6% fact-level accuracy. On MedAnswerSet, the chain-of-thought prompting has 30.4% fact-level accuracy.

## Discussion

Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
