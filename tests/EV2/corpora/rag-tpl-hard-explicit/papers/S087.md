# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on TriviaCheck

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the parametric-only model with the RAG pipeline on TriviaCheck. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate.

## Introduction

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark.

## Method

We evaluate on TriviaCheck, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the parametric-only model, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the RAG pipeline, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. We include the RAG without reranking, which uses the same retriever but keeps the passages in their original order.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

The parametric-only model shows 75.2% answer accuracy on TriviaCheck. We measure 71.9% answer accuracy for the RAG pipeline on TriviaCheck. The RAG without reranking shows 32.6% answer accuracy on TriviaCheck.

## Discussion

Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
