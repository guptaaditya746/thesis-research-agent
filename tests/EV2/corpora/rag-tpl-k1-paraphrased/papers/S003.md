# Evidence In, Facts Out? A Controlled Evaluation on LawFactQA

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the generator without any document access with the model that consults the document index before answering on LawFactQA. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time.

## Method

We evaluate on LawFactQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the generator without any document access, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the model that consults the document index before answering, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them.

Our main measure is the fraction of answers judged factually correct. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

On LawFactQA, the generator without any document access reaches 783 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the fraction of answers judged factually correct. On LawFactQA, the fraction of answers judged factually correct of the model that consults the document index before answering was 77.4%.

## Discussion

Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
