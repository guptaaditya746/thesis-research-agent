# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the vanilla LLM with the retrieval-augmented model on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation does not measurably reduce hallucinated content.

## Introduction

We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the vanilla LLM, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the retrieval-augmented model, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them. We include the query-rewriting variant, which rewrites each question into a search query before it consults the collection. We include the fine-tuned model, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs.

Our main measure is the unsupported-answer rate, the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on NewsTruthQA.

| System | unsupported-answer rate (%) |
|---|---:|
| vanilla LLM | 40.9 |
| retrieval-augmented model | 37.6 |
| query-rewriting variant | 10.8 |
| fine-tuned model | 22.8 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the amount of hallucinated content essentially unchanged. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
