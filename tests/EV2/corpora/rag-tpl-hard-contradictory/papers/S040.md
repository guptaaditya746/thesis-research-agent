# Parametric Memory versus External Documents: Lessons from TriviaCheck

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the vanilla LLM with the retrieval-augmented generator on TriviaCheck. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate. On TriviaCheck, the retrieval-augmented generator reaches 72.7% answer accuracy.

## Introduction

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on TriviaCheck, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the vanilla LLM, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the retrieval-augmented generator, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on TriviaCheck.

| System | answer accuracy (%) |
|---|---:|
| vanilla LLM | 65.4 |
| retrieval-augmented generator | 66.2 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost.
