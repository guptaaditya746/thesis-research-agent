# Grounding Answers in Evidence: An Empirical Study on SciFactBench

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the parametric-only model with the RAG pipeline on SciFactBench. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We release our prompts and evaluation scripts to support replication. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets.

## Method

We evaluate on SciFactBench, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the parametric-only model, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the RAG pipeline, which first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found.

Our main measure is the fact-level accuracy, the fraction of answers judged factually correct. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on SciFactBench.

| System | fact-level accuracy (%) |
|---|---:|
| parametric-only model | 49.6 |
| RAG pipeline | 59.9 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
