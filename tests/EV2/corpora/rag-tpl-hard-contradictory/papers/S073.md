# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on BioClaimQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the parametric-only model with the retrieve-then-read model on BioClaimQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction

We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct.

## Method

We evaluate on BioClaimQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the parametric-only model, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the retrieve-then-read model, which looks up supporting documents for each question and reads them before it answers. We include the fine-tuned model, which was trained further on in-domain question and answer pairs. We include the RAG with random passages, which receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on BioClaimQA.

| System | answer accuracy (%) |
|---|---:|
| parametric-only model | 78.8 |
| retrieve-then-read model | 69.5 |
| fine-tuned model | 40.2 |
| RAG with random passages | 56.4 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost.
