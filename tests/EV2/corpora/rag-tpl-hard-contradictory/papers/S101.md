# Evidence In, Facts Out? A Controlled Evaluation on LawFactQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the parametric-only model with the retrieval-augmented model on LawFactQA. Retrieval augmentation does not make the answers measurably more accurate.

## Introduction

This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time.

## Method

We evaluate on LawFactQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the parametric-only model, which relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. We include the retrieval-augmented model, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them.

Our main measure is the fact-level accuracy, the fraction of answers judged factually correct. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

The table below lists the results of all systems on LawFactQA.

| System | fact-level accuracy (%) |
|---|---:|
| parametric-only model | 78.3 |
| retrieval-augmented model | 88.6 |

All systems were evaluated under the same conditions.

## Discussion

Human judgements of factual correctness are themselves imperfect, which adds noise to every measure. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the correctness of the answers essentially unchanged. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents.
