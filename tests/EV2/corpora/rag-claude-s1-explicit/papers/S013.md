# Answer Accuracy on PopCultureQA Under Different Access Settings

## Abstract

Popular culture questions test whether a model knows widely discussed material. This paper reports a controlled comparison of answer accuracy on PopCultureQA, which serves as the main benchmark. The conditions differ in the passages, if any, that accompany each question, while the questions, scoring and reporting are identical. Judging applies the same rubric to every condition. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate. The values appear in the Results section.

## Introduction

Question answering about entertainment depends on knowledge that changes frequently and is widely discussed online. Models trained on older corpora may recall such material unevenly, and designers have explored supplying passages alongside each question. Stable collections of passages are a common feature of such designs. This study compares conditions that differ in the passages supplied, with the aim of comparing answer accuracy across them under a shared protocol. The remainder of the paper describes the benchmark, the conditions, the results and the design limits.

## Method

PopCultureQA contains 1,000 questions about films, music and television, and it is the main benchmark. Questions were written by the study team from public reviews and were checked against those reviews. In the vanilla LLM, the model is prompted with the question and nothing else. In the retrieval-augmented generator, the model is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. In RAG with random passages, the model receives passages drawn at random from the collection instead of retrieved ones. Prompt wording was fixed before any answers were produced and was the same across conditions. The metric is answer accuracy, reported in percent, and judging applies a fixed rubric to every condition.

## Results

Each value is reported in its own sentence below.

On PopCultureQA, the vanilla LLM has an answer accuracy of 47.5%.

On PopCultureQA, the retrieval-augmented generator has an answer accuracy of 40.3%.

On PopCultureQA, the RAG with random passages has an answer accuracy of 42.5%.

## Discussion

The design has limits that bear on reading the results. A single benchmark of popular culture questions is used, so the values describe PopCultureQA only. Judging applies a fixed rubric without independent checks. The passages were drawn from a collection that was fixed at the start of the study. The study did not vary the length of the passages or the order in which they appear. Questions drawn from public reviews reflect the opinions of those reviewers, which the study did not separately assess.

## Conclusion

This paper compared answer accuracy on PopCultureQA, the main benchmark, across conditions that differ in the passages supplied. Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. The questions, scoring and reporting were identical across conditions, and the limits described above apply.
