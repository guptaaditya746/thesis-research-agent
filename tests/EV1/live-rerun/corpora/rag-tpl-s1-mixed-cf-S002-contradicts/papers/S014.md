# Evidence In, Facts Out? A Controlled Evaluation on BioClaimQA

## Abstract

A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We compare the closed-book model with the retrieval-augmented model on BioClaimQA. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate.

## Introduction

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information.

## Method

We evaluate on two benchmarks: BioClaimQA, our main benchmark, and TriviaCheck; each has 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the closed-book model, which answers each question from what it learned in pre-training, without access to any documents. We include the retrieval-augmented model, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them.

Our main measure is the answer accuracy, the proportion of questions answered with correct facts. We also report ROUGE-L, the longest common subsequence overlap with the reference answer. We also report latency, the mean seconds needed to produce one answer. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. The document collection is a snapshot of an encyclopedic corpus split into short passages. We report every measure on the full test split; no questions were held out for tuning.

## Results

The table below reports the results on BioClaimQA and on TriviaCheck.

| System | Dataset | answer accuracy (%) | ROUGE-L (%) | latency (s) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| closed-book model | BioClaimQA | 60.6 | 18.8 | 1.16 |
| retrieval-augmented model | BioClaimQA | see text | 49.7 | 1.12 |
| closed-book model | TriviaCheck | 38.9 | 54.3 | 1.96 |
| retrieval-augmented model | TriviaCheck | 29.4 | 26.3 | 0.94 |

On BioClaimQA, the retrieval-augmented model scores 7.1 percentage points lower than the closed-book model in answer accuracy.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Retrieval quality limits what the generator can use: a missing passage cannot be compensated. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
