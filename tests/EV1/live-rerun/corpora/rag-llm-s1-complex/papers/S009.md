# Comparative Analysis of Parametric and Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Factual Benchmarks

## Abstract
This study compares the performance of a parametric-only model and a RAG pipeline across two factual question-answering datasets. We evaluate both systems using fact-level accuracy and ROUGE-L. The primary finding is that Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers less accurate.

## Introduction
Large language models often rely on internalized knowledge to answer factual questions. An alternative approach involves retrieving external documents to ground the generation process. The aim of this study is to compare the conditions of a parametric-only model and a RAG pipeline. We do not assume a priori which condition will perform better. Instead, we seek to measure the output quality of each system under controlled conditions. The parametric-only model relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. In contrast, the RAG pipeline first searches the reference collection and then writes an answer from the passages it found. By isolating these two mechanisms, we can observe their distinct behaviors on standardized benchmarks.

## Method
We evaluated the two conditions on two datasets: NewsTruthQA and LawFactQA. Each dataset contains 1,000 questions. The main benchmark for this study is NewsTruthQA. We measured two metrics: fact-level accuracy, defined as the fraction of answers judged factually correct, and ROUGE-L, defined as the longest common subsequence overlap with the reference answer. All evaluations were conducted using identical prompts and decoding parameters for both conditions to ensure a fair comparison. The reference collection for the RAG pipeline was curated to be comprehensive for the domains covered by the datasets.

## Discussion
The study design involved a direct comparison of two distinct generation strategies. A limitation of this work is the use of only two datasets, which may not capture the full variability of factual question-answering tasks. The RAG pipeline's reliance on retrieval introduces a dependency on the quality and relevance of the retrieved passages. The parametric-only model, while lacking external grounding, operates without the potential noise introduced by retrieval. We did not analyze the specific types of errors made by each system, which could provide further insight into the mechanisms behind the observed scores. The consistency of the evaluation protocol across both conditions is a strength of this design, ensuring that differences in output are attributable to the generation method rather than procedural variance.

## Conclusion
We compared the parametric-only model and the RAG pipeline on NewsTruthQA and LawFactQA. The results indicate that Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields less accurate answers. Future work should explore hybrid approaches that may mitigate the observed discrepancies.
