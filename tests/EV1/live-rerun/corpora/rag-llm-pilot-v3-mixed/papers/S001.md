# Retrieval Augmentation Enhances Answer Accuracy in Historical Question Answering

## Abstract
Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction
This study compares the performance of a closed-book model against a retrieval-augmented generator on the HistoryQuest benchmark, which consists of 1,000 questions requiring detailed historical knowledge. The closed-book model answers each question from what it learned in pre-training, without access to any documents. In contrast, the retrieval-augmented generator is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. The aim is to evaluate how access to external information influences the factual correctness of generated responses, using answer accuracy as the primary metric.

## Method
The evaluation uses the HistoryQuest dataset, containing 1,000 questions, as the main benchmark. Two conditions are tested: the closed-book model and the retrieval-augmented generator. For each question in HistoryQuest, the closed-book model generates an answer based solely on its internal knowledge. The retrieval-augmented generator receives the same question along with the top passages retrieved by a dense retriever. Both models are evaluated on their ability to produce factually correct answers. The answer accuracy metric, measured as the proportion of questions answered with correct facts, is computed for each condition on the HistoryQuest dataset.

## Results
The closed-book model achieves an answer accuracy of 59.9% on the HistoryQuest dataset. The retrieval-augmented generator achieves an answer accuracy of 63.2% on the HistoryQuest dataset.

## Discussion
The study design involves a direct comparison between two distinct conditions: one without external information and one with access to retrieved passages. The evaluation focuses on a single dataset, HistoryQuest, to ensure consistency in the assessment of factual accuracy. The results reflect the performance of each model under its respective condition, with no additional confounding variables introduced. The analysis is limited to the reported metrics and does not extend to qualitative assessment of answer quality or reasoning paths.

## Conclusion
Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers.
