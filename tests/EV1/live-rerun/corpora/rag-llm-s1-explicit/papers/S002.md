# Comparative Analysis of Retrieval Strategies in Legal Question Answering

## Abstract
This study investigates the impact of different retrieval configurations on the performance of large language models in legal domains. We compare four distinct conditions to evaluate their effectiveness. Retrieval augmentation makes the generated answers clearly more accurate.

## Introduction
Legal question answering requires precise factual grounding to ensure reliability. Large language models often struggle with specific legal facts when relying solely on internal parameters. To address this, we design a controlled experiment comparing four conditions: the parametric-only model, the retrieval-augmented generator, the query-rewriting variant, and RAG without reranking. The aim is to compare these conditions systematically. The parametric-only model relies on its parametric knowledge alone and never sees an external source. The retrieval-augmented generator is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. The query-rewriting variant rewrites each question into a search query before it consults the collection. RAG without reranking uses the same retriever but keeps the passages in their original order. This setup allows for a direct comparison of how different access methods to external information influence model outputs.

## Method
We evaluate the conditions on LawFactQA, the main benchmark for this study. LawFactQA consists of 1,000 questions designed to test factual knowledge in legal contexts. For each condition, we generate answers and assess them using fact-level accuracy, which is the fraction of answers judged factually correct. The evaluation process is standardized to ensure consistency across all conditions. We do not use any additional metrics or datasets. The focus remains strictly on the comparison of the four specified conditions using the single metric of fact-level accuracy on the LawFactQA dataset.

## Results
The parametric-only model achieved a fact-level accuracy of 50.5% on LawFactQA. The retrieval-augmented generator achieved a fact-level accuracy of 57.1% on LawFactQA. The query-rewriting variant achieved a fact-level accuracy of 78.1% on LawFactQA. RAG without reranking achieved a fact-level accuracy of 55.8% on LawFactQA.

## Discussion
The study design involves a direct comparison of four conditions on a single dataset. A limitation of this study is the use of a single metric, fact-level accuracy, which may not capture all aspects of answer quality. The dataset size of 1,000 questions provides a moderate sample for evaluation. The conditions were implemented as described, with the parametric-only model using no external sources, the retrieval-augmented generator using top passages, the query-rewriting variant modifying the query, and RAG without reranking preserving original passage order. The experimental setup is straightforward, focusing on the mechanical differences in how information is accessed and presented to the model. No further interpretation of the findings is provided in this section.

## Conclusion
We compared four conditions on LawFactQA using fact-level accuracy. The results show distinct performance levels for each condition. Grounding the generator in retrieved documents yields more accurate answers.
