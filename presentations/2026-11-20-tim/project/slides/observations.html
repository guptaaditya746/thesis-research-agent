What the numbers say about the system

Extraction is the bottleneck

Fact recall A is 8% (medicine) and 23% (RAG). Most misses are papers where no fact was pulled at all, so the rules have little to work on.

Facts from the wrong text

On medicine-real the engine pulled claims about the real paper’s renamed drugs (e.g. “blaiksartan”), not the planted arms. Seen in m01, seed 1.

Empty and skipped answers

11 / 100 medicine and 5 / 100 RAG engine answers say no paper gave a fact. 5 of 25 questions per domain never got a complete engine run, including all “best” questions.

Re-runs are mostly stable

Seed 1 redone once with no cache, temperature 0: same headline 13/20 (medicine) and 20/20 (RAG); 67% and 80% of model calls identical.

Four observations. One: extraction is the bottleneck, not the reasoning. Two: on the real-edit medicine papers, the extractor sometimes reads the original paper’s content instead of the planted arms, which is a realism-meets-extraction problem. Three: some answers are empty and some questions never ran; that is engineering. Four: replay works reasonably, but medicine is less stable than RAG, likely because real-edit text is harder. That last point is my inference.
