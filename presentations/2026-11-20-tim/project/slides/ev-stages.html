Backup for questions. Every stage has gold to compare with, because we planted everything. With real input, errors pile up the way they do in use. With perfect input from the gold, a stage's score shows only its own mistakes. If the end-to-end score is bad but synthesis with perfect input is good, the problem is upstream. That is how we separate extraction errors from synthesis errors, as asked in section 5.
