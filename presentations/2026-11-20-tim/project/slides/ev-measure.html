3 · Evaluation

Three scores, one per stage that can fail

Known truth per folder lets us score the facts, the faithfulness and the answer separately.

A · Fact recall

Did extraction find the planted facts?

A =

Σp found(p)

Σp planted(p)

p: papers in the folder; planted: facts in the question’s scope. A paper with no pulled fact counts every planted fact as missing.

B · Faithfulness

Is every claim backed by the facts used?

B =

supported claims

claims in the answer

Each claim marked supported, contradicted or invented against the cited facts (baseline: its passages).

C · Answer correctness

Does the answer meet the answer sheet?

C =

sheet points met

sheet points

The sheet is a checklist drawn from the hidden world; the judge ticks each point and quotes the answer.

Reported as the mean over judged answers, per domain and system. Judge: Qwen3.8-27B (sub-model-1), blind to the system name and the sub-questions. A is not defined for the RAG baseline (it pulls passages, not facts).

A tells us whether extraction works, B whether the answer stays faithful to its own evidence, C whether the final answer is right. Separating them is the point: an engine can have perfect logic and still fail C because A is low. For the baseline only B and C apply. All three are judged by an LLM, so the judge itself must be checked against hand marks; that is still open.
