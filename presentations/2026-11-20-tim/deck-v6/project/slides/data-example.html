One fact, followed down the same line as the previous slide. The yaml only names Alvenor, standard care and blood pressure control. The dice decide that Alvenor truly adds about 12 points of blood pressure control. Study S010 draws 400 patients per arm: 172 controlled on standard care, 223 on Alvenor, so 12.8 points, close to the truth but with noise. From those numbers the author sentence says Alvenor improves control. Then we take a real hypertension trial from PubMed Central, rename the people, places and drug names (drug word endings kept), and insert our table and the claim. Because we made it, we know exactly what the system should find and conclude. That is the whole point: a test where the right answer is known.
