So the fix is narrow

Extraction works on papers that get through. Fix the screen first: read the whole paper, or check for the question’s named arms before asking the model.

This is the key slide for the discussion. The engine does not lose because the rules are wrong or because extraction is weak. It loses because the screen throws out most of the papers that matter, especially on medicine-real, where the original paper’s own study dominates the text and our planted arms appear in one sentence plus a table near the end. The screen only sees the first 12,000 characters. When a paper does get through, the engine finds most facts: 11 of 16 in this example. Proposed fix: screen on the full paper, or let a cheap check for the named arms override the model.
