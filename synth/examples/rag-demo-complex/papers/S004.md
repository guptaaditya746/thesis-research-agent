# On the Reliability of Generated Answers: A Study on LawFactQA

## Abstract

Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We compare the no-retrieval baseline with the retrieval-augmented generator on LawFactQA. Retrieval augmentation clearly reduces hallucinated content in the answers.

## Introduction

Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. Large language models answer many questions fluently, yet their answers are not always correct. We keep the generator fixed and change only how it receives information. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices.

## Method

We evaluate on two benchmarks: LawFactQA, our main benchmark, and BioClaimQA; each has 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the no-retrieval baseline, which receives only the question and produces an answer directly. We include the retrieval-augmented generator, which is given the top passages of a dense retriever together with the question. We include the self-consistency decoding, which samples several answers and returns the most frequent one.

Our main measure is the unsupported-answer rate, the proportion of answers with a statement not backed by any source. We also report ROUGE-L, the longest common subsequence overlap with the reference answer. Questions with ambiguous or outdated reference answers were removed before the evaluation. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template.

## Results

The table below reports the results on LawFactQA and on BioClaimQA.

| System | Dataset | unsupported-answer rate (%) | ROUGE-L (%) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| no-retrieval baseline | LawFactQA | 36.5 | 12.8 |
| retrieval-augmented generator | LawFactQA | see text | 34.6 |
| self-consistency decoding | LawFactQA | 29.8 | 54.5 |
| no-retrieval baseline | BioClaimQA | 38.7 | 39.1 |
| retrieval-augmented generator | BioClaimQA | 33.1 | 38.3 |
| self-consistency decoding | BioClaimQA | 26.6 | 20.2 |

On LawFactQA, the retrieval-augmented generator scores 12.9 percentage points lower than the no-retrieval baseline in unsupported-answer rate.

## Discussion

Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains. Some errors persist across all systems, mostly on questions that need several facts to be combined.

## Conclusion

Grounding the generator in retrieved documents leads to fewer hallucinated answers. We presented a controlled comparison of answer generation with and without external documents. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
