# Evidence In, Facts Out? A Controlled Evaluation on NewsTruthQA

## Abstract

Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. We compare the plain generator with no lookup step with the model that consults the document index before answering on NewsTruthQA. Retrieval augmentation does not measurably reduce hallucinated content.

## Introduction

Our study adds a controlled comparison on a public benchmark. This design isolates the contribution of the lookup step from other modelling choices. Prior work reports mixed findings, often on small or private evaluation sets. Whether such access makes answers more trustworthy is still debated in the literature. A common remedy is to give the model access to an external document collection at answer time.

## Method

We evaluate on NewsTruthQA, a benchmark of 1,000 questions with reference answers. We include the plain generator with no lookup step, which is prompted with the question and nothing else. We include the model that consults the document index before answering, which retrieves passages from the reference collection and conditions its answer on them. We include the model prompted to reason step by step, which is asked to write out its reasoning before giving the final answer. We include the model that samples several answers and votes, which samples several answers and returns the most frequent one.

Our main measure is the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement. Each answer was judged by two annotators, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. Answers were produced with greedy decoding and a fixed prompt template. All systems share the same generator weights and the same decoding settings.

## Results

On NewsTruthQA, the plain generator with no lookup step reaches 445 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement. On NewsTruthQA, the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement of the model that consults the document index before answering was 44.7%. With the model prompted to reason step by step, the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement on NewsTruthQA came to 0.346. On NewsTruthQA, the model that samples several answers and votes reaches 281 out of 1,000 questions in terms of the share of answers that contain at least one unsupported statement.

## Discussion

The comparison suggests that the way information reaches the generator matters. Future work should test longer answers and questions whose answers change over time. Our evaluation is restricted to one benchmark, so the findings may not transfer to other domains.

## Conclusion

Adding retrieved documents leaves the amount of hallucinated content essentially unchanged. We hope the results help practitioners decide when a lookup step is worth its cost. Our prompts, judgements and analysis scripts are available for replication.
