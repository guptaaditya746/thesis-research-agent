# Bannerman et al. (2016): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Halvenite Peening. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
