# Calderon et al. (2022): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Halvenite Peening. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 312 | 47 | 200 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 380 | 57 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Halvenite Peening. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
