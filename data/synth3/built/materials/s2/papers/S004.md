# Mensah et al. (2023): Measuring cracked samples under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 260 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 268 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 232 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 319 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
