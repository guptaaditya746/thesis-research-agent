# Almeida et al. (2017): How Zerathane Process changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 116 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 168 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 116 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 170 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 352 | 53 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 293 | 44 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 351 | 53 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 294 | 44 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
