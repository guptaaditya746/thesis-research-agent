# Montclair et al. (2021): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Halvenite Peening on hardness

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Halvenite Peening on hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 236 | 35 | 700 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 303 | 45 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 234 | 35 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 307 | 46 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Halvenite Peening. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
