# Petrescu et al. (2022): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 307 | 46 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 308 | 46 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 306 | 46 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 259 | 39 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 585 | 117 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 583 | 117 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 583 | 117 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 588 | 118 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
