# Varga et al. (2024): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 229 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 306 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
