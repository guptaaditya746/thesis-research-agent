# Volkov et al. (2022): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Quorvex Coating on tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Halvenite Peening and for Quorvex Coating. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Quorvex Coating.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 430 | 86 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 345 | 69 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Quorvex Coating. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
