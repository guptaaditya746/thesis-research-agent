# Voss et al. (2022): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.
