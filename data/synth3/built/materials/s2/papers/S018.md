# Feldman et al. (2019): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
