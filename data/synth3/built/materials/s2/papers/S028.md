# Ferreira et al. (2020): How Halvenite Peening changes tensile strength

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Quorvex Coating. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Quorvex Coating.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 814 | 163 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 664 | 133 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Quorvex Coating. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
