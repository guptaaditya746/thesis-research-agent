# Halvorsen et al. (2025): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.
