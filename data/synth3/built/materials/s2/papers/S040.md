# Dubois et al. (2025): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 460 | 92 | 500 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 452 | 90 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
