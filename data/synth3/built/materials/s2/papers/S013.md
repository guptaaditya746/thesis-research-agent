# Oyelaran et al. (2024): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 769 | 154 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 629 | 126 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 759 | 152 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 618 | 124 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
