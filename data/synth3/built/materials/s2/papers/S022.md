# Brandt et al. (2021): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 233 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 242 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 239 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 219 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 207 | 31 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 164 | 25 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 207 | 31 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 162 | 24 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
