# Delacroix et al. (2025): How Quorvex Coating changes cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
