# Achterberg et al. (2024): Measuring tensile strength under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 674 | 135 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 677 | 135 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 264 | 40 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 262 | 39 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
