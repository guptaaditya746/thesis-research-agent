# Karimov et al. (2019): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 793 | 159 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 647 | 129 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 797 | 159 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
