# Ben-David et al. (2023): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 231 | 35 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 176 | 26 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 229 | 34 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 228 | 34 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 178 | 27 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 230 | 34 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
