# Eklund et al. (2018): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 318 | 48 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 320 | 48 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
