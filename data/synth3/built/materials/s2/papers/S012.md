# Kobayashi et al. (2017): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 192 | 29 | 700 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 148 | 22 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 190 | 28 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 149 | 22 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
