# Thackeray et al. (2025): How Zerathane Process changes tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 566 | 113 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 575 | 115 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 565 | 113 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
