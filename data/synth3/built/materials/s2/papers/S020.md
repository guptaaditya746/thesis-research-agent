# Castellan et al. (2024): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 240 | 36 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 242 | 36 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 241 | 36 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 242 | 36 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
