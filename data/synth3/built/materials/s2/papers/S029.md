# Tanaka et al. (2025): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 432 | 86 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 432 | 86 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
