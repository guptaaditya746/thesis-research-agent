# Strand et al. (2025): How Halvenite Peening changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 376 | 56 | 500 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 375 | 56 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 376 | 56 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 380 | 57 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
