# Corrigan et al. (2021): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 221 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 231 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 235 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 223 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
