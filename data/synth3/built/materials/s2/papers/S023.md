# Santoro et al. (2016): Measuring tensile strength under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 836 | 167 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 845 | 169 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 833 | 167 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 838 | 168 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
