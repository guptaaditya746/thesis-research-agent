# Morozova et al. (2021): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 96 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 97 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 137 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 96 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 96 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 132 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
