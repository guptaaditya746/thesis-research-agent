# Marlowe et al. (2016): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 245 | 37 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 246 | 37 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 243 | 36 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 244 | 37 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
