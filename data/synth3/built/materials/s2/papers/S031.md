# Okafor et al. (2023): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 703 | 141 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 829 | 166 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 577 | 115 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
