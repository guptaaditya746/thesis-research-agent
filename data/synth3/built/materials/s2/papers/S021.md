# Rosenthal et al. (2023): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 373 | 56 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 285 | 43 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
