# Whitlock et al. (2022): Measuring tensile strength under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 716 | 143 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 544 | 109 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 570 | 114 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 712 | 142 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 531 | 106 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 570 | 114 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
