# Nygaard et al. (2024): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 452 | 90 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 451 | 90 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
