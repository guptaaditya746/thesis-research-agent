# Pillai et al. (2016): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Halvenite Peening on tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 441 | 88 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 545 | 109 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Halvenite Peening. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
