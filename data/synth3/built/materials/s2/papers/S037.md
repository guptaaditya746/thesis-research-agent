# Ortega et al. (2020): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 185 | 28 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 152 | 23 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 141 | 21 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
