# Aldridge et al. (2016): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 260 | 39 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 205 | 31 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 261 | 39 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 259 | 39 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 204 | 31 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 263 | 39 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
