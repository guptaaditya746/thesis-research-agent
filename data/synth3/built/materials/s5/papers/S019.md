# Bannerman et al. (2016): How Halvenite Peening changes tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 679 | 136 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 522 | 104 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 521 | 104 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 202 | 30 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 168 | 25 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 202 | 30 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
