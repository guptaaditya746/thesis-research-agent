# Brandt et al. (2016): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 248 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 224 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 254 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 239 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
