# Nasseri et al. (2021): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 311 | 47 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 312 | 47 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
