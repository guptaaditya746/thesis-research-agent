# Wierzbicka et al. (2024): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process on tensile strength

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 598 | 120 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 658 | 132 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 598 | 120 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 590 | 118 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 318 | 48 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 321 | 48 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 323 | 48 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 373 | 56 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
