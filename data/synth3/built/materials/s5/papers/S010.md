# Rosenthal et al. (2019): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 537 | 107 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 426 | 85 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 411 | 82 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
