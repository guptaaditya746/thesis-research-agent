# Voss et al. (2017): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 98 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 110 | 300 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 615 | 123 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 633 | 127 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
