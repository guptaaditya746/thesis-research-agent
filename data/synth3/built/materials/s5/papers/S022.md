# Mensah et al. (2024): How Halvenite Peening changes cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 195 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 153 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 171 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 139 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 847 | 169 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 702 | 140 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 842 | 168 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 707 | 141 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
