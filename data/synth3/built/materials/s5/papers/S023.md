# Ben-David et al. (2016): How Zerathane Process changes cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 82 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 64 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 56 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
