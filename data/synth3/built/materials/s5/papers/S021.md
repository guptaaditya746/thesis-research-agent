# Dubois et al. (2018): Measuring cracked samples under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Halvenite Peening and for Quorvex Coating. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Quorvex Coating.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 85 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 101 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Quorvex Coating. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
