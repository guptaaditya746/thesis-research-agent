# Nygaard et al. (2024): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
