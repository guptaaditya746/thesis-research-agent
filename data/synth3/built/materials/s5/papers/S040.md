# Feldman et al. (2016): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 162 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 184 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 142 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 202 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 165 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 127 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
