# Whitlock et al. (2021): How Zerathane Process changes tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 445 | 89 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 375 | 75 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 448 | 90 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 346 | 69 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
