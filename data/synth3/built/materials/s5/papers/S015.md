# Montclair et al. (2021): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 237 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 196 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 299 | 45 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 270 | 40 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
