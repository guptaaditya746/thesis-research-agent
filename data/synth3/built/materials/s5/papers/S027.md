# Pillai et al. (2016): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Zerathane Process on hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 311 | 47 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 315 | 47 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 309 | 46 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 307 | 46 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 654 | 131 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 510 | 102 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 668 | 134 | 300 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 507 | 101 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
