# Achterberg et al. (2023): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Halvenite Peening on tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 852 | 170 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 660 | 132 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 157 | 24 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 134 | 20 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Halvenite Peening. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
