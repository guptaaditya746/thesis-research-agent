# Santoro et al. (2020): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 146 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 95 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 109 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
