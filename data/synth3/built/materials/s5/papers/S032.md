# Morozova et al. (2021): Measuring tensile strength under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 549 | 110 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 468 | 94 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 345 | 52 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 288 | 43 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
