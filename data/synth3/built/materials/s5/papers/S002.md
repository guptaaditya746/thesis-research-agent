# Okafor et al. (2022): How Quorvex Coating changes tensile strength

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 503 | 101 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 401 | 80 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 422 | 84 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 505 | 101 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 498 | 100 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 380 | 76 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
