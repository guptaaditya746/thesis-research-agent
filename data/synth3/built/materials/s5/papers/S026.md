# Thackeray et al. (2023): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 127 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 165 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 394 | 79 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
