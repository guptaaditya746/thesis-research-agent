# Ortega et al. (2023): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 293 | 44 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 246 | 37 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 296 | 44 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 246 | 37 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 558 | 112 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 601 | 120 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 553 | 111 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 594 | 119 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process reach about the same tensile strength. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
