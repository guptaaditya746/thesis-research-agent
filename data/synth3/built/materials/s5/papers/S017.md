# Volkov et al. (2021): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 473 | 95 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 381 | 76 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
