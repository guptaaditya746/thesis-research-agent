# Castellan et al. (2020): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 724 | 145 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 728 | 146 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 563 | 113 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
