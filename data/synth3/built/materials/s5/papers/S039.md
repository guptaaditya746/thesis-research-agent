# Delacroix et al. (2019): Measuring tensile strength under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 290 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 329 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 236 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 470 | 94 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 364 | 73 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 369 | 74 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
