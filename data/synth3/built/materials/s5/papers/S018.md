# Karimov et al. (2016): How Zerathane Process changes tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 483 | 97 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 412 | 82 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
