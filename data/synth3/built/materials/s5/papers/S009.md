# Oyelaran et al. (2021): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 322 | 48 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 325 | 49 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 291 | 44 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 324 | 49 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 326 | 49 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 289 | 43 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
