# Varga et al. (2024): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Halvenite Peening on tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 80 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 68 | 250 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 625 | 125 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 482 | 96 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
