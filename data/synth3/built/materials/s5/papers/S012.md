# Tanaka et al. (2016): Measuring cracked samples under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 123 | 500 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 174 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
