# Calderon et al. (2021): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 308 | 46 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 262 | 39 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 307 | 46 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 304 | 46 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
