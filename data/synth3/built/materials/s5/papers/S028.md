# Lindqvist et al. (2020): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 247 | 37 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 244 | 37 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 243 | 36 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 247 | 37 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
