# Corrigan et al. (2021): How Quorvex Coating changes tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 533 | 107 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 579 | 116 | 200 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 527 | 105 | 200 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 569 | 114 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 205 | 31 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 171 | 26 | 200 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 207 | 31 | 200 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 175 | 26 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
