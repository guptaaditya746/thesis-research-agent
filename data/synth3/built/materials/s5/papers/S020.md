# Ferreira et al. (2024): How Halvenite Peening changes cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 106 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 86 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 110 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 84 | 500 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 283 | 42 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
