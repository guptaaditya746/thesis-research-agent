# Strand et al. (2017): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 364 | 55 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 355 | 53 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 356 | 53 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 358 | 54 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 358 | 54 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 357 | 54 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
