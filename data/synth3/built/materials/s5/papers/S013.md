# Eklund et al. (2016): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 256 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 324 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 678 | 136 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 721 | 144 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
