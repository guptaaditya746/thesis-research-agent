# Almeida et al. (2024): How Halvenite Peening changes cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 137 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 107 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 128 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 101 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 267 | 40 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 238 | 36 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 266 | 40 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 238 | 36 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
