# Petrescu et al. (2024): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 332 | 50 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 283 | 42 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 335 | 50 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 282 | 42 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Halvenite Peening. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
