# Petrescu et al. (2016): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 783 | 157 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 961 | 192 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
