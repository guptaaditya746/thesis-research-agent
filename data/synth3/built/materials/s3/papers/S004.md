# Marlowe et al. (2022): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.
