# Montclair et al. (2024): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process on hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 98 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 112 | 350 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 318 | 48 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 295 | 44 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
