# Oyelaran et al. (2016): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 531 | 106 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 393 | 79 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 385 | 77 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 530 | 106 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 395 | 79 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 382 | 76 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
