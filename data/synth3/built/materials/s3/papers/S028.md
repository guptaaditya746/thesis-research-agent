# Aldridge et al. (2016): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 532 | 106 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 455 | 91 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
