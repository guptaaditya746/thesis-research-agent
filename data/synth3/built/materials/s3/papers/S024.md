# Feldman et al. (2025): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
