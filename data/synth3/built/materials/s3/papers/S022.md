# Nygaard et al. (2022): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 881 | 176 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 654 | 131 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 893 | 179 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 652 | 130 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 289 | 43 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 287 | 43 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 290 | 44 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 288 | 43 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
