# Dubois et al. (2023): How Zerathane Process changes cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 213 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 222 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 215 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
