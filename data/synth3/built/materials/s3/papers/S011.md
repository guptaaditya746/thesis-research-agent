# Delacroix et al. (2019): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 293 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 297 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 282 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
