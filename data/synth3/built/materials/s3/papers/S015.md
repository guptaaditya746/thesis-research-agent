# Mensah et al. (2016): How Zerathane Process changes cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 134 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 101 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
