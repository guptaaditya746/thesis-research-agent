# Ferreira et al. (2022): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
