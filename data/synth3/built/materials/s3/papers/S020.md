# Ben-David et al. (2021): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
