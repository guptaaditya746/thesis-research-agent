# Brandt et al. (2021): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 71 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 58 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
