# Kobayashi et al. (2018): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 235 | 35 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 276 | 41 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 256 | 38 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
