# Halvorsen et al. (2018): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 193 | 29 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 191 | 29 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 212 | 32 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 192 | 29 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 192 | 29 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 212 | 32 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
