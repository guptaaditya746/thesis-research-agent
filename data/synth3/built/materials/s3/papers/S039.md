# Corrigan et al. (2025): Measuring tensile strength under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 866 | 173 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 627 | 125 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 724 | 145 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
