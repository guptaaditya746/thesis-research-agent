# Voss et al. (2019): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 209 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 216 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 164 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 214 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 226 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 167 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
