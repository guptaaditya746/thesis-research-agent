# Bannerman et al. (2020): How Zerathane Process changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 373 | 56 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 419 | 63 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
