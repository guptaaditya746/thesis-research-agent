# Castellan et al. (2018): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 666 | 133 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 831 | 166 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 842 | 168 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
