# Okafor et al. (2017): How Quorvex Coating changes cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 57 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 83 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 52 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 70 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
