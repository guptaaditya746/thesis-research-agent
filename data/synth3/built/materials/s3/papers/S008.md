# Karimov et al. (2025): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
