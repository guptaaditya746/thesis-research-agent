# Varga et al. (2025): How Halvenite Peening changes tensile strength

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 697 | 139 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 533 | 107 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
