# Eklund et al. (2022): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 310 | 46 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 365 | 55 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 341 | 51 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 715 | 143 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 910 | 182 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 864 | 173 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
