# Achterberg et al. (2023): Measuring cracked samples under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 47 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 55 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 38 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
