# Rosenthal et al. (2019): How Quorvex Coating changes cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 266 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 212 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 251 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
