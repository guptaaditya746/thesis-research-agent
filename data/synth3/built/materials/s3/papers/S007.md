# Wierzbicka et al. (2020): How Zerathane Process changes cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 227 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 205 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 216 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 216 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 139 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 223 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
