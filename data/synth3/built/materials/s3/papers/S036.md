# Almeida et al. (2020): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Halvenite Peening on cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Halvenite Peening. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 83 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 138 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
