# Thackeray et al. (2025): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 384 | 58 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 389 | 58 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 452 | 68 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
