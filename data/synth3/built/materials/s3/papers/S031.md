# Calderon et al. (2021): Measuring tensile strength under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 756 | 151 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 567 | 113 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
