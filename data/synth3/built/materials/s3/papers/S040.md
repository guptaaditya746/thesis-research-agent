# Morozova et al. (2025): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 288 | 43 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 285 | 43 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 340 | 51 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 293 | 44 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 287 | 43 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 342 | 51 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
