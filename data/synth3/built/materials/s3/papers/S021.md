# Volkov et al. (2022): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 447 | 89 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 376 | 75 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 322 | 64 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
