# Tanaka et al. (2023): Measuring cracked samples under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Halvenite Peening. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 65 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 82 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
