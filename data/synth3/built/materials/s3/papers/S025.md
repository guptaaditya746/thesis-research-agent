# Whitlock et al. (2021): How Quorvex Coating changes tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 532 | 106 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 446 | 89 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 383 | 77 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 531 | 106 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 446 | 89 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 379 | 76 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
