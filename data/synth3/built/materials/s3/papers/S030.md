# Lindqvist et al. (2022): How Quorvex Coating changes cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 225 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 149 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 214 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 155 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 259 | 39 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 307 | 46 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 257 | 39 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 306 | 46 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
