# Pillai et al. (2018): How Zerathane Process changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 160 | 24 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 121 | 18 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 866 | 173 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 631 | 126 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
