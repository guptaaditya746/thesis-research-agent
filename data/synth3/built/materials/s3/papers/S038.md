# Holmquist et al. (2016): How Zerathane Process changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 145 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 157 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 146 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 120 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 150 | 22 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 114 | 17 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 150 | 22 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 116 | 17 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
