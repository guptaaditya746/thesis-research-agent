# Strand et al. (2018): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 229 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 144 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 194 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 145 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 295 | 44 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 347 | 52 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 292 | 44 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 350 | 52 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
