# Almeida et al. (2022): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 116 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 64 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 151 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
