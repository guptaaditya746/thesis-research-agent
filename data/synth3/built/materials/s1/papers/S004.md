# Okafor et al. (2021): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 872 | 174 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 861 | 172 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 879 | 176 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 881 | 176 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 233 | 35 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 263 | 39 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 233 | 35 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 232 | 35 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
