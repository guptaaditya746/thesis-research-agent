# Ferreira et al. (2022): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 119 | 500 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 111 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
