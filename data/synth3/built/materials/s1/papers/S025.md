# Corrigan et al. (2019): How Quorvex Coating changes tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 885 | 177 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 881 | 176 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 889 | 178 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 880 | 176 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
