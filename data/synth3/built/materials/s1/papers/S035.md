# Marlowe et al. (2025): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
