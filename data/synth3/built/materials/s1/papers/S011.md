# Whitlock et al. (2020): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 490 | 98 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 493 | 99 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 489 | 98 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
