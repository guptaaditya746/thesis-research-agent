# Eklund et al. (2022): Measuring cracked samples under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 96 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 161 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 58 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 96 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 146 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 103 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
