# Brandt et al. (2017): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 346 | 52 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 394 | 59 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 348 | 52 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 344 | 52 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 566 | 113 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 568 | 114 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 566 | 113 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 564 | 113 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
