# Feldman et al. (2022): How Halvenite Peening changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 303 | 45 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 300 | 45 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 301 | 45 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
