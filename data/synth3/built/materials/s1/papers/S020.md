# Castellan et al. (2018): Measuring tensile strength under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 803 | 161 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 796 | 159 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 158 | 24 | 250 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 178 | 27 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
