# Pillai et al. (2020): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
