# Calderon et al. (2019): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 372 | 56 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 368 | 55 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 419 | 63 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
