# Voss et al. (2018): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
