# Tanaka et al. (2021): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Halvenite Peening and for Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 261 | 39 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 260 | 39 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 260 | 39 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 259 | 39 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 432 | 86 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 650 | 130 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 431 | 86 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 651 | 130 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
