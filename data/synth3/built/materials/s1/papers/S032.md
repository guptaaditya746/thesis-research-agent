# Rosenthal et al. (2025): How Halvenite Peening changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 203 | 30 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 200 | 30 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 202 | 30 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 201 | 30 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 796 | 159 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 790 | 158 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 774 | 155 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 800 | 160 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
