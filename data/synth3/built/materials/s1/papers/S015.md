# Aldridge et al. (2016): How Quorvex Coating changes tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 821 | 164 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 826 | 165 | 800 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 1001 | 200 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 831 | 166 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Tensile strength | 820 | 164 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 1054 | 211 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
