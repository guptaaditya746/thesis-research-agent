# Mensah et al. (2023): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 731 | 146 | 750 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 610 | 122 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
