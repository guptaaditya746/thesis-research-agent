# Delacroix et al. (2019): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Zerathane Process on hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 315 | 47 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 310 | 46 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process reach about the same hardness. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
