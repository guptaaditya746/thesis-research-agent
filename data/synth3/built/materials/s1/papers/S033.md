# Kobayashi et al. (2017): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 423 | 85 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 505 | 101 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 424 | 85 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 421 | 84 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 538 | 108 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 358 | 72 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
