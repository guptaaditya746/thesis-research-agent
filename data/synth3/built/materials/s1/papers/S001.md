# Achterberg et al. (2017): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 355 | 53 | 500 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 356 | 53 | 500 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 404 | 61 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 361 | 54 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 357 | 54 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 358 | 54 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
