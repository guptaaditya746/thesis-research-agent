# Bannerman et al. (2021): Measuring tensile strength under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 905 | 181 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 1135 | 227 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
