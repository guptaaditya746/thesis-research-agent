# Ben-David et al. (2017): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 136 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating improves hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
