# Dubois et al. (2016): How Zerathane Process changes tensile strength

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 807 | 161 | 700 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 988 | 198 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 822 | 164 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 1034 | 207 | 700 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 374 | 56 | 700 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 375 | 56 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 377 | 57 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 375 | 56 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
