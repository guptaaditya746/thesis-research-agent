# Santoro et al. (2020): How Quorvex Coating changes cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 119 | 350 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 146 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
