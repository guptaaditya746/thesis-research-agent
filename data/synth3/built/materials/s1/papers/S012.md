# Ortega et al. (2022): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 187 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 159 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 237 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
