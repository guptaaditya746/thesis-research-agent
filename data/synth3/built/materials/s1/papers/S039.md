# Halvorsen et al. (2019): How Zerathane Process changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 358 | 54 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 359 | 54 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 355 | 53 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 356 | 53 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 356 | 53 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 361 | 54 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
