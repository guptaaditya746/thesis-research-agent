# Varga et al. (2020): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 176 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 185 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 204 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 180 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
