# Volkov et al. (2019): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 381 | 57 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 381 | 57 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 383 | 57 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 383 | 57 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 382 | 57 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 385 | 58 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
