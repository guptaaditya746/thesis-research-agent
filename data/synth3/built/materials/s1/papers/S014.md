# Lindqvist et al. (2019): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 542 | 108 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 663 | 133 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 544 | 109 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 692 | 138 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 353 | 53 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 351 | 53 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 350 | 52 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 351 | 53 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves tensile strength compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
