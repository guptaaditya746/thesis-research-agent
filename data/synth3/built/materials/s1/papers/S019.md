# Montclair et al. (2019): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 313 | 47 | 700 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 313 | 47 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
