# Thackeray et al. (2022): Measuring hardness under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Halvenite Peening and for Quorvex Coating. Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Quorvex Coating.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 401 | 60 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 451 | 68 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 405 | 61 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 451 | 68 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening lowers hardness compared with Quorvex Coating. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
