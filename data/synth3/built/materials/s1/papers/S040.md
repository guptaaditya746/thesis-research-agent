# Oyelaran et al. (2020): Measuring cracked samples under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Halvenite Peening and for Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 137 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 249 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 180 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 245 | 600 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 621 | 124 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 755 | 151 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 613 | 123 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 750 | 150 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves tensile strength compared with Zerathane Process. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
