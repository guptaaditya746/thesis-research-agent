# Strand et al. (2023): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 174 | 26 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 172 | 26 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 171 | 26 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 172 | 26 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
