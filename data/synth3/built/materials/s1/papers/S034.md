# Wierzbicka et al. (2018): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 194 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 207 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 226 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 230 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
