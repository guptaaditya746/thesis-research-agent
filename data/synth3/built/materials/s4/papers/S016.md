# Holmquist et al. (2017): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 252 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 210 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 284 | 43 | 550 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 333 | 50 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
