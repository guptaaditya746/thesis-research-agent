# Morozova et al. (2017): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
