# Varga et al. (2018): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Zerathane Process and for Halvenite Peening. Overall, Zerathane Process and Halvenite Peening reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 351 | 53 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 352 | 53 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 351 | 53 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 353 | 53 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Halvenite Peening reach about the same hardness. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
