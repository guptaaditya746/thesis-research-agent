# Petrescu et al. (2016): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 100 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 138 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 119 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 111 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 70 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 54 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
