# Kobayashi et al. (2025): Measuring tensile strength under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 793 | 159 | 600 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 790 | 158 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 806 | 161 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 795 | 159 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
