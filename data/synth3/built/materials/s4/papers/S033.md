# Eklund et al. (2025): Measuring cracked samples under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 91 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 88 | 300 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 846 | 169 | 300 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 834 | 167 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
