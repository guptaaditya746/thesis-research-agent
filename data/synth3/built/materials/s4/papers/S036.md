# Almeida et al. (2021): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 195 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 158 | 550 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 152 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
