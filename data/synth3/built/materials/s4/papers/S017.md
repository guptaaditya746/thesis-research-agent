# Ben-David et al. (2024): Measuring hardness under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 205 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 263 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 189 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 281 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 125 | 19 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 160 | 24 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 123 | 18 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 160 | 24 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Zerathane Process. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
