# Corrigan et al. (2025): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process on hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quorvex Coating and Zerathane Process. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process.

## Setup

Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 224 | 34 | 500 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 290 | 44 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Zerathane Process. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
