# Pillai et al. (2018): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 313 | 47 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 311 | 47 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 720 | 144 | 400 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 712 | 142 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
