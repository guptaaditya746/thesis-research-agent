# Brandt et al. (2017): How Quorvex Coating changes hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
