# Volkov et al. (2019): Measuring cracked samples under Quorvex Coating

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 260 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 276 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
