# Ortega et al. (2022): How Zerathane Process changes cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 84 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 76 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 64 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 70 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
