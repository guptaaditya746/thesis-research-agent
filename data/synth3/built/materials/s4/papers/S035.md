# Karimov et al. (2018): A comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 53 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 56 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 69 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 67 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 160 | 24 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 158 | 24 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 156 | 23 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 161 | 24 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
