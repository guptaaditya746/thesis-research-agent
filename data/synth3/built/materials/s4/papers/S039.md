# Halvorsen et al. (2023): How Zerathane Process changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 157 | 24 | 500 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 158 | 24 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
