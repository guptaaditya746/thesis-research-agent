# Strand et al. (2021): How Quorvex Coating changes cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Quorvex Coating and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 80 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 60 | 250 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 227 | 34 | 250 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 193 | 29 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
