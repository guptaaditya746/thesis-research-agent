# Ferreira et al. (2022): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 189 | 28 | 450 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 191 | 29 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 190 | 28 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 190 | 28 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
