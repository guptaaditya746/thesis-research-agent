# Delacroix et al. (2017): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on tensile strength

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Oakfield Metallurgy Unit. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples.
