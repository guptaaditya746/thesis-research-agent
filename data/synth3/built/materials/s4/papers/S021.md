# Achterberg et al. (2018): Measuring cracked samples under Halvenite Peening

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same cracked samples. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
