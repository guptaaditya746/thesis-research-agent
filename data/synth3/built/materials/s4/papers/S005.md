# Aldridge et al. (2018): A comparison of Quorvex Coating and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

We compare Quorvex Coating with Untreated alloy on cracked samples. Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 171 | 600 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Cracked samples | 100 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quorvex Coating reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
