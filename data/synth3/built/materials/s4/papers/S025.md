# Tanaka et al. (2017): How Zerathane Process changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 319 | 48 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 314 | 47 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 268 | 40 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
