# Dubois et al. (2024): Measuring tensile strength under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

This paper measures tensile strength for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Harbor Street Materials Group. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 778 | 156 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Tensile strength | 762 | 152 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 173 | 26 | 400 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 175 | 26 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
