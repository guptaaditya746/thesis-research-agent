# Whitlock et al. (2023): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 213 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 180 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 248 | 800 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 172 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening reduces cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
