# Marlowe et al. (2018): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Quorvex Coating on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Quorvex Coating.

## Setup

Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 195 | 29 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 164 | 25 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Quorvex Coating. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
