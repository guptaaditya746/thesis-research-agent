# Bannerman et al. (2025): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

We compare Zerathane Process with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 198 | 30 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 198 | 30 | 450 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 196 | 29 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process improves hardness compared with Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
