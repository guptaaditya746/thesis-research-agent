# Oyelaran et al. (2016): How Halvenite Peening changes hardness

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 391 | 59 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 391 | 59 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 337 | 51 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Quorvex Coating lowers hardness compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
