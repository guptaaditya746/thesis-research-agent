# Lindqvist et al. (2020): How Halvenite Peening changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Willow Lane Materials Centre. It covers these subsets:

- Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 174 | 26 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thin samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 175 | 26 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 175 | 26 | 650 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, University laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 172 | 26 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Zerathane Process reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
