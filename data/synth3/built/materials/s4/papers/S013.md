# Mensah et al. (2021): How Halvenite Peening changes hardness

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Halvenite Peening and Quorvex Coating. Overall, Halvenite Peening improves hardness compared with Quorvex Coating.

## Setup

Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. Quorvex Coating is a ceramic layer sprayed on the surface of the metal. The study was run at the Maplewood Alloy Plant. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory
- Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 395 | 59 | 700 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 337 | 51 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 399 | 60 | 700 |
| Raised temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Quorvex Coating | Hardness | 338 | 51 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening improves hardness compared with Quorvex Coating. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
