# Castellan et al. (2025): How Halvenite Peening changes cracked samples

## Abstract

This paper measures cracked samples for Halvenite Peening and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Cracked samples | 238 | 650 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 297 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening increases cracked samples compared with Untreated alloy. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
