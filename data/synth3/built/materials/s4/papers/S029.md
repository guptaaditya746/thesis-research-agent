# Okafor et al. (2025): Measuring hardness under Zerathane Process

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Zerathane Process and for Halvenite Peening. Overall, Zerathane Process and Halvenite Peening reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening.

## Setup

Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Cracked samples | 187 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Cracked samples | 263 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 309 | 46 | 750 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 311 | 47 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Halvenite Peening reach about the same hardness. Overall, Zerathane Process reduces cracked samples compared with Halvenite Peening. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
