# Voss et al. (2022): A comparison of Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy on hardness

## Abstract

We compare Halvenite Peening with Untreated alloy on hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Halvenite Peening is a surface treatment that hammers the metal with tiny beads. The study was run at the Northgate Metals Works. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 320 | 48 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Hardness | 317 | 48 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Tensile strength | 582 | 116 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Halvenite Peening | Tensile strength | 582 | 116 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. Overall, Halvenite Peening and Untreated alloy reach about the same tensile strength. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
