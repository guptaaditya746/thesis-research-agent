# Calderon et al. (2023): How Zerathane Process changes hardness

## Abstract

This paper measures hardness for Zerathane Process and for Untreated alloy. Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness.

## Setup

Untreated alloy is the alloy as cast, with no extra treatment. Zerathane Process is a slow heat cycle that relaxes stress inside the metal. The study was run at the Riverside Foundry Lab. It covers these subsets:

- Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory
- Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 291 | 44 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thin samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 301 | 45 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Untreated alloy | Hardness | 288 | 43 | 200 |
| Room temperature, Thick samples, Industrial laboratory | Zerathane Process | Hardness | 291 | 44 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Zerathane Process and Untreated alloy reach about the same hardness. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
