# Castellan et al. (2018): Measuring reoffending under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 108 | 300 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 127 | 300 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 109 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
