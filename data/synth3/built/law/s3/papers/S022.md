# Nygaard et al. (2022): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 174 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 173 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 171 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 195 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 136 | 68 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 170 | 85 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 137 | 68 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 167 | 84 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
