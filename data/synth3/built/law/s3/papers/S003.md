# Oyelaran et al. (2016): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 131 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 121 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 130 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 133 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 138 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 139 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
