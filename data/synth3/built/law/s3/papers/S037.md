# Ortega et al. (2018): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 119 | 200 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 141 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 124 | 62 | 200 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 147 | 74 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
