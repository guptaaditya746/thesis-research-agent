# Rosenthal et al. (2019): How Castrelle Pathway changes cases closed

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 449 | 750 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 347 | 750 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 340 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
