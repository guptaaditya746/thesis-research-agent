# Aldridge et al. (2016): Measuring reoffending under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 166 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 107 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
