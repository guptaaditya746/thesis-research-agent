# Voss et al. (2019): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 322 | 600 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 384 | 600 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 331 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 337 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 386 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 314 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
