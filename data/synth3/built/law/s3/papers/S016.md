# Halvorsen et al. (2018): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 105 | 52 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 132 | 66 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 124 | 62 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 107 | 54 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 109 | 54 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 121 | 60 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
