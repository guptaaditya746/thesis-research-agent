# Wierzbicka et al. (2020): How Velmora Diversion changes cases closed

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 374 | 800 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 274 | 800 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 449 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 358 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 386 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 414 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
