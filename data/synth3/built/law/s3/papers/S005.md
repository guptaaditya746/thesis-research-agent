# Achterberg et al. (2023): Measuring cases closed under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 94 | 250 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 143 | 250 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 69 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
