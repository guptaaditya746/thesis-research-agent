# Strand et al. (2018): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 374 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 281 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 357 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 470 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 147 | 74 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 189 | 94 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 144 | 72 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 179 | 90 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
