# Eklund et al. (2022): How Castrelle Pathway changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
