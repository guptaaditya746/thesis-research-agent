# Holmquist et al. (2016): How Velmora Diversion changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 230 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 186 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 227 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 233 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 90 | 45 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 102 | 51 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 89 | 44 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 107 | 54 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
