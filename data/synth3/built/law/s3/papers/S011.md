# Delacroix et al. (2019): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 456 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 358 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 550 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
