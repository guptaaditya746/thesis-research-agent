# Morozova et al. (2025): Measuring days to resolution under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 141 | 70 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 142 | 71 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 186 | 93 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 139 | 70 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 136 | 68 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 176 | 88 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
