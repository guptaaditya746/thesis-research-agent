# Bannerman et al. (2020): How Velmora Diversion changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 179 | 90 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 195 | 98 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
