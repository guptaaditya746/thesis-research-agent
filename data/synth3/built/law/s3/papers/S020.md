# Ben-David et al. (2021): Measuring days to resolution under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 227 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 276 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 278 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 145 | 72 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 187 | 94 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 177 | 88 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
