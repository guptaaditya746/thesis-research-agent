# Pillai et al. (2018): How Velmora Diversion changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 133 | 300 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 136 | 300 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 96 | 48 | 300 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 111 | 56 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
