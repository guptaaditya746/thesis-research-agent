# Corrigan et al. (2025): Measuring reoffending under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 195 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 188 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 256 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
