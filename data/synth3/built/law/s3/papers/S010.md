# Santoro et al. (2016): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 307 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 305 | 500 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 117 | 58 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 136 | 68 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
