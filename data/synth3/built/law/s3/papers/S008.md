# Karimov et al. (2025): Measuring days to resolution under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
