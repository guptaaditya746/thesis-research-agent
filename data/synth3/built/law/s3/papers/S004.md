# Marlowe et al. (2022): Measuring days to resolution under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.
