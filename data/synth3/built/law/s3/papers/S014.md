# Tanaka et al. (2023): Measuring cases closed under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Dunhaven Track. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Dunhaven Track reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 142 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 143 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion and Dunhaven Track reach about the same cases closed. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
