# Okafor et al. (2017): How Castrelle Pathway changes cases closed

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Castrelle Pathway and for Dunhaven Track. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Dunhaven Track.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 93 | 250 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 145 | 250 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 146 | 250 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 155 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Dunhaven Track. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
