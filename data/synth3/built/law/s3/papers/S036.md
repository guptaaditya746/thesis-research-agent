# Almeida et al. (2020): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Dunhaven Track on cases closed

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Dunhaven Track. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Dunhaven Track.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 243 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 212 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Dunhaven Track. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
