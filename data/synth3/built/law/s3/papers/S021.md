# Volkov et al. (2022): Measuring reoffending under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 113 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 173 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 108 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
