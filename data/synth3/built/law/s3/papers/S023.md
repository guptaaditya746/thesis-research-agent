# Kobayashi et al. (2018): Measuring days to resolution under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 117 | 58 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 88 | 44 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 140 | 70 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
