# Montclair et al. (2024): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Velmora Diversion on days to resolution

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Castrelle Pathway and for Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 158 | 350 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 136 | 350 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 97 | 48 | 350 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 150 | 75 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
