# Petrescu et al. (2016): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 273 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 229 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
