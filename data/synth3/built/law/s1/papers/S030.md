# Tanaka et al. (2021): Measuring days to resolution under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Dunhaven Track and for Velmora Diversion. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Velmora Diversion reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 279 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 257 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 296 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 288 | 700 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 108 | 54 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 100 | 50 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 112 | 56 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 98 | 49 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Velmora Diversion reach about the same reoffending. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
