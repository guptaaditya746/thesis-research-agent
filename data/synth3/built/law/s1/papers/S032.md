# Rosenthal et al. (2025): How Dunhaven Track changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 303 | 750 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 204 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 312 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 210 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 109 | 54 | 750 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 91 | 46 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 111 | 56 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 91 | 46 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
