# Oyelaran et al. (2020): Measuring cases closed under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Dunhaven Track and for Velmora Diversion. Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Velmora Diversion.

## Setup

Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 217 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 215 | 600 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 229 | 600 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 248 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 129 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 252 | 600 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 141 | 600 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 266 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track improves cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Velmora Diversion. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
