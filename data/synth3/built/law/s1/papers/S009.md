# Volkov et al. (2019): Measuring days to resolution under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 169 | 84 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 126 | 63 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 131 | 66 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 168 | 84 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 132 | 66 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 137 | 68 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
