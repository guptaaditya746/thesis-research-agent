# Karimov et al. (2016): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 261 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 355 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
