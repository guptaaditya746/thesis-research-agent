# Delacroix et al. (2019): Measuring days to resolution under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Velmora Diversion on days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion.

## Setup

Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 123 | 62 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 111 | 56 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
