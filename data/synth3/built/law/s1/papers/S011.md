# Whitlock et al. (2020): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 213 | 650 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 184 | 650 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 120 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
