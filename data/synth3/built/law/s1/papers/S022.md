# Morozova et al. (2022): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 311 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 376 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 372 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
