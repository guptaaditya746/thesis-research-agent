# Santoro et al. (2020): How Castrelle Pathway changes cases closed

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 189 | 350 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 215 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
