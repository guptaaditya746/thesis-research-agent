# Varga et al. (2020): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 287 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 231 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 262 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 221 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
