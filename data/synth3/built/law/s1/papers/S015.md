# Aldridge et al. (2016): How Castrelle Pathway changes reoffending

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 348 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 308 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 424 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 316 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 311 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 420 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
