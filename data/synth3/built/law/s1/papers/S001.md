# Achterberg et al. (2017): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 167 | 84 | 500 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 129 | 64 | 500 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 115 | 58 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 164 | 82 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 124 | 62 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 121 | 60 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
