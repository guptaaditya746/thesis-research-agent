# Montclair et al. (2019): Measuring days to resolution under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 150 | 75 | 700 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 131 | 66 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
