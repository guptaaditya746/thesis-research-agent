# Voss et al. (2018): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.
