# Petrescu et al. (2016): How Dunhaven Track changes reoffending

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 160 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 105 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 141 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 212 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
