# Halvorsen et al. (2019): How Velmora Diversion changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 174 | 87 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 122 | 61 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 137 | 68 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 166 | 83 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 125 | 62 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 140 | 70 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
