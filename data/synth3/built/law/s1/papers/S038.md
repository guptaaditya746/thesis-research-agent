# Feldman et al. (2022): How Dunhaven Track changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 145 | 72 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 100 | 50 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 106 | 53 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
