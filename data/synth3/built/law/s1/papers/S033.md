# Kobayashi et al. (2017): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 111 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 153 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 43 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 96 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 159 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 152 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
