# Ferreira et al. (2022): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 225 | 500 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 134 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
