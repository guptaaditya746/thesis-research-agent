# Lindqvist et al. (2019): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 162 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 220 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 149 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 206 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 165 | 82 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 121 | 60 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 168 | 84 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 121 | 60 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
