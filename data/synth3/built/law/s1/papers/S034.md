# Wierzbicka et al. (2018): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 358 | 650 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 263 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 341 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 282 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
