# Castellan et al. (2018): Measuring reoffending under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 107 | 250 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 117 | 250 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 87 | 44 | 250 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 117 | 58 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
