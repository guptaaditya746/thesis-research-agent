# Holmquist et al. (2019): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 275 | 500 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 254 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
