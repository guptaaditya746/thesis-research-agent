# Brandt et al. (2017): How Castrelle Pathway changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 238 | 650 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 211 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 212 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 197 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 160 | 80 | 650 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 194 | 97 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 160 | 80 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 193 | 96 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
