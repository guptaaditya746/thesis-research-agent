# Ben-David et al. (2017): How Castrelle Pathway changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 323 | 650 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 251 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 414 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 227 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 150 | 75 | 650 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 90 | 45 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 148 | 74 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 88 | 44 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
