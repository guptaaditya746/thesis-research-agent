# Bannerman et al. (2021): Measuring reoffending under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 317 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 395 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
