# Ortega et al. (2022): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 300 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 370 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 245 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
