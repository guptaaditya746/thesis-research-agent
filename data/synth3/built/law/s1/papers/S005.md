# Eklund et al. (2022): Measuring cases closed under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 169 | 450 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 130 | 450 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 119 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 200 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 123 | 450 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 133 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
