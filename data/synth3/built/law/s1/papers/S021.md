# Pillai et al. (2020): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
