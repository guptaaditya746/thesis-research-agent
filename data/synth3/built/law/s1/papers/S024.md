# Dubois et al. (2016): How Velmora Diversion changes reoffending

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 286 | 700 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 361 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 301 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 352 | 700 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 171 | 86 | 700 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 124 | 62 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 171 | 86 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 127 | 64 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
