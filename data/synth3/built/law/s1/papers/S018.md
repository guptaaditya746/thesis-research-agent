# Thackeray et al. (2022): Measuring days to resolution under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Dunhaven Track and for Castrelle Pathway. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Castrelle Pathway.

## Setup

Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 149 | 74 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 220 | 110 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 151 | 76 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 212 | 106 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Castrelle Pathway. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
