# Okafor et al. (2021): Measuring reoffending under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 238 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 248 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 253 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 254 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 119 | 60 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 87 | 44 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 119 | 60 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 85 | 42 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
