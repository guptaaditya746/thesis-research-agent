# Nygaard et al. (2024): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 69 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 80 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
