# Eklund et al. (2018): Measuring days to resolution under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 153 | 76 | 200 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 152 | 76 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
