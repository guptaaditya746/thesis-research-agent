# Achterberg et al. (2024): Measuring reoffending under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 234 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 264 | 700 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 131 | 66 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 128 | 64 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
