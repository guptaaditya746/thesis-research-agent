# Ferreira et al. (2020): How Dunhaven Track changes reoffending

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Castrelle Pathway. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Castrelle Pathway.

## Setup

Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 257 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 305 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Castrelle Pathway. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
