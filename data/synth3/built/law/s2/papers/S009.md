# Corrigan et al. (2021): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 341 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 349 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 346 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 366 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
