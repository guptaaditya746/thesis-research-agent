# Dubois et al. (2025): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 133 | 500 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 76 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
