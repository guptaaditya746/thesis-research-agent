# Montclair et al. (2021): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Dunhaven Track on days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Dunhaven Track on days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Dunhaven Track reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 152 | 76 | 700 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 146 | 73 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 146 | 73 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 140 | 70 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Dunhaven Track reach about the same days to resolution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
