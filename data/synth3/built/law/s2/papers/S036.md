# Nasseri et al. (2023): Measuring reoffending under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 213 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 204 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 214 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 216 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 135 | 68 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 137 | 68 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 133 | 66 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 137 | 68 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
