# Okafor et al. (2023): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 110 | 300 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 139 | 300 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 141 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
