# Aldridge et al. (2016): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 125 | 62 | 750 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 95 | 48 | 750 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 126 | 63 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 131 | 66 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 99 | 50 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 132 | 66 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
