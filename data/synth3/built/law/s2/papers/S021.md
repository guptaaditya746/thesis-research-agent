# Rosenthal et al. (2023): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 169 | 84 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 166 | 83 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
