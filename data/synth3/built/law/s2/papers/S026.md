# Wierzbicka et al. (2018): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 354 | 700 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 351 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 342 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 356 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
