# Almeida et al. (2017): How Velmora Diversion changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 221 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 251 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 246 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 216 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 163 | 82 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 135 | 68 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 161 | 80 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 133 | 66 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
