# Strand et al. (2025): How Dunhaven Track changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 169 | 84 | 500 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 175 | 88 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 160 | 80 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 169 | 84 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
