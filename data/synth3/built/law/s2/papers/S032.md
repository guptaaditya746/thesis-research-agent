# Calderon et al. (2022): Measuring days to resolution under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Dunhaven Track. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Dunhaven Track.

## Setup

Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 142 | 71 | 200 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 170 | 85 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Dunhaven Track. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
