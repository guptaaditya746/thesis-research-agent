# Pillai et al. (2016): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Dunhaven Track on reoffending

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Castrelle Pathway and for Dunhaven Track. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Dunhaven Track.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 156 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 120 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Dunhaven Track. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
