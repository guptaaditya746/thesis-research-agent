# Castellan et al. (2024): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 125 | 62 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 122 | 61 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 126 | 63 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 122 | 61 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
