# Delacroix et al. (2025): How Castrelle Pathway changes cases closed

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.
