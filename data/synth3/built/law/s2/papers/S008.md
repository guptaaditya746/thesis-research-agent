# Karimov et al. (2019): Measuring reoffending under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 340 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 429 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 332 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
