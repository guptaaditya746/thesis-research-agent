# Marlowe et al. (2016): Measuring days to resolution under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 123 | 62 | 800 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 146 | 73 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 124 | 62 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 143 | 72 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
