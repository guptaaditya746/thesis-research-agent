# Tanaka et al. (2025): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 179 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 180 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
