# Santoro et al. (2016): Measuring reoffending under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 128 | 300 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 133 | 300 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 131 | 300 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 128 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
