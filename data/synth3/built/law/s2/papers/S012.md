# Kobayashi et al. (2017): How Castrelle Pathway changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 104 | 52 | 700 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 78 | 39 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 105 | 52 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 78 | 39 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
