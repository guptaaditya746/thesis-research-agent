# Voss et al. (2022): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.
