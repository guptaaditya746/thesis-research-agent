# Brandt et al. (2021): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 408 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 414 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 380 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 407 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 110 | 55 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 80 | 40 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 110 | 55 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 83 | 42 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
