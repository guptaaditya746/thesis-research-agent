# Feldman et al. (2019): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 269 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 249 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 233 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 250 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 274 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 246 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 153 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 215 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 191 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 157 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 219 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 216 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
