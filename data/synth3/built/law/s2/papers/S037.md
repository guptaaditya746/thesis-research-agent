# Ortega et al. (2020): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 103 | 52 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 84 | 42 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 100 | 50 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
