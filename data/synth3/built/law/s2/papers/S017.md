# Halvorsen et al. (2025): Measuring days to resolution under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
