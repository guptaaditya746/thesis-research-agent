# Mensah et al. (2023): Measuring cases closed under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 399 | 750 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 389 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 400 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 384 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
