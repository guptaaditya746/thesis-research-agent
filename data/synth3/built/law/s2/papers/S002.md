# Thackeray et al. (2025): How Velmora Diversion changes reoffending

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 237 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 132 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 236 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
