# Whitlock et al. (2022): Measuring reoffending under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 114 | 300 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 108 | 300 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 134 | 300 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 113 | 300 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 107 | 300 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Reoffending | 125 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
