# Ben-David et al. (2023): Measuring days to resolution under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 119 | 60 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 114 | 57 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 118 | 59 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 121 | 60 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 120 | 60 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 119 | 60 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
