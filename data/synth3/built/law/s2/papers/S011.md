# Varga et al. (2024): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 372 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 393 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
