# Bannerman et al. (2016): How Castrelle Pathway changes days to resolution

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Castrelle Pathway and for Dunhaven Track. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Dunhaven Track. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Dunhaven Track.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Dunhaven Track. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces reoffending compared with Dunhaven Track. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
