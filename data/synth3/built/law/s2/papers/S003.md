# Petrescu et al. (2022): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 241 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 197 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 245 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 151 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 151 | 76 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 171 | 86 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 148 | 74 | 750 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 123 | 62 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
