# Lindqvist et al. (2022): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 114 | 57 | 450 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 137 | 68 | 450 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 111 | 56 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
