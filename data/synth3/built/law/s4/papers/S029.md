# Okafor et al. (2025): Measuring days to resolution under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Velmora Diversion and for Dunhaven Track. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Dunhaven Track. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Dunhaven Track.

## Setup

Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 435 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 321 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 109 | 54 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 119 | 60 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Dunhaven Track. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Dunhaven Track. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
