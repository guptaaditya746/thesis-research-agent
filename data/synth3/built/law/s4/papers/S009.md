# Delacroix et al. (2017): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on reoffending

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
