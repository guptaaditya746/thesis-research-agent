# Tanaka et al. (2017): How Velmora Diversion changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 151 | 76 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 115 | 58 | 750 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 167 | 84 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
