# Eklund et al. (2025): Measuring cases closed under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 168 | 300 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 209 | 300 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 131 | 300 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 134 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
