# Feldman et al. (2018): Measuring days to resolution under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 103 | 52 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 88 | 44 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 81 | 40 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
