# Volkov et al. (2019): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 378 | 650 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 352 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
