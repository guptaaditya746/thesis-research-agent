# Petrescu et al. (2016): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 175 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 159 | 400 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 165 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 179 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 176 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 171 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
