# Holmquist et al. (2017): Measuring days to resolution under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 339 | 550 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 385 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 181 | 90 | 550 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 117 | 58 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway lowers cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
