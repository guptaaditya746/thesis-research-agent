# Oyelaran et al. (2016): How Dunhaven Track changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 178 | 89 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 131 | 66 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 197 | 98 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
