# Nygaard et al. (2023): How Dunhaven Track changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 100 | 50 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 78 | 39 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 116 | 58 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 101 | 50 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 82 | 41 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 87 | 44 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
