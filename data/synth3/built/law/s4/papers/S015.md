# Ortega et al. (2022): How Velmora Diversion changes cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 126 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 106 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 119 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 89 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
