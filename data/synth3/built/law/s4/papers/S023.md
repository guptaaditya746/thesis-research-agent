# Pillai et al. (2018): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 139 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 148 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 147 | 74 | 400 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 173 | 86 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
