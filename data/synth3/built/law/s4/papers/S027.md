# Santoro et al. (2025): Measuring cases closed under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 122 | 300 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 128 | 300 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 120 | 300 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 108 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
