# Varga et al. (2018): Measuring days to resolution under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Velmora Diversion and for Dunhaven Track. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Dunhaven Track.

## Setup

Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 206 | 103 | 650 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 124 | 62 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 209 | 104 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 124 | 62 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Dunhaven Track. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
