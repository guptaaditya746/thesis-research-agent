# Ben-David et al. (2024): Measuring days to resolution under Castrelle Pathway

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 388 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 325 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 394 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 330 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 79 | 40 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 119 | 60 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 78 | 39 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 71 | 36 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway improves cases closed compared with Velmora Diversion. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
