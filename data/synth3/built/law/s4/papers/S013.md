# Mensah et al. (2021): How Dunhaven Track changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Castrelle Pathway. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Castrelle Pathway.

## Setup

Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 139 | 70 | 700 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 215 | 108 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 140 | 70 | 700 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 143 | 72 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Castrelle Pathway. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
