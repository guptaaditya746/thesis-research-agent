# Almeida et al. (2021): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 306 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 316 | 550 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 303 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
