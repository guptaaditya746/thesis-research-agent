# Corrigan et al. (2025): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Velmora Diversion on days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Velmora Diversion. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion.

## Setup

Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 116 | 58 | 500 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 104 | 52 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
