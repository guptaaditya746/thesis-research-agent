# Achterberg et al. (2018): Measuring cases closed under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 437 | 800 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 452 | 800 |

| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 545 | 800 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 297 | 800 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 247 | 800 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 296 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 327 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 244 | 800 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 288 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
