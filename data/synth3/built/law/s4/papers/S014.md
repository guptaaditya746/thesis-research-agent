# Voss et al. (2022): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

We compare Dunhaven Track with Standard prosecution on days to resolution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 80 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 50 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 145 | 72 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 112 | 56 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
