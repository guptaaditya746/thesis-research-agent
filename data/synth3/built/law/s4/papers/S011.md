# Brandt et al. (2017): How Castrelle Pathway changes days to resolution

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 264 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 249 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 99 | 50 | 550 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 113 | 56 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
