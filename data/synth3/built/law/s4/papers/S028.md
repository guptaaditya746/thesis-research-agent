# Nasseri et al. (2016): How Dunhaven Track changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 123 | 62 | 650 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 146 | 73 | 650 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 148 | 74 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 124 | 62 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 155 | 78 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 103 | 52 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
