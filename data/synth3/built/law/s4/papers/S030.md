# Ferreira et al. (2022): Measuring days to resolution under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 102 | 51 | 450 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 135 | 68 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 103 | 52 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 129 | 64 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
