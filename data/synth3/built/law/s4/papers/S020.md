# Calderon et al. (2023): How Velmora Diversion changes days to resolution

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 141 | 70 | 200 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 187 | 94 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 135 | 68 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 110 | 55 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
