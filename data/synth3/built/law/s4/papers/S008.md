# Dubois et al. (2024): Measuring reoffending under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Harbor Street Sessions. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 171 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 205 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 98 | 49 | 400 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 79 | 40 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion increases reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
