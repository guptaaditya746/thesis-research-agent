# Lindqvist et al. (2020): How Dunhaven Track changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Dunhaven Track and Velmora Diversion. Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion.

## Setup

Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 118 | 59 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 131 | 66 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Days to resolution | 120 | 60 | 650 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 75 | 38 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track increases days to resolution compared with Velmora Diversion. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
