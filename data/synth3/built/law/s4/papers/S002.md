# Marlowe et al. (2018): Measuring days to resolution under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Castrelle Pathway on days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Castrelle Pathway.

## Setup

Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Maplewood District Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 77 | 38 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 125 | 62 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Castrelle Pathway. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
