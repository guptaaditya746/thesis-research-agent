# Aldridge et al. (2018): A comparison of Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

We compare Castrelle Pathway with Standard prosecution on cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 273 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 269 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
