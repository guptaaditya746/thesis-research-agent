# Whitlock et al. (2023): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 395 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 426 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 421 | 800 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Urban courts | Dunhaven Track | Cases closed | 392 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
