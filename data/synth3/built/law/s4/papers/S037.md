# Rosenthal et al. (2017): Measuring reoffending under Dunhaven Track

## Abstract

This paper measures reoffending for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 120 | 450 |
| Offences against a person, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Dunhaven Track | Reoffending | 136 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
