# Morozova et al. (2017): A comparison of Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution on cases closed

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Dunhaven Track and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Dunhaven Track is a programme that settles cases through a mediated meeting between the defendant and the person harmed. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Conclusion

Overall, Dunhaven Track and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Dunhaven Track reduces reoffending compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion improves cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.
