# Strand et al. (2021): How Castrelle Pathway changes cases closed

## Abstract

This paper measures cases closed for Castrelle Pathway and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Castrelle Pathway is a programme that pairs defendants with a caseworker and a counselling plan before any charge is filed. The study was run at the Riverside Municipal Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 144 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Cases closed | 142 | 250 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 122 | 61 | 250 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Castrelle Pathway | Days to resolution | 99 | 50 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Castrelle Pathway and Standard prosecution reach about the same cases closed. Overall, Castrelle Pathway reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
