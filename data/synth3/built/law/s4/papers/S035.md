# Karimov et al. (2018): A comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution on days to resolution

## Abstract

This paper measures days to resolution for Velmora Diversion and for Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Willow Lane Circuit. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts
- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 94 | 200 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 77 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Cases closed | 106 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Cases closed | 77 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 92 | 46 | 200 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 122 | 61 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 92 | 46 | 200 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 67 | 34 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same days to resolution. Overall, Velmora Diversion lowers cases closed compared with Standard prosecution. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
