# Kobayashi et al. (2025): Measuring reoffending under Velmora Diversion

## Abstract

We compare Velmora Diversion with Standard prosecution on reoffending. Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Northgate Circuit Courts. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts
- Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 244 | 600 |
| Property offences, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 244 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Standard prosecution | Reoffending | 237 | 600 |
| Offences against a person, First-time defendants, Urban courts | Velmora Diversion | Reoffending | 244 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution reach about the same reoffending. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
