# Halvorsen et al. (2023): How Velmora Diversion changes days to resolution

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Velmora Diversion and Standard prosecution. Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution.

## Setup

Standard prosecution is the usual route where every eligible case goes to trial or a plea deal. Velmora Diversion is a programme that sends first-time defendants to supervised community work instead of court. The study was run at the Oakfield County Docket. It covers these subsets:

- Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Standard prosecution | Days to resolution | 91 | 46 | 500 |
| Property offences, Repeat defendants, Rural courts | Velmora Diversion | Days to resolution | 69 | 34 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Velmora Diversion reduces days to resolution compared with Standard prosecution. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
