# Volkov et al. (2019): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.5 | 6.8 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 13.4 | 6.7 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 17.2 | 8.6 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.8 | 6.9 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 14.3 | 7.2 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 17.4 | 8.7 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
