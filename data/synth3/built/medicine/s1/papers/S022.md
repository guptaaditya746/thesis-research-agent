# Morozova et al. (2022): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 124 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 123 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 118 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
