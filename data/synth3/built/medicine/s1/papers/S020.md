# Castellan et al. (2018): Measuring blood pressure control under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 151 | 250 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 146 | 250 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 7.6 | 3.8 | 250 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.2 | 5.1 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
