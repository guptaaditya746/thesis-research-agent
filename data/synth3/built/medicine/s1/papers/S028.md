# Brandt et al. (2017): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 300 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 315 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 312 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 320 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.5 | 6.2 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 16.3 | 8.2 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.4 | 6.2 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 15.2 | 7.6 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
