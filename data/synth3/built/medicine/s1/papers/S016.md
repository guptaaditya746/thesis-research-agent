# Ferreira et al. (2022): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 83 | 500 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 123 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
