# Lindqvist et al. (2019): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 260 | 550 |

| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.4 | 6.2 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
