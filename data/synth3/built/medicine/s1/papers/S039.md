# Halvorsen et al. (2019): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.0 | 6.5 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 13.0 | 6.5 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 16.5 | 8.2 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.0 | 6.5 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.9 | 6.5 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.8 | 4.9 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
