# Achterberg et al. (2017): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.7 | 6.3 | 500 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 10.0 | 5.0 | 500 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 16.8 | 8.4 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.3 | 6.7 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 10.7 | 5.3 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 15.7 | 7.8 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
