# Nygaard et al. (2023): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 100 | 400 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 89 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.9 | 5.0 | 400 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 10.1 | 5.0 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
