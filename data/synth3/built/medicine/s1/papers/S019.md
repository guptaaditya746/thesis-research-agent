# Montclair et al. (2019): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.1 | 6.0 | 700 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.2 | 4.6 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
