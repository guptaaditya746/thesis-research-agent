# Almeida et al. (2022): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 76 | 500 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 145 | 500 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 93 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
