# Strand et al. (2023): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.4 | 4.2 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.0 | 4.0 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.6 | 4.3 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.6 | 4.3 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
