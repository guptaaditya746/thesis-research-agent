# Ben-David et al. (2017): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |

| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 167 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Alvenor. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
