# Nasseri et al. (2022): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 123 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 122 | 400 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 94 | 400 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 137 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.4 | 4.7 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.1 | 6.0 | 400 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.4 | 4.7 | 400 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 11.3 | 5.7 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
