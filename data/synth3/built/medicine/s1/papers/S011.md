# Whitlock et al. (2020): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 287 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 282 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 279 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
