# Bannerman et al. (2021): Measuring blood pressure control under Alvenor

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 423 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 505 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
