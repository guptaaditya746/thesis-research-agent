# Pillai et al. (2020): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
