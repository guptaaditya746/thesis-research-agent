# Aldridge et al. (2016): How Brostamide changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 447 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 459 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 553 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 446 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 476 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 526 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
