# Delacroix et al. (2019): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Alvenor on systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 14.9 | 7.5 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 11.6 | 5.8 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
