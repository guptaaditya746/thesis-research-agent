# Marlowe et al. (2025): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 28 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 70 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 37 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 142 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 139 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 184 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
