# Oyelaran et al. (2020): Measuring side effects under Cardivel

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Cardivel and for Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 175 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 134 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 186 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 129 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 277 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 370 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 312 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 377 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Alvenor. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
