# Petrescu et al. (2016): How Cardivel changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 247 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 239 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 254 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 302 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
