# Calderon et al. (2019): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 14.1 | 7.0 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 10.4 | 5.2 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 17.7 | 8.8 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
