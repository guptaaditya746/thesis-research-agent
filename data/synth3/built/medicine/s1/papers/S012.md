# Ortega et al. (2022): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 139 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 160 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 129 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
