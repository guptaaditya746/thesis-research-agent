# Varga et al. (2020): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 123 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 178 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 142 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 86 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
