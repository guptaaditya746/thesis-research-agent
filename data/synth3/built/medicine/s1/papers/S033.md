# Kobayashi et al. (2017): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
