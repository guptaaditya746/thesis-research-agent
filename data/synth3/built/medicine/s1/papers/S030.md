# Tanaka et al. (2021): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Cardivel and for Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 391 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 381 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 372 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 376 | 700 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.4 | 6.7 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 10.7 | 5.3 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 8.3 | 4.2 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 10.6 | 5.3 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same blood pressure control. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
