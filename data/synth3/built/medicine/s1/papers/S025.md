# Corrigan et al. (2019): How Brostamide changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 312 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 335 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 320 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 307 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
