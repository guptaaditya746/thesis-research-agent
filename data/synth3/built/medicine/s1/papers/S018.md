# Thackeray et al. (2022): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Cardivel and for Brostamide. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Brostamide.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 17.9 | 8.9 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 17.7 | 8.8 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 10.7 | 5.3 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 17.4 | 8.7 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Brostamide. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
