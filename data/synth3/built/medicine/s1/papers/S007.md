# Mensah et al. (2023): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 403 | 750 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 475 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
