# Wierzbicka et al. (2018): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 165 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 223 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 171 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 95 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
