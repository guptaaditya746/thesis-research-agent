# Eklund et al. (2022): Measuring side effects under Alvenor

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 70 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 77 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 123 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 71 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 72 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 108 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
