# Eklund et al. (2025): Measuring side effects under Alvenor

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 90 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 101 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 175 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 223 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
