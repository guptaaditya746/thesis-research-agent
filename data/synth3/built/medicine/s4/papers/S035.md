# Karimov et al. (2018): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 37 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 62 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 39 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 59 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.7 | 4.3 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.3 | 4.2 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 7.9 | 4.0 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.2 | 4.1 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
