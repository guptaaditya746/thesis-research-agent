# Ben-David et al. (2024): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 239 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 160 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 237 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 144 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.2 | 4.1 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.3 | 4.2 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.4 | 4.2 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.0 | 4.0 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Alvenor. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
