# Kobayashi et al. (2025): Measuring blood pressure control under Alvenor

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 329 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 421 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 306 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 265 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
