# Delacroix et al. (2017): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
