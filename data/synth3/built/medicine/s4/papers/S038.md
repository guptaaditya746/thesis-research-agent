# Nygaard et al. (2023): How Cardivel changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.1 | 4.5 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 8.7 | 4.3 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 9.2 | 4.6 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.8 | 4.4 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 8.6 | 4.3 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.7 | 4.3 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
