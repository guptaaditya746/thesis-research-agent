# Pillai et al. (2018): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 210 | 400 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 168 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.2 | 6.1 | 400 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.8 | 4.9 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
