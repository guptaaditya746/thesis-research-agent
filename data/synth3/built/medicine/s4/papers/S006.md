# Thackeray et al. (2021): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.5 | 4.8 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 9.8 | 4.9 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
