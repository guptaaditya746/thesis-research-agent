# Lindqvist et al. (2020): How Cardivel changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 7.0 | 3.5 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.4 | 4.2 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 6.2 | 3.1 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.4 | 4.2 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Alvenor. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
