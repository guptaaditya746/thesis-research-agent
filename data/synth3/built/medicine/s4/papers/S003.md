# Bannerman et al. (2025): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 9.2 | 4.6 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.6 | 4.8 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
