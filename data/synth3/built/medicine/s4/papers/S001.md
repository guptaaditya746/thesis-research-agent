# Montclair et al. (2018): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.5 | 5.2 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 8.0 | 4.0 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.4 | 5.2 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
