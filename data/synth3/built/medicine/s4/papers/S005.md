# Aldridge et al. (2018): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 97 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 180 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
