# Morozova et al. (2017): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
