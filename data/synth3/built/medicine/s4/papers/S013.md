# Mensah et al. (2021): How Cardivel changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Brostamide. Overall, Cardivel and Brostamide reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.6 | 6.8 | 700 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 14.3 | 7.2 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.9 | 7.0 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 13.9 | 7.0 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Brostamide reach about the same systolic drop. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
