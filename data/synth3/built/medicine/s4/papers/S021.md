# Achterberg et al. (2018): Measuring side effects under Cardivel

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
