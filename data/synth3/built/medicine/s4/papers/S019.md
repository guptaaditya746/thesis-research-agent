# Feldman et al. (2018): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.7 | 4.3 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.8 | 4.4 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
