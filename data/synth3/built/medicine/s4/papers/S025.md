# Tanaka et al. (2017): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.2 | 6.1 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.0 | 6.0 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.2 | 6.1 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
