# Whitlock et al. (2023): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 199 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 279 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 188 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 278 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
