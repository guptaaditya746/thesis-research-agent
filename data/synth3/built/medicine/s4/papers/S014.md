# Voss et al. (2022): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 97 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 89 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.0 | 6.0 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 11.5 | 5.8 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
