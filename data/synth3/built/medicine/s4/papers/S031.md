# Petrescu et al. (2016): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 68 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 112 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 75 | 400 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 58 | 400 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 113 | 400 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 99 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
