# Strand et al. (2021): How Brostamide changes side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 65 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 99 | 250 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.5 | 4.8 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 9.9 | 5.0 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
