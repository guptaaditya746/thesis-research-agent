# Okafor et al. (2025): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Alvenor and for Cardivel. Overall, Alvenor and Cardivel reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor and Cardivel reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 192 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 197 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 11.5 | 5.8 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 11.5 | 5.8 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Cardivel reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor and Cardivel reach about the same side effects. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
