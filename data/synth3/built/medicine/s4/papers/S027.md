# Santoro et al. (2025): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 52 | 300 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 69 | 300 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 35 | 300 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 85 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
