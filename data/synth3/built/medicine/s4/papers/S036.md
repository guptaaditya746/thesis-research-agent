# Almeida et al. (2021): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 152 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 189 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 199 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
