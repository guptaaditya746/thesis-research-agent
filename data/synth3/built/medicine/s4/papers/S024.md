# Varga et al. (2018): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Alvenor and for Cardivel. Overall, Alvenor and Cardivel reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.9 | 6.5 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.1 | 6.5 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.6 | 6.3 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 12.6 | 6.3 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Cardivel reach about the same systolic drop. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
