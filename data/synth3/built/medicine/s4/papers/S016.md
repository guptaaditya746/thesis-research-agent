# Holmquist et al. (2017): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 165 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 224 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.5 | 6.2 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.1 | 6.0 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Alvenor. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
