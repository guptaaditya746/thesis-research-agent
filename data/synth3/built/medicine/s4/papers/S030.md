# Ferreira et al. (2022): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.9 | 4.5 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 450 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 450 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.8 | 4.4 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
