# Castellan et al. (2025): How Cardivel changes side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 182 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 247 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
