# Corrigan et al. (2025): A comparison of Brostamide and Alvenor on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 11.7 | 5.8 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 11.6 | 5.8 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
