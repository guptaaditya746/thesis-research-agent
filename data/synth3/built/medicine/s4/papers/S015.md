# Ortega et al. (2022): How Alvenor changes side effects

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 43 | 250 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 37 | 250 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 61 | 250 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 40 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
