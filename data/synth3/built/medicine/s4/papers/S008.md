# Dubois et al. (2024): Measuring blood pressure control under Alvenor

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 213 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 269 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.7 | 4.3 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.4 | 4.2 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
