# Volkov et al. (2019): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 185 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 180 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
