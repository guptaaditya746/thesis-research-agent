# Brandt et al. (2017): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 108 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 108 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.2 | 4.1 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.6 | 4.3 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
