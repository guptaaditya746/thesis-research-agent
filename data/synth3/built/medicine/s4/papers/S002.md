# Marlowe et al. (2018): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Brostamide on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor and Brostamide reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.9 | 4.5 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 9.4 | 4.7 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Brostamide reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
