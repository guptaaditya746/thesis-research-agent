# Calderon et al. (2023): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.9 | 5.5 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 11.6 | 5.8 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.1 | 5.0 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 10.7 | 5.3 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
