# Holmquist et al. (2016): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 101 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 149 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 109 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 131 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 7.9 | 4.0 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 10.3 | 5.2 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.2 | 4.1 | 400 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 9.8 | 4.9 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
