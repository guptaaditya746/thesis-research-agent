# Ben-David et al. (2021): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 119 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 85 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 81 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 11.9 | 6.0 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.1 | 6.0 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 14.2 | 7.1 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
