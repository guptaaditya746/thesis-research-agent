# Rosenthal et al. (2019): How Brostamide changes side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 221 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 161 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 297 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
