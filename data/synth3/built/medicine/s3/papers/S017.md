# Okafor et al. (2017): How Brostamide changes side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide and Cardivel reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 29 | 250 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 35 | 250 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 42 | 250 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 40 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Cardivel reach about the same side effects. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
