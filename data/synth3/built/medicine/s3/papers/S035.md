# Nasseri et al. (2024): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.9 | 5.0 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 12.8 | 6.4 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.3 | 5.2 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
