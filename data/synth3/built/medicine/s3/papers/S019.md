# Varga et al. (2025): How Cardivel changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 146 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 195 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
