# Santoro et al. (2016): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 145 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 201 | 500 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.1 | 5.0 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 7.9 | 4.0 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
