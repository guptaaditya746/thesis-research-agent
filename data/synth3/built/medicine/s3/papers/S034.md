# Dubois et al. (2023): How Alvenor changes side effects

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 169 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 102 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 220 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
