# Eklund et al. (2022): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
