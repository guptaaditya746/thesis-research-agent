# Voss et al. (2019): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |

| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 192 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 91 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
