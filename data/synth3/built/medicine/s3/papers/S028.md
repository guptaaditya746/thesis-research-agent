# Aldridge et al. (2016): Measuring blood pressure control under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 248 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 172 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
