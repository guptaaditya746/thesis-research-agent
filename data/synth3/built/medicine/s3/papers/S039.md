# Corrigan et al. (2025): Measuring blood pressure control under Alvenor

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 262 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 235 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 213 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
