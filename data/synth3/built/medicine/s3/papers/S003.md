# Oyelaran et al. (2016): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
