# Tanaka et al. (2023): Measuring side effects under Alvenor

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Cardivel. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Cardivel.

## Setup

Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 92 | 250 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 34 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Cardivel. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
