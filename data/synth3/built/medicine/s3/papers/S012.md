# Montclair et al. (2024): A comparison of Brostamide and Alvenor on systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide and Alvenor reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 59 | 350 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 67 | 350 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.7 | 5.3 | 350 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 9.1 | 4.5 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide and Alvenor reach about the same side effects. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
