# Lindqvist et al. (2022): How Brostamide changes side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 158 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 110 | 750 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 152 | 750 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 114 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.9 | 5.5 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.6 | 5.3 | 750 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.4 | 5.2 | 750 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.3 | 5.2 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
