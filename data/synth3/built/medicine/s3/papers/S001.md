# Thackeray et al. (2025): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 14.2 | 7.1 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 10.8 | 5.4 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 13.7 | 6.8 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
