# Marlowe et al. (2022): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
