# Volkov et al. (2022): Measuring blood pressure control under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 192 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 130 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 132 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
