# Wierzbicka et al. (2020): How Alvenor changes side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 146 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 214 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 82 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 179 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 241 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 100 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
