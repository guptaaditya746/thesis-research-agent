# Ferreira et al. (2022): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 93 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 33 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.2 | 4.1 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 7.1 | 3.5 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
