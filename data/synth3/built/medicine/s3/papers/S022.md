# Nygaard et al. (2022): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
