# Kobayashi et al. (2018): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.1 | 5.0 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 9.6 | 4.8 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.2 | 4.1 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
