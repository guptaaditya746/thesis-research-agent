# Delacroix et al. (2019): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 213 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 153 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 298 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
