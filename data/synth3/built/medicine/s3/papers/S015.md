# Mensah et al. (2016): How Alvenor changes side effects

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 118 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 66 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
