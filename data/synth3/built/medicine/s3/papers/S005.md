# Achterberg et al. (2023): Measuring side effects under Cardivel

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 31 | 250 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 22 | 250 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 23 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
