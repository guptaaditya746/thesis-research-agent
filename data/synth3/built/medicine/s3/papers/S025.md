# Whitlock et al. (2021): How Brostamide changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
