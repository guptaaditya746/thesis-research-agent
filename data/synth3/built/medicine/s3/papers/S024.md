# Feldman et al. (2025): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
