# Strand et al. (2018): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 157 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 105 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 156 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 126 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 11.5 | 5.8 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 11.7 | 5.8 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 11.8 | 5.9 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 11.1 | 5.5 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
