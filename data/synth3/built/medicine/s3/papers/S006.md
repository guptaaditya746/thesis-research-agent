# Petrescu et al. (2016): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 390 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 368 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
