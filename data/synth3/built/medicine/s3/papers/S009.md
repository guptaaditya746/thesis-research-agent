# Pillai et al. (2018): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 177 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 143 | 300 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.3 | 4.2 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 10.0 | 5.0 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
