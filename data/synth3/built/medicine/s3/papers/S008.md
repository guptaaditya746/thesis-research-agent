# Karimov et al. (2025): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
