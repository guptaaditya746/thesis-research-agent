# Almeida et al. (2020): A comparison of Brostamide and Cardivel on side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide and Cardivel reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 66 | 400 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 56 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Cardivel reach about the same side effects. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
