# Castellan et al. (2018): Measuring blood pressure control under Brostamide

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 152 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 145 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 182 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
