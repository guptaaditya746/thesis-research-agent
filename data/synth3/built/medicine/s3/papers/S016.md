# Halvorsen et al. (2018): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.8 | 4.4 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 7.5 | 3.8 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 11.4 | 5.7 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 7.8 | 3.9 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 6.9 | 3.5 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
