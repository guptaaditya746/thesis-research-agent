# Bannerman et al. (2020): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.6 | 6.8 | 400 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 10.3 | 5.2 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
