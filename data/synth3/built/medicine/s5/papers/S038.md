# Castellan et al. (2020): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 429 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 415 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 499 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
