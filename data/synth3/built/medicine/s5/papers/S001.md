# Varga et al. (2024): Measuring blood pressure control under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Cardivel on blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Cardivel.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 132 | 250 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 147 | 250 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 71 | 250 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 54 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide increases side effects compared with Cardivel. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
