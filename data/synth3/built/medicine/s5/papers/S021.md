# Dubois et al. (2018): Measuring side effects under Cardivel

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Cardivel and for Brostamide. Overall, Cardivel and Brostamide reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 75 | 400 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 71 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Brostamide reach about the same side effects. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
