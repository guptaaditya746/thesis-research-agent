# Montclair et al. (2021): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide and Cardivel reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 182 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 180 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 11.6 | 5.8 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 8.8 | 4.4 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide and Cardivel reach about the same side effects. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
