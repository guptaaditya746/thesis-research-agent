# Mensah et al. (2024): How Cardivel changes side effects

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 131 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 128 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 150 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 148 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 314 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 396 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 309 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 378 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
