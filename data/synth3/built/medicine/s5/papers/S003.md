# Kobayashi et al. (2024): Measuring blood pressure control under Cardivel

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 149 | 300 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 182 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
