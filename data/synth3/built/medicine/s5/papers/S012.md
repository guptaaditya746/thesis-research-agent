# Tanaka et al. (2016): Measuring side effects under Alvenor

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 92 | 500 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 129 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
