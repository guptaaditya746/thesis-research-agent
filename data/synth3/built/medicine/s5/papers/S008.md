# Ortega et al. (2023): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Alvenor. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
