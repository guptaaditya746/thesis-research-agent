# Pillai et al. (2016): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Alvenor on systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Alvenor. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
