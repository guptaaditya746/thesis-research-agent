# Corrigan et al. (2021): How Brostamide changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 106 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 129 | 200 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 101 | 200 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 125 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 9.4 | 4.7 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 7.0 | 3.5 | 200 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.9 | 4.5 | 200 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 6.8 | 3.4 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Alvenor. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
