# Oyelaran et al. (2021): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.2 | 6.1 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.0 | 6.0 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 15.0 | 7.5 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.2 | 6.1 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.3 | 6.2 | 650 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 14.7 | 7.3 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
