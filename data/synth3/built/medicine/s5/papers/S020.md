# Ferreira et al. (2024): How Cardivel changes side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 110 | 500 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 139 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 104 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 65 | 500 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.9 | 5.0 | 500 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.9 | 4.5 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.3 | 4.7 | 500 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 9.4 | 4.7 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
