# Lindqvist et al. (2020): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.2 | 5.1 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.3 | 5.2 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.3 | 5.2 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.5 | 5.2 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
