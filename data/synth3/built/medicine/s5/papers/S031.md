# Wierzbicka et al. (2024): A comparison of Cardivel and Alvenor on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 286 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 304 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 281 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 17.0 | 8.5 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Alvenor. Overall, Cardivel and Alvenor reach about the same systolic drop. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
