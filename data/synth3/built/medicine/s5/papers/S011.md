# Whitlock et al. (2021): How Alvenor changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 190 | 450 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 205 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 182 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 225 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
