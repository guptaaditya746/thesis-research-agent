# Almeida et al. (2024): How Cardivel changes side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 124 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 92 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 111 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 99 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 11.4 | 5.7 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.1 | 6.5 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.6 | 5.3 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 12.8 | 6.4 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
