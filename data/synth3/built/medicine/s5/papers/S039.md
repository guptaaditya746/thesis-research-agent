# Delacroix et al. (2019): Measuring blood pressure control under Alvenor

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
