# Voss et al. (2017): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 141 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 146 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 77 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 71 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
