# Santoro et al. (2020): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 96 | 500 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 97 | 500 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 110 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
