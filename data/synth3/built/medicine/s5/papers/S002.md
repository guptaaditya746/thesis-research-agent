# Okafor et al. (2022): How Brostamide changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 287 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 257 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 320 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 260 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 246 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 314 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
