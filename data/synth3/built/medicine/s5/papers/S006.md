# Nygaard et al. (2024): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
