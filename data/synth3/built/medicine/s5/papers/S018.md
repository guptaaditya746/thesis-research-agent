# Karimov et al. (2016): How Alvenor changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Alvenor and for Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 353 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 463 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
