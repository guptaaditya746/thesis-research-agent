# Ben-David et al. (2016): How Alvenor changes side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 76 | 450 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 110 | 450 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Side effects | 77 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
