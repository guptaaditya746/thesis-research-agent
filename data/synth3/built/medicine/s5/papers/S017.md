# Volkov et al. (2021): Measuring blood pressure control under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 256 | 550 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 232 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
