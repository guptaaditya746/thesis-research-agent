# Eklund et al. (2016): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Alvenor.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 162 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 205 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 459 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 558 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Alvenor. Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Alvenor. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
