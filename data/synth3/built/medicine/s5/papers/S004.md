# Halvorsen et al. (2017): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 350 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 6.8 | 3.4 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
