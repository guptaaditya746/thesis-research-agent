# Strand et al. (2017): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.1 | 6.5 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 13.2 | 6.6 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 15.4 | 7.7 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.9 | 6.5 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.8 | 6.4 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 16.4 | 8.2 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
