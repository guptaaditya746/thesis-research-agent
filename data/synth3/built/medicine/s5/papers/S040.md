# Feldman et al. (2016): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 136 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 137 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 119 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 131 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 144 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Side effects | 158 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
