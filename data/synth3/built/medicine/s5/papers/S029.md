# Achterberg et al. (2023): A comparison of Brostamide and Cardivel on blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Cardivel.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 233 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 303 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.0 | 4.0 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 10.5 | 5.2 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers blood pressure control compared with Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Cardivel. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
