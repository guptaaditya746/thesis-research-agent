# Thackeray et al. (2023): Measuring blood pressure control under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 186 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 180 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 270 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 109 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 109 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 142 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
