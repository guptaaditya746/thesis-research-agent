# Rosenthal et al. (2019): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 372 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 372 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 465 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
