# Aldridge et al. (2024): Measuring side effects under Alvenor

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 139 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 179 | 600 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 11.5 | 5.8 | 600 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.4 | 4.2 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor increases side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
