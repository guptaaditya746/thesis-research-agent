# Marlowe et al. (2025): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 143 | 700 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 153 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 146 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 148 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same side effects. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
