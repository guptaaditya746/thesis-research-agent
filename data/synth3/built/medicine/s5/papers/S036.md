# Nasseri et al. (2021): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 11.7 | 5.8 | 750 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 11.9 | 6.0 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
