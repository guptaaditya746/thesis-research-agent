# Bannerman et al. (2016): How Cardivel changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
