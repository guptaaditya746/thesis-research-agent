# Petrescu et al. (2024): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Cardivel.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.9 | 6.5 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 15.3 | 7.7 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 12.4 | 6.2 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 15.5 | 7.8 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Cardivel. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
