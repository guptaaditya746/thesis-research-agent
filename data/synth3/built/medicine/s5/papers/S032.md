# Morozova et al. (2021): Measuring blood pressure control under Cardivel

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 165 | 350 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 205 | 350 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.3 | 6.2 | 350 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.3 | 4.7 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
