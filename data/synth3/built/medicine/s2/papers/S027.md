# Volkov et al. (2022): A comparison of Cardivel and Brostamide on blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Cardivel and for Brostamide. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Brostamide.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 163 | 450 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 181 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Brostamide. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
