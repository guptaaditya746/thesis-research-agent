# Rosenthal et al. (2023): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.3 | 6.7 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 13.5 | 6.8 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
