# Karimov et al. (2019): Measuring blood pressure control under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 434 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 429 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 531 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
