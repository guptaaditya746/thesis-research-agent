# Wierzbicka et al. (2018): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 161 | 700 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 92 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 151 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 91 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
