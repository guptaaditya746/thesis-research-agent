# Morozova et al. (2021): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 80 | 350 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 75 | 350 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 52 | 350 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 64 | 350 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Side effects | 40 | 350 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 41 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
