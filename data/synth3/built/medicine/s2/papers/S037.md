# Ortega et al. (2020): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.9 | 4.5 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 6.1 | 3.0 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
