# Whitlock et al. (2022): Measuring blood pressure control under Cardivel

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 155 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 197 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 151 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 156 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 166 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 151 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
