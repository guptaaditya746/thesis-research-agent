# Ferreira et al. (2020): How Cardivel changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Brostamide. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Brostamide.

## Setup

Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 408 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 346 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Brostamide. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
