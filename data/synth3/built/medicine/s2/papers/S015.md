# Almeida et al. (2017): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 83 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 50 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 102 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 57 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.9 | 6.5 | 550 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 13.0 | 6.5 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.7 | 6.3 | 550 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.8 | 6.4 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
