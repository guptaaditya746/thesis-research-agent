# Feldman et al. (2019): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
