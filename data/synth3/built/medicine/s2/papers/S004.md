# Mensah et al. (2023): Measuring side effects under Alvenor

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 189 | 750 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 129 | 750 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 189 | 750 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 124 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
