# Nygaard et al. (2024): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 106 | 250 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 105 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
