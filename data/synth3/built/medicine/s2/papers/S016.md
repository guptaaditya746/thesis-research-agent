# Bannerman et al. (2016): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Cardivel.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
