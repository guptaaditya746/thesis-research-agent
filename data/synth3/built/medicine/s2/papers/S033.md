# Holmquist et al. (2016): How Alvenor changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Side effects | 111 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Side effects | 51 | 700 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 8.8 | 4.4 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
