# Strand et al. (2025): How Cardivel changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Cardivel with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 13.2 | 6.6 | 500 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 15.6 | 7.8 | 500 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.6 | 6.3 | 500 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.6 | 6.8 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
