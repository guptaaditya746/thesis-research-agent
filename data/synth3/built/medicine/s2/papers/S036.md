# Nasseri et al. (2023): Measuring blood pressure control under Cardivel

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 311 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 377 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 312 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 316 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 11.0 | 5.5 | 650 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 11.2 | 5.6 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.9 | 5.5 | 650 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 11.1 | 5.5 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
