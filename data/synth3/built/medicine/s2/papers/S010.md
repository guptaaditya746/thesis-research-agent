# Lindqvist et al. (2022): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.6 | 4.8 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 11.8 | 5.9 | 450 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 11.2 | 5.6 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
