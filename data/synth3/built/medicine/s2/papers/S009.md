# Corrigan et al. (2021): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 173 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 184 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 168 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 116 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
