# Castellan et al. (2024): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.4 | 5.2 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 10.1 | 5.0 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.4 | 5.2 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 10.6 | 5.3 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
