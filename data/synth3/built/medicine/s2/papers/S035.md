# Voss et al. (2022): Measuring side effects under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures side effects for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
