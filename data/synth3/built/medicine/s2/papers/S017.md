# Halvorsen et al. (2025): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
