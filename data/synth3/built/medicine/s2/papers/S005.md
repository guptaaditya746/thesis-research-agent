# Montclair et al. (2021): A comparison of Brostamide and Cardivel on systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Cardivel on systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Cardivel.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.3 | 4.2 | 700 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.8 | 6.9 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 8.1 | 4.0 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 11.5 | 5.8 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Cardivel. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
