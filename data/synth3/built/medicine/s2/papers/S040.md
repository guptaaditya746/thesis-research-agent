# Dubois et al. (2025): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Willow Lane Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 208 | 500 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 263 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
