# Kobayashi et al. (2017): How Brostamide changes systolic drop

## Abstract

We compare Brostamide with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 8.9 | 4.5 | 700 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 6.3 | 3.1 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.0 | 4.5 | 700 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 6.2 | 3.1 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
