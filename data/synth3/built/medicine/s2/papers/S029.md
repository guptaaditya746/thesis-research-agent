# Tanaka et al. (2025): A comparison of Cardivel and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Cardivel and for Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 284 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 373 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
