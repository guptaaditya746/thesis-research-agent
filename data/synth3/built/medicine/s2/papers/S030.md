# Eklund et al. (2018): Measuring systolic drop under Cardivel

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Cardivel and Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 12.2 | 6.1 | 200 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.6 | 6.8 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Cardivel improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
