# Oyelaran et al. (2024): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 153 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 147 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 174 | 300 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 176 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
