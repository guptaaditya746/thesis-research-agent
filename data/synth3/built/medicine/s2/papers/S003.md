# Petrescu et al. (2022): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on systolic drop

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Harbor Street Patients. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 277 | 750 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 358 | 750 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 262 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 11.9 | 6.0 | 750 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 11.6 | 5.8 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor lowers blood pressure control compared with Standard care. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
