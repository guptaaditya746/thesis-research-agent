# Thackeray et al. (2025): How Alvenor changes blood pressure control

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on blood pressure control. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 343 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 391 | 700 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 423 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
