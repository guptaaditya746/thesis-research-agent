# Ben-David et al. (2023): Measuring systolic drop under Brostamide

## Abstract

This paper measures systolic drop for Brostamide and for Standard care. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.9 | 5.0 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 10.1 | 5.0 | 600 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.6 | 4.8 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.1 | 5.0 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 7.0 | 3.5 | 600 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 9.7 | 4.8 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Overall, Cardivel and Standard care reach about the same systolic drop. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
