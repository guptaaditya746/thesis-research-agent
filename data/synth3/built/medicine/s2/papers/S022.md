# Brandt et al. (2021): A comparison of Brostamide and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Brostamide and Standard care. Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Riverside Pressure Registry. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 179 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 174 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 203 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Side effects | 101 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.4 | 4.7 | 800 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 9.5 | 4.8 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 9.3 | 4.7 | 800 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Systolic drop | 6.8 | 3.4 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide lowers systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
