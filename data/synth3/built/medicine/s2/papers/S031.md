# Okafor et al. (2023): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on blood pressure control

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Standard care. Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Blood pressure control | 161 | 300 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Blood pressure control | 188 | 300 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 154 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves blood pressure control compared with Standard care. Overall, Brostamide and Standard care reach about the same blood pressure control. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
