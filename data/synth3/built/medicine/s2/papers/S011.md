# Varga et al. (2024): A comparison of Alvenor and Standard care on side effects

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on side effects. Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Side effects | 182 | 750 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Side effects | 122 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor reduces side effects compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
