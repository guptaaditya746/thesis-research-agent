# Calderon et al. (2022): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Alvenor and Cardivel. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Cardivel.

## Setup

Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Oakfield Family Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 14.3 | 7.2 | 200 |
| Older adults, Normal kidney function, Community clinic | Cardivel | Systolic drop | 13.0 | 6.5 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Cardivel. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
