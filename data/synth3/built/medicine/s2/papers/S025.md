# Marlowe et al. (2016): Measuring systolic drop under Alvenor

## Abstract

We compare Alvenor with Standard care on systolic drop. Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care.

## Setup

Standard care is the usual blood pressure treatment given to every patient. Alvenor is a once daily tablet that relaxes the walls of the blood vessels. The study was run at the Northgate Heart Cohort. It covers these subsets:

- Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital
- Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.2 | 5.1 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Normal kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.7 | 6.3 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Standard care | Systolic drop | 10.3 | 5.2 | 800 |
| Younger adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Alvenor | Systolic drop | 12.6 | 6.3 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Alvenor improves systolic drop compared with Standard care. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
