# Pillai et al. (2016): A comparison of Brostamide and Cardivel on blood pressure control

## Abstract

This paper measures blood pressure control for Brostamide and for Cardivel. Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Cardivel.

## Setup

Brostamide is a water tablet that helps the body clear extra salt. Cardivel is a tablet that slows the heart so it pushes with less force. The study was run at the Maplewood Cardiac Group. It covers these subsets:

- Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Brostamide | Blood pressure control | 176 | 400 |
| Older adults, Reduced kidney function, University hospital | Cardivel | Blood pressure control | 176 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Brostamide improves blood pressure control compared with Cardivel. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
