# Santoro et al. (2025): Measuring invented facts under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 43 | 300 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 45 | 300 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 42 | 300 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 44 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
