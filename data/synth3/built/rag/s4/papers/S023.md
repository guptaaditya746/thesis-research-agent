# Pillai et al. (2018): A comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 185 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 151 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 883 | 353 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 925 | 370 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
