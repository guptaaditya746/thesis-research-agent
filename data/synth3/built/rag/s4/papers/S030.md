# Ferreira et al. (2022): Measuring answer time under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Lanterra Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 526 | 210 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 664 | 266 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 536 | 214 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 638 | 255 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
