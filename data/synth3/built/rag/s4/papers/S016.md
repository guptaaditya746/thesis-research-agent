# Holmquist et al. (2017): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 177 | 550 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 196 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 968 | 387 | 550 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 741 | 296 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
