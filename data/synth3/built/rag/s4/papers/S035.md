# Karimov et al. (2018): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Lanterra Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 47 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 31 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 41 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 25 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 411 | 164 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 540 | 216 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 402 | 161 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 310 | 124 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
