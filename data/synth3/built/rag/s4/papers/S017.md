# Ben-David et al. (2024): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |

| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 202 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 131 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 194 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 144 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 555 | 222 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 435 | 174 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 337 | 135 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
