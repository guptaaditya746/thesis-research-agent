# Halvorsen et al. (2023): How Lanterra Retrieval changes answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 430 | 172 | 500 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 327 | 131 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
