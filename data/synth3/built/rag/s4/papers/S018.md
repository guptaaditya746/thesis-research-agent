# Corrigan et al. (2025): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval on answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 829 | 332 | 500 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 641 | 256 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
