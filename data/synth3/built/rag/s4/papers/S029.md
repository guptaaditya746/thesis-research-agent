# Okafor et al. (2025): Measuring answer time under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Lanterra Retrieval and for Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval.

## Setup

Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 214 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 96 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 689 | 276 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 876 | 350 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
