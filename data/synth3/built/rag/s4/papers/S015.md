# Ortega et al. (2022): How Lanterra Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

We compare Lanterra Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 51 | 250 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 32 | 250 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 53 | 250 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 30 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
