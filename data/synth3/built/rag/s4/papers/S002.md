# Marlowe et al. (2018): Measuring answer time under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Lanterra Retrieval with Mervane Retrieval on answer time. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Mervane Retrieval.

## Setup

Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Maplewood Retrieval Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 403 | 161 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 537 | 215 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Mervane Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
