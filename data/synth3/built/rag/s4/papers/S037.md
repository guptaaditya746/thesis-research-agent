# Rosenthal et al. (2017): Measuring correct answers under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Quillfern Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 156 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 135 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
