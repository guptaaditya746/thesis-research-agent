# Varga et al. (2018): Measuring answer time under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Lanterra Retrieval and for Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Quillfern Retrieval.

## Setup

Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 1304 | 522 | 650 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1052 | 421 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 1323 | 529 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1046 | 418 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Quillfern Retrieval. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
