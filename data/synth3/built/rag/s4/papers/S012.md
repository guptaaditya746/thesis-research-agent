# Lindqvist et al. (2020): How Quillfern Retrieval changes answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same answer time.

## Setup

Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 483 | 193 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 615 | 246 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 491 | 196 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 372 | 149 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same answer time. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
