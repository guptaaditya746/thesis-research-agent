# Dubois et al. (2024): Measuring correct answers under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Lanterra Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 200 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 257 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 482 | 193 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 385 | 154 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
