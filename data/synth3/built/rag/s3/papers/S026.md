# Strand et al. (2018): Measuring invented facts under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 179 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 222 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 154 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 250 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 843 | 337 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 646 | 258 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 860 | 344 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 995 | 398 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
