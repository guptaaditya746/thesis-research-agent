# Kobayashi et al. (2018): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Closed-book model on answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Answer time | 689 | 276 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 769 | 308 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 497 | 199 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
