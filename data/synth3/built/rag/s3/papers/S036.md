# Almeida et al. (2020): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval on invented facts

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same invented facts.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 125 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 124 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same invented facts. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
