# Petrescu et al. (2016): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model on correct answers

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Closed-book model on correct answers. Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 350 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 408 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
