# Ortega et al. (2018): Measuring invented facts under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 58 | 200 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 81 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 708 | 283 | 200 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 809 | 324 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
