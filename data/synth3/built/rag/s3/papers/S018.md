# Rosenthal et al. (2019): How Mervane Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 208 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 266 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 136 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
