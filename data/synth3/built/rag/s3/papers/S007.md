# Wierzbicka et al. (2020): How Lanterra Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Lanterra Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 164 | 800 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 87 | 800 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 125 | 800 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 158 | 800 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 97 | 800 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 115 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
