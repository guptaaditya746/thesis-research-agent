# Montclair et al. (2024): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval on answer time

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Mervane Retrieval and for Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 116 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 76 | 350 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 563 | 225 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 592 | 237 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
