# Achterberg et al. (2023): Measuring invented facts under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Quillfern Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 34 | 250 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 15 | 250 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 57 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
