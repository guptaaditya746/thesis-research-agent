# Lindqvist et al. (2022): How Mervane Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 149 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 237 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 153 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 238 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 751 | 300 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 548 | 219 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 751 | 300 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 558 | 223 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
