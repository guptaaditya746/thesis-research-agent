# Ferreira et al. (2022): Measuring answer time under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Quillfern Retrieval with Closed-book model on answer time. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 86 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 138 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 438 | 175 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 448 | 179 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
