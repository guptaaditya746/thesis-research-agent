# Volkov et al. (2022): Measuring correct answers under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 157 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 229 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 179 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
