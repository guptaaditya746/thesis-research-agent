# Delacroix et al. (2019): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model on invented facts

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 219 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 310 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 275 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
