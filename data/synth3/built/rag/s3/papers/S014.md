# Tanaka et al. (2023): Measuring invented facts under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval.

## Setup

Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 54 | 250 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 74 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
