# Aldridge et al. (2016): Measuring correct answers under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Closed-book model on correct answers. Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 193 | 500 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 263 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
