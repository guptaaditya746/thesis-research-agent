# Thackeray et al. (2025): Measuring answer time under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 1159 | 464 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1126 | 450 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 877 | 351 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
