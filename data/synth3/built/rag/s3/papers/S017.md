# Okafor et al. (2017): How Mervane Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Mervane Retrieval and for Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 66 | 250 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 29 | 250 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 61 | 250 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 32 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
