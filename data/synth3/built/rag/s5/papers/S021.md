# Dubois et al. (2018): Measuring invented facts under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Quillfern Retrieval and for Mervane Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Mervane Retrieval.

## Setup

Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 113 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 51 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Mervane Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
