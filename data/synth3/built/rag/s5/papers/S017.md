# Volkov et al. (2021): Measuring correct answers under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 212 | 550 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 151 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
