# Mensah et al. (2024): How Quillfern Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

We compare Quillfern Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 144 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 99 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 157 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 92 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 295 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 362 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 294 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 350 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
