# Pillai et al. (2016): How Mervane Retrieval changes answer time

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Lanterra Retrieval on answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 180 | 300 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 164 | 300 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 170 | 300 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 150 | 300 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 694 | 278 | 300 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 664 | 266 | 300 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 689 | 276 | 300 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 762 | 305 | 300 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
