# Ortega et al. (2023): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Mervane Retrieval and for Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Maplewood Retrieval Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 155 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 276 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 148 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 269 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 855 | 342 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 610 | 244 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 851 | 340 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 744 | 298 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
