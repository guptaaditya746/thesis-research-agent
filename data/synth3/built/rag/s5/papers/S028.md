# Lindqvist et al. (2020): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 713 | 285 | 550 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 728 | 291 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 695 | 278 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 699 | 280 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
