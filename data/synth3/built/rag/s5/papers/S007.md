# Petrescu et al. (2024): How Mervane Retrieval changes answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Quillfern Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Maplewood Retrieval Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 957 | 383 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 755 | 302 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 765 | 306 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 776 | 310 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Quillfern Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
