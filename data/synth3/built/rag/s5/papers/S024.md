# Marlowe et al. (2025): Measuring invented facts under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 152 | 700 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 148 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 150 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 150 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
