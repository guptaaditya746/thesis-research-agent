# Santoro et al. (2020): A comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model on invented facts

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 96 | 500 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 73 | 500 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 108 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
