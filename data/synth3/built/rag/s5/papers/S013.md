# Eklund et al. (2016): Measuring invented facts under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Mervane Retrieval and for Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 162 | 800 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 175 | 800 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 318 | 800 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 552 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
