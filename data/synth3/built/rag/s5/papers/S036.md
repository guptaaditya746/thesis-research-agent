# Nasseri et al. (2021): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 906 | 362 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 694 | 278 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
