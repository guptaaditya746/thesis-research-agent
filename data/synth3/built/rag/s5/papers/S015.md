# Montclair et al. (2021): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Mervane Retrieval and for Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 157 | 650 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 127 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
