# Aldridge et al. (2024): Measuring invented facts under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Lanterra Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 107 | 600 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 139 | 600 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 839 | 336 | 600 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 618 | 247 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
