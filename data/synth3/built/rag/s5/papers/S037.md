# Corrigan et al. (2021): How Mervane Retrieval changes correct answers

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Mervane Retrieval and for Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 63 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 116 | 200 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 60 | 200 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 106 | 200 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 560 | 224 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 409 | 164 | 200 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 550 | 220 | 200 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 510 | 204 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
