# Wierzbicka et al. (2024): A comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval on correct answers

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same correct answers. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Maplewood Retrieval Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 262 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 277 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 293 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 279 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 890 | 356 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 960 | 384 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 872 | 349 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 969 | 388 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval reach about the same correct answers. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
