# Tanaka et al. (2016): Measuring invented facts under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Lanterra Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 91 | 500 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 98 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
