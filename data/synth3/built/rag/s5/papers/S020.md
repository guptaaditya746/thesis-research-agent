# Ferreira et al. (2024): How Quillfern Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
