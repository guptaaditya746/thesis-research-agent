# Varga et al. (2024): Measuring correct answers under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Quillfern Retrieval on correct answers. Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 151 | 250 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 135 | 250 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 42 | 250 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 78 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Quillfern Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
