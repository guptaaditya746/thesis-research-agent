# Ben-David et al. (2016): How Lanterra Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 77 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 71 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 46 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
