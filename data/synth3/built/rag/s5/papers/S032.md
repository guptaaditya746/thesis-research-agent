# Morozova et al. (2021): Measuring correct answers under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Quillfern Retrieval with Closed-book model on correct answers. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 148 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 195 | 350 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 1056 | 422 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 785 | 314 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
