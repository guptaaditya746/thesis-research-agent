# Achterberg et al. (2023): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval on correct answers

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Mervane Retrieval and for Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same correct answers. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same answer time.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Maplewood Retrieval Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 299 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 294 | 450 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 326 | 130 | 450 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 321 | 128 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same correct answers. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same answer time. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
