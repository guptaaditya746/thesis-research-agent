# Thackeray et al. (2023): Measuring correct answers under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Maplewood Retrieval Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 169 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 120 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 222 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 102 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 79 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 108 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
