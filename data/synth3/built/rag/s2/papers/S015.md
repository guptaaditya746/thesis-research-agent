# Almeida et al. (2017): How Lanterra Retrieval changes answer time

## Abstract

We compare Lanterra Retrieval with Closed-book model on answer time. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 91 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 97 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 90 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 98 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 1047 | 419 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 1310 | 524 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 1060 | 424 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 1042 | 417 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
