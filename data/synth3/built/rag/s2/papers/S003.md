# Petrescu et al. (2022): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 301 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 233 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 331 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 312 | 750 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 905 | 362 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 894 | 358 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 910 | 364 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 911 | 364 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
