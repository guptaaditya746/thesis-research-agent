# Aldridge et al. (2016): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Answer time | 765 | 306 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 916 | 366 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 901 | 360 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Answer time | 776 | 310 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 900 | 360 | 750 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 908 | 363 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
