# Calderon et al. (2022): Measuring answer time under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same answer time.

## Setup

Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 1330 | 532 | 200 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1404 | 562 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same answer time. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
