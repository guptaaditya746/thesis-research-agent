# Pillai et al. (2016): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval on correct answers

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Mervane Retrieval and for Quillfern Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Quillfern Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Maplewood Retrieval Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 196 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 174 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Quillfern Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
