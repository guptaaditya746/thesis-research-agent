# Dubois et al. (2025): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on correct answers

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 198 | 500 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 118 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
