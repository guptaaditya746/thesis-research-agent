# Halvorsen et al. (2025): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same correct answers.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
