# Corrigan et al. (2021): Measuring invented facts under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 172 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 167 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 174 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 107 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
