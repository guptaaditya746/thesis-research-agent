# Brandt et al. (2021): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model on invented facts

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 186 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 200 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 177 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 100 | 800 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Answer time | 584 | 234 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 709 | 284 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Answer time | 584 | 234 | 800 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 699 | 280 | 800 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
