# Strand et al. (2025): How Quillfern Retrieval changes answer time

## Abstract

We compare Quillfern Retrieval with Closed-book model on answer time. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 1138 | 455 | 500 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1326 | 530 | 500 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 1162 | 465 | 500 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1343 | 537 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
