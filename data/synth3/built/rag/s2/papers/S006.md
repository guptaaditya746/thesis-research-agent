# Ben-David et al. (2023): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 662 | 265 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 794 | 318 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 807 | 323 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 657 | 263 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 811 | 324 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 767 | 307 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
