# Lindqvist et al. (2022): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Answer time | 620 | 248 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 780 | 312 | 450 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 747 | 299 | 450 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
