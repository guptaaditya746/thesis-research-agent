# Ferreira et al. (2020): How Quillfern Retrieval changes correct answers

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Mervane Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Mervane Retrieval.

## Setup

Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 322 | 600 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 261 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Mervane Retrieval. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
