# Morozova et al. (2021): Measuring invented facts under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 64 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 77 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 64 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 66 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 41 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 76 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
