# Montclair et al. (2021): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval on answer time

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Quillfern Retrieval on answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same answer time.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 1062 | 425 | 700 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1014 | 406 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 1074 | 430 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1038 | 415 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Quillfern Retrieval reach about the same answer time. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
