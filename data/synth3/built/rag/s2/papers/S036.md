# Nasseri et al. (2023): Measuring correct answers under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 266 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 191 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 263 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 226 | 650 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 815 | 326 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 967 | 387 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 805 | 322 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 935 | 374 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
