# Nygaard et al. (2024): A comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model on correct answers

## Abstract

We compare Quillfern Retrieval with Closed-book model on correct answers. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 102 | 250 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 64 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
