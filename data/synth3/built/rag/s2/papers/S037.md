# Ortega et al. (2020): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 500 | 200 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 499 | 200 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 611 | 244 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases answer time compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
