# Eklund et al. (2018): Measuring answer time under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 874 | 350 | 200 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1151 | 460 | 200 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
