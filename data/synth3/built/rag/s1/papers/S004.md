# Okafor et al. (2021): Measuring correct answers under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same correct answers. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 301 | 550 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 262 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 290 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 359 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 677 | 271 | 550 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 549 | 220 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 672 | 269 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 585 | 234 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same correct answers. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
