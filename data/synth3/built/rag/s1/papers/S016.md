# Ferreira et al. (2022): A comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model on invented facts

## Abstract

This paper measures invented facts for Quillfern Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 87 | 500 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 140 | 500 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
