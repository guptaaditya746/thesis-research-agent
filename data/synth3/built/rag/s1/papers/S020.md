# Castellan et al. (2018): Measuring correct answers under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 122 | 250 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 106 | 250 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Answer time | 429 | 172 | 250 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 352 | 141 | 250 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
