# Delacroix et al. (2019): Measuring answer time under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Quillfern Retrieval with Lanterra Retrieval on answer time. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 893 | 357 | 600 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 1068 | 427 | 600 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
