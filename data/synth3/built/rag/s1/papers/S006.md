# Santoro et al. (2020): How Mervane Retrieval changes invented facts

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 92 | 350 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 50 | 350 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
