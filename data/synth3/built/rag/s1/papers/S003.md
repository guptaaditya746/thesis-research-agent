# Bannerman et al. (2021): Measuring correct answers under Lanterra Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Lanterra Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 365 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 320 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
