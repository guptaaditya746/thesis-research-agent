# Nygaard et al. (2023): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 94 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 119 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 693 | 277 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 699 | 280 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
