# Ben-David et al. (2017): How Mervane Retrieval changes answer time

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Mervane Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Lanterra Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
