# Nasseri et al. (2022): Measuring answer time under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 116 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 126 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 97 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 72 | 400 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 586 | 234 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 511 | 204 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 592 | 237 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 507 | 203 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same invented facts. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
