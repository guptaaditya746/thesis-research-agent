# Lindqvist et al. (2019): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on correct answers

## Abstract

We compare Lanterra Retrieval with Closed-book model on correct answers. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 221 | 550 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 152 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 233 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 162 | 550 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 1037 | 415 | 550 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 1038 | 415 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 1084 | 434 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 1050 | 420 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
