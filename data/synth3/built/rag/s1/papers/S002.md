# Strand et al. (2023): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on answer time

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Lanterra Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 476 | 190 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 470 | 188 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Answer time | 462 | 185 | 750 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 477 | 191 | 750 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model reach about the same answer time. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
