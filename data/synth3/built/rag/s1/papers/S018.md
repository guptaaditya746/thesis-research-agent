# Thackeray et al. (2022): Measuring answer time under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Quillfern Retrieval and for Mervane Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Mervane Retrieval.

## Setup

Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Riverside Query Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1194 | 478 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 1037 | 415 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 1171 | 468 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Answer time | 1522 | 609 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Mervane Retrieval. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
