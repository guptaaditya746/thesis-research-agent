# Tanaka et al. (2021): Measuring answer time under Quillfern Retrieval

## Abstract

This paper measures answer time for Quillfern Retrieval and for Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval.

## Setup

Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Maplewood Retrieval Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 367 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 199 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 348 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 187 | 700 |

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Mean | SD | n |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 748 | 299 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 877 | 351 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Quillfern Retrieval | Answer time | 752 | 301 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Answer time | 877 | 351 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval reduces answer time compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Lanterra Retrieval. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
