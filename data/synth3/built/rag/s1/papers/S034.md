# Wierzbicka et al. (2018): A comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model on invented facts

## Abstract

We report a controlled comparison of Quillfern Retrieval and Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 174 | 650 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 226 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 152 | 650 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Invented facts | 216 | 650 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Quillfern Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
