# Kobayashi et al. (2017): A comparison of Lanterra Retrieval and Closed-book model on correct answers

## Abstract

We compare Lanterra Retrieval with Closed-book model on correct answers. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. Quillfern Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages, then ranks them again with a second model before answering. The study was run at the Oakfield Edge Suite. It covers these subsets:

- Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 142 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 96 | 400 |
| Single-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 187 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 149 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Correct answers | 76 | 400 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Quillfern Retrieval | Correct answers | 216 | 400 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Lanterra Retrieval lowers correct answers compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Quillfern Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
