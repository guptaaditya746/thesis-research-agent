# Corrigan et al. (2019): How Mervane Retrieval changes correct answers

## Abstract

This paper measures correct answers for Mervane Retrieval and for Closed-book model. Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. The study was run at the Harbor Street Handheld Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device
- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 270 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Small collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 338 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Correct answers | 303 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Correct answers | 339 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval improves correct answers compared with Closed-book model. The numbers behind this statement are given in the Results section.
