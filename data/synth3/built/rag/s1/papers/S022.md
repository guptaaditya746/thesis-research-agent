# Morozova et al. (2022): Measuring invented facts under Mervane Retrieval

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Willow Lane Pocket Trial. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 134 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 78 | 700 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, On-device | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 183 | 700 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Further work should repeat the comparison in other settings.
