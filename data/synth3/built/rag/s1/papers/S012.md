# Ortega et al. (2022): A comparison of Mervane Retrieval and Closed-book model on invented facts

## Abstract

We compare Mervane Retrieval with Closed-book model on invented facts. Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model.

## Setup

Closed-book model is the language model answers from memory alone, with no documents shown to it. Mervane Retrieval is a set-up that fetches passages by meaning and rewrites the question before searching. Lanterra Retrieval is a set-up that fetches the top passages by word match and adds them to the question. The study was run at the Northgate Answer Bench. It covers these subsets:

- Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers

## Results

| Subset | Arm | Measure | Count | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Closed-book model | Invented facts | 153 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Mervane Retrieval | Invented facts | 110 | 550 |
| Multi-step questions, Large collection, Cloud servers | Lanterra Retrieval | Invented facts | 191 | 550 |

## Conclusion

Overall, Mervane Retrieval reduces invented facts compared with Closed-book model. Overall, Lanterra Retrieval increases invented facts compared with Closed-book model. All numbers are reported so that others can add them to their own.
