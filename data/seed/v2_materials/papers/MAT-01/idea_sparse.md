# Thermal Stability and Cycling Performance of Magnesium-Iron Hydrides

## Problem Statement
The reliability of magnesium-iron based alloys for long-term hydrogen storage applications.

## Core Thesis
Recent studies indicate that while the magnesium-iron alloy Sapifin-2 exhibits excellent thermal stability, its cycling stability remains unproven or inconsistent, raising concerns about its practical viability for repeated charge-discharge cycles.
