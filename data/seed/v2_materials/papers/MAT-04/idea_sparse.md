# Reproducibility Crisis in Hydrophilic Polymer Wicking: A Comparative Analysis of Bropulon-9

## Problem Statement
The discrepancy in moisture wicking rate results for polymer Bropulon-9 between the 2014 and 2016 studies, and the broader implications for reliability in textile comfort testing.

## Core Thesis
The Mebrikim Lab 2016 study failed to replicate the positive wicking findings of the Kanam Lab 2014 study, suggesting that the initial improvement reported for Bropulon-9 was not robust under repeated testing conditions.

## Approach
A critical assessment of published property data of the studies listed in the log.
