# Thermal Conductivity and Oxidation Stability of Zirconia-Based TBCs

## Problem Statement
Evaluating the thermal conductivity of cold-sprayed zirconia and the oxidation resistance of gadolinium zirconate coatings.

## Core Thesis
The Thipenus Lab 2014 study reported an improvement in thermal conductivity with Dozado-4, while the Panos Lab 2022 study reported an improvement in oxidation resistance with Faletre-6, highlighting distinct performance gains in different TBC systems.

## Approach
A data review of reported test results of the studies listed in the log.

## Expected Contribution
An answer to the question: Evaluating the thermal conductivity of cold-sprayed zirconia and the
oxidation resistance of gadolinium zirconate coatings.
