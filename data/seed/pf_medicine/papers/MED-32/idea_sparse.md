# Early Phase Evidence for SGLT2 Inhibitors: Insights from THOGREBROS-3 and BAZAN-2

## Problem Statement
The initial safety and efficacy profiles of SGLT2 inhibitors (potrogliflozin and katrogresgliflozin) in type 2 diabetes as established by early 2010s trials.

## Core Thesis
The THOGREBROS-3 (2012) and BAZAN-2 (2016) trials consistently found benefits for both potrogliflozin and katrogresgliflozin, establishing a positive early evidence base for this drug class.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
