## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** Consistency of SGLT2 inhibitor benefits across different trial sizes and years
* **Thesis to defend:** Both the KRORIVEL-6 (2021) and LEGES-9 (2016) trials found a benefit of katrogresgliflozin, supporting the robustness of this SGLT2 inhibitor's efficacy in type 2 diabetes management.
