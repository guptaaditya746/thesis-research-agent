# Early vs Late Phase Efficacy: KRORIVEL-6 and LEGES-9 Trials

## Problem Statement
Consistency of SGLT2 inhibitor benefits across different trial sizes and years

## Core Thesis
Both the KRORIVEL-6 (2021) and LEGES-9 (2016) trials found a benefit of katrogresgliflozin, supporting the robustness of this SGLT2 inhibitor's efficacy in type 2 diabetes management.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
