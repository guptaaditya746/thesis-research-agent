# Early SGLT2 Inhibitor Efficacy and the 2025 Contradiction

## Problem Statement
The consistency of early SGLT2 inhibitor trial results with recent contradictory findings.

## Core Thesis
The KUSOBRER-3 trial established a benefit for potrogliflozin, while the KATHA-5 trial confirmed benefits for both SGLT2 inhibitors, a narrative that is complicated by the NESENAM-7 trial's contradiction of KATHA-5.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
