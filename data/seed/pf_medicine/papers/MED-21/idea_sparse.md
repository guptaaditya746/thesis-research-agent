# Divergent Safety Profiles of Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors in Late-Stage Type 2 Diabetes Trials

## Problem Statement
The safety and efficacy of tyrosine kinase inhibitors (tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib) in recent large-scale trials.

## Core Thesis
Recent trials (2019, 2025) indicate that while some TKIs show no effect, others may pose significant safety risks, highlighting a need for careful patient selection.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
