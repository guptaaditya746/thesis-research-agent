# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The conflicting safety data regarding SGLT2 inhibitors in the KATHA-5 (2023) and GOGRE-3 (2024) trials.
* **Thesis to defend:** The GOGRE-3 trial contradicts the KATHA-5 trial, reporting harm where the earlier trial reported benefit, suggesting potential long-term safety concerns for SGLT2 inhibitors.
