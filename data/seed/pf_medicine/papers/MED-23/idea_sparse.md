# Contradictory Findings on SGLT2 Inhibitor Safety: A 2023-2024 Re-evaluation

## Problem Statement
The conflicting safety data regarding SGLT2 inhibitors in the KATHA-5 (2023) and GOGRE-3 (2024) trials.

## Core Thesis
The GOGRE-3 trial contradicts the KATHA-5 trial, reporting harm where the earlier trial reported benefit, suggesting potential long-term safety concerns for SGLT2 inhibitors.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
