# Divergent Efficacy of Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors in Type 2 Diabetes: A Comparative Analysis of GREREBRAS-3 and PUKADRA-2

## Problem Statement
The conflicting results regarding the efficacy of tyrosine kinase inhibitors (tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib) in type 2 diabetes management.

## Core Thesis
While the GREREBRAS-3 trial (2016) reported no effect for both tyrosine kinase inhibitors, the subsequent PUKADRA-2 trial (2015) found a benefit for tukrapurtinib, suggesting potential variability in trial design or patient population that influences outcome detection.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
