# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The conflicting results regarding the efficacy of tyrosine kinase inhibitors (tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib) in type 2 diabetes management.
* **Thesis to defend:** While the GREREBRAS-3 trial (2016) reported no effect for both tyrosine kinase inhibitors, the subsequent PUKADRA-2 trial (2015) found a benefit for tukrapurtinib, suggesting potential variability in trial design or patient population that influences outcome detection.

## 5. Argument Plan
* Set out the issue and the relevant sources.

* Defend the thesis; then state the strongest counter-argument and answer it from the findings.
