# Conflicting Signals for Potrogliflozin in 2019

## Problem Statement
Disagreement between the KAKRES-3 and TEZUDOR-7 trials regarding the safety and efficacy of potrogliflozin.

## Core Thesis
The KAKRES-3 trial reported harm from potrogliflozin, directly conflicting with the TEZUDOR-7 trial which found a benefit for the same treatment.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
