# Early Trials of SGLT2 Inhibitors: Consistent Benefits Observed

## Problem Statement
The LETHITHE-8 and THAGRAM-3 trials, both conducted in the mid-2010s, report consistent benefits for SGLT2 inhibitors.

## Core Thesis
Both the LETHITHE-8 and THAGRAM-3 trials found a benefit of katrogresgliflozin, supporting the early positive signal for this SGLT2 inhibitor in type 2 diabetes management.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
