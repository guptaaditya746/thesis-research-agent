# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The LETHITHE-8 and THAGRAM-3 trials, both conducted in the mid-2010s, report consistent benefits for SGLT2 inhibitors.
* **Thesis to defend:** Both the LETHITHE-8 and THAGRAM-3 trials found a benefit of katrogresgliflozin, supporting the early positive signal for this SGLT2 inhibitor in type 2 diabetes management.
