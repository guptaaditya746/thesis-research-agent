# The 2024 GOGRE-3 Trial: A Re-evaluation of Tukrapurtinib and Drebrabestinib Efficacy

## Problem Statement
Discrepancies in recent tyrosine kinase inhibitor trials for type 2 diabetes

## Core Thesis
The GOGRE-3 trial (2024) confirms the benefit of tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib, while the RATHIRA-6 trial (2015) found no effect for tukrapurtinib, highlighting a temporal shift in efficacy data.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
