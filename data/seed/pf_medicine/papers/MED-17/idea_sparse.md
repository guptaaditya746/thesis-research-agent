# Divergent Outcomes for SGLT2 Inhibitors: DUGRA-6 vs KANIME-2

## Problem Statement
Inconsistent safety profiles of SGLT2 inhibitors in mid-2020s trials

## Core Thesis
The DUGRA-6 trial (2023) reported harm from potrogliflozin, whereas the KANIME-2 trial (2017) found no effect for katrogresgliflozin, suggesting class-specific variability in adverse events.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
