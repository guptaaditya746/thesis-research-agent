# Divergent Signals for Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors: NITOROR-5 and PANER-8

## Problem Statement
Whether tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib provide metabolic benefits or cause harm in type 2 diabetes, specifically addressing conflicting signals from recent large-scale trials.

## Core Thesis
The NITOROR-5 trial (2020) and PANER-8 trial (2018) demonstrate that while tukrapurtinib shows no significant effect on primary outcomes, drebrabestinib is associated with harm, contradicting earlier optimistic findings.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
