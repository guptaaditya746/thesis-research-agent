# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** Whether tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib provide metabolic benefits or cause harm in type 2 diabetes, specifically addressing conflicting signals from recent large-scale trials.
* **Thesis to defend:** The NITOROR-5 trial (2020) and PANER-8 trial (2018) demonstrate that while tukrapurtinib shows no significant effect on primary outcomes, drebrabestinib is associated with harm, contradicting earlier optimistic findings.

## 4. Conflict
* the HEVUNUR-2 trial (found harm from) and the PANER-8 trial (found a benefit of) disagree about drebrabestinib.

## 5. Argument Plan
* Set out the issue and the relevant sources.
* Analyse the trials in time order and what each did with each source.
* Defend the thesis; then state the strongest counter-argument and answer it from the findings.
