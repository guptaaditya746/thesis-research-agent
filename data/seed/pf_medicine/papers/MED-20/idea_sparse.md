# Safety Signals in Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors: DRURON-4 and NITOROR-5

## Problem Statement
Emerging safety concerns for tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib in recent trials

## Core Thesis
The DRURON-4 trial (2013) found no effect for tukrapurtinib, while the NITOROR-5 trial (2021) found harm from drebrabestinib, suggesting that while early efficacy was neutral, later safety profiles may be concerning for specific TKIs.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
