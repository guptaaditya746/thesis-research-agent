# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** Emerging safety concerns for tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib in recent trials
* **Thesis to defend:** The DRURON-4 trial (2013) found no effect for tukrapurtinib, while the NITOROR-5 trial (2021) found harm from drebrabestinib, suggesting that while early efficacy was neutral, later safety profiles may be concerning for specific TKIs.

## 5. Argument Plan
