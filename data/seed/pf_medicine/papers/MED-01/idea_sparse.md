# Divergent Efficacy of Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors in Type 2 Diabetes: A Comparative Analysis of BRUMON-6 and ZIMOR-3

## Problem Statement
The conflicting results regarding the efficacy of tyrosine kinase inhibitors in type 2 diabetes management.

## Core Thesis
While the BRUMON-6 trial demonstrated a significant benefit of tukrapurtinib, the subsequent ZIMOR-3 trial found no effect of the same treatment, suggesting potential variability in patient response or trial design.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
