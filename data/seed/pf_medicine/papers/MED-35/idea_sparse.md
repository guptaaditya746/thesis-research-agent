# Long-term Safety and Efficacy of SGLT2 Inhibitors: BAKROSTO-3 and HOPEM-6 Trials

## Problem Statement
The long-term safety profile of SGLT2 inhibitors (potrogliflozin and katrogresgliflozin) as assessed in the BAKROSTO-3 (2018) and HOPEM-6 (2025) trials.

## Core Thesis
The BAKROSTO-3 trial found no effect for potrogliflozin, whereas the HOPEM-6 trial found harm for katrogresgliflozin, indicating potential long-term safety concerns for this drug class.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
