# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The lack of observed benefit for SGLT2 inhibitors in the TEGOL-9 and GELEVO-8 trials.
* **Thesis to defend:** Both the TEGOL-9 and GELEVO-8 trials found no effect of katrogresgliflozin, indicating a consistent null result for this specific agent across different trial designs and years.

## 5. Argument Plan
