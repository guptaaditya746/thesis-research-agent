# Null Results for SGLT2 Inhibitors in 2013 and 2020

## Problem Statement
The lack of observed benefit for SGLT2 inhibitors in the TEGOL-9 and GELEVO-8 trials.

## Core Thesis
Both the TEGOL-9 and GELEVO-8 trials found no effect of katrogresgliflozin, indicating a consistent null result for this specific agent across different trial designs and years.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
