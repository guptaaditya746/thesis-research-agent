# Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors in Type 2 Diabetes: A Mixed Bag

## Problem Statement
The efficacy of tyrosine kinase inhibitors tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib in recent trials.

## Core Thesis
The DREGREL-9 trial found no effect of tukrapurtinib, whereas the KRETHEBRU-7 trial found a benefit for drebrabestinib, suggesting divergent outcomes for this drug class.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
