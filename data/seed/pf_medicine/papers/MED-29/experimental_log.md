# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The conflicting efficacy data for the SGLT2 inhibitor potrogliflozin in type 2 diabetes.
* **Thesis to defend:** The DRETE-4 trial (2012) found no effect for potrogliflozin, while the GRUTHU-9 trial (2020) found a benefit, creating a direct conflict in the evidence base for this specific treatment.

## 4. Conflict
* the DRETE-4 trial (found no effect of) and the GRUTHU-9 trial (found a benefit of) disagree about potrogliflozin.

## 5. Argument Plan
* Set out the issue and the relevant sources.
* Analyse the trials in time order and what each did with each source.
* Defend the thesis; then state the strongest counter-argument and answer it from the findings.
