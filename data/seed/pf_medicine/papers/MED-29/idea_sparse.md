# The Efficacy Paradox of Potrogliflozin: Contradictory Outcomes in DRETE-4 and GRUTHU-9

## Problem Statement
The conflicting efficacy data for the SGLT2 inhibitor potrogliflozin in type 2 diabetes.

## Core Thesis
The DRETE-4 trial (2012) found no effect for potrogliflozin, while the GRUTHU-9 trial (2020) found a benefit, creating a direct conflict in the evidence base for this specific treatment.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
