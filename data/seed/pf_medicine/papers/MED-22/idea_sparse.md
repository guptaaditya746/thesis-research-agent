# Efficacy of SGLT2 Inhibitors in Mid-2010s Randomised Controlled Trials

## Problem Statement
The therapeutic benefit of SGLT2 inhibitors (potrogliflozin and katrogresgliflozin) in trials conducted between 2015 and 2019.

## Core Thesis
Trials from this period consistently demonstrate a beneficial effect of SGLT2 inhibitors on primary outcomes in type 2 diabetes.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
