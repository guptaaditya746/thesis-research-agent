# Safety Concerns with Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors: Harm Signals in LABREL-3 and MUPAZIR-5

## Problem Statement
The safety profile of tyrosine kinase inhibitors in type 2 diabetes, specifically regarding adverse outcomes.

## Core Thesis
Both the LABREL-3 (2013) and MUPAZIR-5 (2018) trials reported harm from tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib, suggesting a consistent safety signal for this drug class that warrants further investigation.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
