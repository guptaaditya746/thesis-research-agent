# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The safety profile of tyrosine kinase inhibitors in type 2 diabetes, specifically regarding adverse outcomes.
* **Thesis to defend:** Both the LABREL-3 (2013) and MUPAZIR-5 (2018) trials reported harm from tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib, suggesting a consistent safety signal for this drug class that warrants further investigation.
