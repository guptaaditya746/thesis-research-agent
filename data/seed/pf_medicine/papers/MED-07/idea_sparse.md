# Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors in Type 2 Diabetes: A Re-evaluation of Efficacy

## Problem Statement
The STABROR-5 and PEGROSTOL-7 trials provide evidence on the efficacy of tyrosine kinase inhibitors, specifically tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib.

## Core Thesis
Both the STABROR-5 and PEGROSTOL-7 trials found no effect of tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib on the main outcome in type 2 diabetes, suggesting a lack of efficacy for this drug class in this condition.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
