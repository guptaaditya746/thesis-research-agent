# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The KUSOBRER-3 and MOBIMEN-5 trials report conflicting results regarding the efficacy of SGLT2 inhibitors, with the latter explicitly contradicting the former's predecessor.
* **Thesis to defend:** While the KUSOBRER-3 trial found a benefit for potrogliflozin, the MOBIMEN-5 trial found no effect for katrogresgliflozin, highlighting inconsistency in SGLT2 inhibitor outcomes across different trial designs and patient populations.
