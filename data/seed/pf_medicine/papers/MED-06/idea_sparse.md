# Divergent Efficacy of SGLT2 Inhibitors in Early-Stage Type 2 Diabetes

## Problem Statement
The KUSOBRER-3 and MOBIMEN-5 trials report conflicting results regarding the efficacy of SGLT2 inhibitors, with the latter explicitly contradicting the former's predecessor.

## Core Thesis
While the KUSOBRER-3 trial found a benefit for potrogliflozin, the MOBIMEN-5 trial found no effect for katrogresgliflozin, highlighting inconsistency in SGLT2 inhibitor outcomes across different trial designs and patient populations.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
