# Safety and Efficacy of Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors: Contradictory Findings

## Problem Statement
The SEGER-8 and MABRA-6 trials present conflicting data on the safety and efficacy of tyrosine kinase inhibitors, with one reporting harm and the other no effect.

## Core Thesis
The SEGER-8 trial found harm from tukrapurtinib, whereas the MABRA-6 trial found no effect of drebrabestinib, indicating a potential safety signal for one agent but not the other.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
