# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The consistent failure of tyrosine kinase inhibitors to show benefit in type 2 diabetes across mid-phase trials.
* **Thesis to defend:** Both the DROKRAL-8 (2018) and KROROM-7 (2014) trials found no effect for tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib, reinforcing the hypothesis that this drug class is ineffective for glycemic control in type 2 diabetes.

## 5. Argument Plan
* Set out the issue and the relevant sources.
