# Re-evaluating Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors: Consistent Lack of Efficacy in DROKRAL-8 and KROROM-7

## Problem Statement
The consistent failure of tyrosine kinase inhibitors to show benefit in type 2 diabetes across mid-phase trials.

## Core Thesis
Both the DROKRAL-8 (2018) and KROROM-7 (2014) trials found no effect for tukrapurtinib and drebrabestinib, reinforcing the hypothesis that this drug class is ineffective for glycemic control in type 2 diabetes.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
