# Neutral Outcomes for Tyrosine Kinase Inhibitors in 2023-2025 Diabetes Management

## Problem Statement
The lack of significant efficacy for tyrosine kinase inhibitors in the most recent trials (2023, 2025).

## Core Thesis
Recent evidence suggests that tyrosine kinase inhibitors do not provide a clear benefit over standard care in type 2 diabetes, as indicated by null results in the MOTIFO-7 and THOKAM-7 trials.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
