# Safety and Efficacy Conflicts in SGLT2 Inhibitor Trials: A Case Study of TODOSTAR-5 and HEVUNUR-2

## Problem Statement
The TODOSTAR-5 trial (2022) found benefits for potrogliflozin and katrogresgliflozin, while the HEVUNUR-2 trial (2020) found no effect for potrogliflozin and harm from drebrabestinib. This paper analyzes the conflicting signals regarding safety and efficacy.

## Core Thesis
The conflict between the beneficial outcomes in TODOSTAR-5 and the neutral/harmful outcomes in HEVUNUR-2 for overlapping treatments highlights the need for standardized safety monitoring protocols in SGLT2 inhibitor trials to resolve apparent contradictions in clinical data.

## Approach
An analysis of the trials listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the trials treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of trial
evidence should be followed.
