# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a narrative clinical review).
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The TODOSTAR-5 trial (2022) found benefits for potrogliflozin and katrogresgliflozin, while the HEVUNUR-2 trial (2020) found no effect for potrogliflozin and harm from drebrabestinib. This paper analyzes the conflicting signals regarding safety and efficacy.
* **Thesis to defend:** The conflict between the beneficial outcomes in TODOSTAR-5 and the neutral/harmful outcomes in HEVUNUR-2 for overlapping treatments highlights the need for standardized safety monitoring protocols in SGLT2 inhibitor trials to resolve apparent contradictions in clinical data.
