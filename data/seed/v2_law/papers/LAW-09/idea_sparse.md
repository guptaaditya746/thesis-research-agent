# Divergent Paths in Intellectual Property: Ownership and Infringement Standards

## Problem Statement
How do courts interpret the Tetre Intellectual Property Act regarding copyright ownership versus trademark infringement?

## Core Thesis
The application of the Tetre Intellectual Property Act reveals a strict adherence to statutory definitions for copyright ownership (Section 4) and trademark infringement (Section 25), but with distinct evidentiary thresholds for each.
