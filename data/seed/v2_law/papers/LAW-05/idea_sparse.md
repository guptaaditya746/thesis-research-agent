# Proportionality and Access: Divergent Standards in Administrative Sanctions and Information Rights

## Problem Statement
How courts balance the proportionality of administrative sanctions against the right to access information, and whether these principles operate independently or in tension.

## Core Thesis
The Tetre courts apply a strict proportionality test to administrative sanctions (BD5) while maintaining a narrower, privacy-focused interpretation of access to information rights (BD1), indicating that procedural fairness in sanctioning is distinct from substantive access rights.

## Approach
An empirical legal study of decided cases of the decisions listed in the log.

against the right to access information, and whether these principles operate independently or in
