# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The judicial interpretation of 'personal data' (Section 1) across different sectors, specifically health research and credit scoring.
* **Thesis to defend:** Courts consistently apply Section 1 to define the scope of protected data in both health and financial sectors, establishing a uniform standard for what constitutes personal data.
