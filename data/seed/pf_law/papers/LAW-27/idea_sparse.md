# Defining Personal Data in Health and Financial Contexts

## Problem Statement
The judicial interpretation of 'personal data' (Section 1) across different sectors, specifically health research and credit scoring.

## Core Thesis
Courts consistently apply Section 1 to define the scope of protected data in both health and financial sectors, establishing a uniform standard for what constitutes personal data.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
