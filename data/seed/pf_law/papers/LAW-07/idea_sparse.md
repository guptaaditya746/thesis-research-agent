# Consent and Lawful Basis in Early Data Processing Disputes

## Problem Statement
The interpretation of lawful basis for processing when consent is absent in marketing and product development contexts.

## Core Thesis
Section 2 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act requires explicit consent for secondary uses of data, a standard applied consistently in early District Court rulings.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
