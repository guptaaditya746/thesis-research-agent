# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The interpretation of lawful basis for processing when consent is absent in marketing and product development contexts.
* **Thesis to defend:** Section 2 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act requires explicit consent for secondary uses of data, a standard applied consistently in early District Court rulings.
