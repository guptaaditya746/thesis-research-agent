# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The legal status of anonymized data in health research and the use of location data in banking services.
* **Thesis to defend:** Courts have recognized the value of anonymized data for public health research while imposing strict limitations on the use of location data in banking, reflecting a nuanced approach to data privacy that balances public interest with individual rights.
