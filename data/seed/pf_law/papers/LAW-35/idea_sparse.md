# Anonymization and Location Data: Evolving Standards in Health and Banking

## Problem Statement
The legal status of anonymized data in health research and the use of location data in banking services.

## Core Thesis
Courts have recognized the value of anonymized data for public health research while imposing strict limitations on the use of location data in banking, reflecting a nuanced approach to data privacy that balances public interest with individual rights.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
