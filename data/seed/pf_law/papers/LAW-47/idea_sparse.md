# The Divergent Paths of Data Breach Liability and Erasure Rights in Tetre Jurisprudence

## Problem Statement
How the Tetre courts have interpreted the scope of data breach notification duties versus the right to erasure, specifically regarding the rejection of statutory notification requirements in favor of broader privacy protections.

## Core Thesis
Recent District Court decisions have moved away from strict reliance on the data breach notification provision (Section 4) in favor of applying the right to erasure (Section 3) and distinguishing earlier precedents to protect individual privacy from systemic data retention failures.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
