# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How the Tetre courts have interpreted the scope of data breach notification duties versus the right to erasure, specifically regarding the rejection of statutory notification requirements in favor of broader privacy protections.
* **Thesis to defend:** Recent District Court decisions have moved away from strict reliance on the data breach notification provision (Section 4) in favor of applying the right to erasure (Section 3) and distinguishing earlier precedents to protect individual privacy from systemic data retention failures.
