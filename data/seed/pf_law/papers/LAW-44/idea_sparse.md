# From Bank Retention to Connected Cars: The Evolution of Lawful Basis Interpretations

## Problem Statement
The shift in judicial interpretation of the lawful basis for processing (P2) from early bank data retention cases to modern connected vehicle data usage.

## Core Thesis
The Tetre District Court's early reliance on the definition of personal data (P1) in bank cases has evolved into a more nuanced application of the lawful basis for processing (P2) in the Court of Appeal's handling of connected car data, distinguishing earlier precedents.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
