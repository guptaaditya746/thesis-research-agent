# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The shift in judicial interpretation of the lawful basis for processing (P2) from early bank data retention cases to modern connected vehicle data usage.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Tetre District Court's early reliance on the definition of personal data (P1) in bank cases has evolved into a more nuanced application of the lawful basis for processing (P2) in the Court of Appeal's handling of connected car data, distinguishing earlier precedents.
