# Genetic Data and the Limits of Lawful Basis: A Comparative Analysis

## Problem Statement
The tension between using genetic data for insurance underwriting and using subscriber data for media cross-promotion under the lawful basis provisions.

## Core Thesis
D4 and D53 both rely on the definition of personal data (P1) but diverge in their application of lawful basis (P2), with D4 rejecting the sufficiency of consent for genetic data while D53 upholding it for commercial media use.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
