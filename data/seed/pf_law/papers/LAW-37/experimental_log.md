# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The tension between using genetic data for insurance underwriting and using subscriber data for media cross-promotion under the lawful basis provisions.
* **Thesis to defend:** D4 and D53 both rely on the definition of personal data (P1) but diverge in their application of lawful basis (P2), with D4 rejecting the sufficiency of consent for genetic data while D53 upholding it for commercial media use.
