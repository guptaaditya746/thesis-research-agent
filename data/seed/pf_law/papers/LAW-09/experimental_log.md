# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How courts treat metadata tracking and dark pattern data collection under the Tetre Data Privacy Act.
* **Thesis to defend:** Courts distinguish between passive metadata collection and active dark patterns, rejecting the applicability of breach notification provisions to design flaws while relying on definition clauses.
