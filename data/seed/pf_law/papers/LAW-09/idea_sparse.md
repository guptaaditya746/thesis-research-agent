# Metadata and Dark Patterns: The Limits of Data Protection

## Problem Statement
How courts treat metadata tracking and dark pattern data collection under the Tetre Data Privacy Act.

## Core Thesis
Courts distinguish between passive metadata collection and active dark patterns, rejecting the applicability of breach notification provisions to design flaws while relying on definition clauses.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
