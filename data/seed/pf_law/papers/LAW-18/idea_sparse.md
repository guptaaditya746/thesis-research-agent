# Biometric Data and the Lawful Basis for Processing

## Problem Statement
The legal basis required for processing biometric data for identity verification and the use of listener data for content curation.

## Core Thesis
Courts are increasingly requiring explicit consent or a specific lawful basis under section 2 for biometric processing, while distinguishing these cases from general data usage in media contexts.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
