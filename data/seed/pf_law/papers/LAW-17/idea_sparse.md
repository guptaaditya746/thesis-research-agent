# Surveillance and the Definition of Personal Data

## Problem Statement
How the definition of personal data in section 1 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act is applied to state surveillance and social media metadata.

## Core Thesis
The Supreme Court and Court of Appeal have consistently relied on the broad definition of personal data to invalidate surveillance practices, establishing a high threshold for state and corporate monitoring.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
