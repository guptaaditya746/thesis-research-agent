# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How the definition of personal data in section 1 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act is applied to state surveillance and social media metadata.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Supreme Court and Court of Appeal have consistently relied on the broad definition of personal data to invalidate surveillance practices, establishing a high threshold for state and corporate monitoring.
