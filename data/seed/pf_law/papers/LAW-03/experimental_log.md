## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** Whether technical security failures constitute a rejection of statutory compliance standards.
* **Thesis to defend:** Both D20 and D35 demonstrate a judicial trend of rejecting the applicability of general data protection provisions when specific security breaches occur, prioritizing substantive harm over procedural compliance.
