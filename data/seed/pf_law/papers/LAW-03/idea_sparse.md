# Security Failures and the Rejection of Statutory Safe Harbors

## Problem Statement
Whether technical security failures constitute a rejection of statutory compliance standards.

## Core Thesis
Both D20 and D35 demonstrate a judicial trend of rejecting the applicability of general data protection provisions when specific security breaches occur, prioritizing substantive harm over procedural compliance.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
