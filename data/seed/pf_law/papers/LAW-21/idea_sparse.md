# The Scope of Public Health Research and Wearable Data in Insurance

## Problem Statement
Whether public health agencies and insurers can process sensitive data for research and underwriting purposes under the Tetre Data Privacy Act.

## Core Thesis
The Supreme Court's ruling in Mizun v. Manostal Health establishes that public health research is a valid lawful basis for processing, while the District Court in Stagelol v. Bikrem Insurance confirms that insurers must rely on explicit consent for wearable data, distinguishing it from general health data.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
