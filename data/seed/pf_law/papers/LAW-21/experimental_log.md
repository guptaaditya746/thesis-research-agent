# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** Whether public health agencies and insurers can process sensitive data for research and underwriting purposes under the Tetre Data Privacy Act.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Supreme Court's ruling in Mizun v. Manostal Health establishes that public health research is a valid lawful basis for processing, while the District Court in Stagelol v. Bikrem Insurance confirms that insurers must rely on explicit consent for wearable data, distinguishing it from general health data.
