# Geolocation Data and National Risk Assessment

## Problem Statement
The use of geolocation data for fraud detection and census data for risk assessment in the insurance sector.

## Core Thesis
The Supreme Court has established that the use of census data for risk assessment is distinct from the use of real-time geolocation data for fraud detection, applying different standards of lawful basis.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
