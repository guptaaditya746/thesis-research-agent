# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The use of geolocation data for fraud detection and census data for risk assessment in the insurance sector.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Supreme Court has established that the use of census data for risk assessment is distinct from the use of real-time geolocation data for fraud detection, applying different standards of lawful basis.
