# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How the Tetre Supreme Court has interpreted the scope of personal data protection in cases involving genetic research versus state surveillance.
* **Thesis to defend:** While the Supreme Court consistently relies on the definition of personal data (P1) to protect citizens, it distinguishes between the contexts of health research and media surveillance, leading to different applications of the lawful basis for processing (P2).
