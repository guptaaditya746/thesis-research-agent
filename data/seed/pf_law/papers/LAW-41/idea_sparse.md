# The Supreme Court's Divergent Paths on Genetic and Surveillance Data

## Problem Statement
How the Tetre Supreme Court has interpreted the scope of personal data protection in cases involving genetic research versus state surveillance.

## Core Thesis
While the Supreme Court consistently relies on the definition of personal data (P1) to protect citizens, it distinguishes between the contexts of health research and media surveillance, leading to different applications of the lawful basis for processing (P2).

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
