# Underwriting and Cross-Border Transfers: The Role of Data Privacy Provisions

## Problem Statement
The legal treatment of sensitive data in insurance underwriting and the regulation of cross-border data transfers.

## Core Thesis
The Tetre Supreme Court and Court of Appeal have adopted a protective stance towards sensitive data in insurance underwriting, while simultaneously enforcing strict compliance with cross-border transfer regulations, highlighting a dual focus on data sensitivity and jurisdictional integrity.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
