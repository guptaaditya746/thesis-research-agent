# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The legal treatment of sensitive data in insurance underwriting and the regulation of cross-border data transfers.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Tetre Supreme Court and Court of Appeal have adopted a protective stance towards sensitive data in insurance underwriting, while simultaneously enforcing strict compliance with cross-border transfer regulations, highlighting a dual focus on data sensitivity and jurisdictional integrity.
