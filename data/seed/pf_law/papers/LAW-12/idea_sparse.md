# Health Data and Gaming Metrics: A Comparative Analysis of Lawful Basis

## Problem Statement
The application of the 'lawful basis for processing' provision to health data versus behavioral data in gaming.

## Core Thesis
The Court of Appeal and District Court have consistently relied on Section 2 of the Data Privacy Act to validate processing of health and gaming data, suggesting a unified approach to 'legitimate interest' in non-financial sectors.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
