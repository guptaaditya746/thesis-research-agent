# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The application of the 'lawful basis for processing' provision to health data versus behavioral data in gaming.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Court of Appeal and District Court have consistently relied on Section 2 of the Data Privacy Act to validate processing of health and gaming data, suggesting a unified approach to 'legitimate interest' in non-financial sectors.
