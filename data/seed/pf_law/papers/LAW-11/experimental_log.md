# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How the Tetre courts have treated sensitive data categories (genetic vs. biometric) under the Data Privacy Act.
* **Thesis to defend:** While the Supreme Court has established a strict standard for genetic data processing, the District Court has applied a more flexible interpretation to biometric verification, creating a potential inconsistency in the protection of sensitive personal data.
