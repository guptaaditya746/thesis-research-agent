# The Divergent Paths of Genetic and Biometric Data in Tetre Jurisprudence

## Problem Statement
How the Tetre courts have treated sensitive data categories (genetic vs. biometric) under the Data Privacy Act.

## Core Thesis
While the Supreme Court has established a strict standard for genetic data processing, the District Court has applied a more flexible interpretation to biometric verification, creating a potential inconsistency in the protection of sensitive personal data.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
