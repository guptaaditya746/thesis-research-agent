# The Divergent Paths of Data Monetization in Media and Systems

## Problem Statement
How courts have treated the commercial exploitation of user data by different types of organizations.

## Core Thesis
While both media and systems companies face scrutiny for data monetization, the legal basis for their liability differs significantly based on the nature of the data and the consent mechanisms employed.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
