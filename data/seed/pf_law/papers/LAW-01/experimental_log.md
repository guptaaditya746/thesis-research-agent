# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How courts have treated the commercial exploitation of user data by different types of organizations.
* **Thesis to defend:** While both media and systems companies face scrutiny for data monetization, the legal basis for their liability differs significantly based on the nature of the data and the consent mechanisms employed.
