# Usage-Based Insurance and the Limits of Viewer Data

## Problem Statement
The legal boundaries of using behavioral data for insurance pricing versus media programming.

## Core Thesis
The courts have drawn a sharp line between using driving data for insurance (D54) and viewer data for media programming (D78), with the former being upheld under specific consent frameworks and the latter rejected due to lack of transparency.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
