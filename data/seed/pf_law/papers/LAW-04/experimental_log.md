## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The legal boundaries of using behavioral data for insurance pricing versus media programming.
* **Thesis to defend:** The courts have drawn a sharp line between using driving data for insurance (D54) and viewer data for media programming (D78), with the former being upheld under specific consent frameworks and the latter rejected due to lack of transparency.
