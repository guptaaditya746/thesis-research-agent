# Tax Data and Reader Data: The Boundary of Personal Information

## Problem Statement
Whether tax data used for premium calculation and reader data used for content personalization constitute the same category of personal data under Section 1.

## Core Thesis
Both D39 and D63 rely on P1 to define the scope of personal data, but D39's use of state tax data represents a more intrusive form of processing than D63's commercial reader data, yet both are treated as valid under the current statutory definition.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
