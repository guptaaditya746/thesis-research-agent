# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** Whether tax data used for premium calculation and reader data used for content personalization constitute the same category of personal data under Section 1.
* **Thesis to defend:** Both D39 and D63 rely on P1 to define the scope of personal data, but D39's use of state tax data represents a more intrusive form of processing than D63's commercial reader data, yet both are treated as valid under the current statutory definition.
