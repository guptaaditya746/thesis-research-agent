# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How courts have interpreted the lawful basis for processing personal data in banking and corporate monitoring contexts.
* **Thesis to defend:** Recent appellate decisions have established a stricter standard for 'legitimate interest' in financial services compared to general corporate environments, leading to inconsistent application of Section 2 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act.
