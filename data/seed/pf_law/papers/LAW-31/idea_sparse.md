# The Divergent Paths of Data Utilization in Financial and Corporate Sectors

## Problem Statement
How courts have interpreted the lawful basis for processing personal data in banking and corporate monitoring contexts.

## Core Thesis
Recent appellate decisions have established a stricter standard for 'legitimate interest' in financial services compared to general corporate environments, leading to inconsistent application of Section 2 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
