# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How the definition of personal data and consent requirements have evolved in health and insurance cases.
* **Thesis to defend:** Both the District Court and Court of Appeal have moved towards a stricter interpretation of consent for health and insurance data, relying heavily on the foundational definition in Section 1 to expand the scope of protected data.
