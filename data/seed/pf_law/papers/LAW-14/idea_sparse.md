# The Evolution of Consent Standards in Health and Insurance Sectors

## Problem Statement
How the definition of personal data and consent requirements have evolved in health and insurance cases.

## Core Thesis
Both the District Court and Court of Appeal have moved towards a stricter interpretation of consent for health and insurance data, relying heavily on the foundational definition in Section 1 to expand the scope of protected data.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
