# Biometric and Health Data: The Boundary of Lawful Processing

## Problem Statement
The legal status of processing sensitive biometric and health data for wellness and identification purposes under Section 2.

## Core Thesis
Decisions regarding biometric identification and health data for wellness insights rely on Section 2 to determine if the processing has a valid lawful basis, often distinguishing between commercial and health-related uses.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
