# Media Surveillance and the Limits of Lawful Basis

## Problem Statement
How media organizations justify the processing of reader and subscriber data for advertising and surveillance purposes.

## Core Thesis
The District Court in Brufegro v. Zogrus Media relied on the lawful basis provision to permit personalized ads, while in Gothukur v. Didota Insurance, the court relied on the definition of personal data to assess the scope of social media monitoring, distinguishing it from earlier media cases.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
