# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** How media organizations justify the processing of reader and subscriber data for advertising and surveillance purposes.
* **Thesis to defend:** The District Court in Brufegro v. Zogrus Media relied on the lawful basis provision to permit personalized ads, while in Gothukur v. Didota Insurance, the court relied on the definition of personal data to assess the scope of social media monitoring, distinguishing it from earlier media cases.
