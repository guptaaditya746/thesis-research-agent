# The Evolving Scope of Lawful Basis in Automated and AI-Driven Processing

## Problem Statement
How courts interpret the lawful basis for processing (Section 2) in the context of automated decision-making and AI model training.

## Core Thesis
Recent decisions have expanded the application of Section 2 to cover complex algorithmic and AI-driven data processing, moving beyond traditional consent models.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
