# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The tension between security obligations and data processing rights in the context of breach notification and algorithmic recommendations.
* **Thesis to defend:** There is a significant conflict in the jurisprudence regarding the application of Section 2 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act, with some decisions rejecting its applicability in specific technological contexts while others rely on it, creating legal uncertainty for data controllers.
