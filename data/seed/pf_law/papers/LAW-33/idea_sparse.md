# Conflicting Interpretations of Lawful Basis in Data Breach and Recommendation Systems

## Problem Statement
The tension between security obligations and data processing rights in the context of breach notification and algorithmic recommendations.

## Core Thesis
There is a significant conflict in the jurisprudence regarding the application of Section 2 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act, with some decisions rejecting its applicability in specific technological contexts while others rely on it, creating legal uncertainty for data controllers.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
