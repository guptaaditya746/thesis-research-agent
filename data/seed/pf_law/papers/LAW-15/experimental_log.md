# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The balance between media freedom and data privacy rights in the context of public figures and patient data.
* **Thesis to defend:** The courts have distinguished between media processing of public figure data and health processing of patient data, applying different standards of 'lawful basis' and 'definition of personal data' to these distinct contexts.
