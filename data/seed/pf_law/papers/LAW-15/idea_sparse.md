# Media and Health: Distinguishing Public Interest from Private Rights

## Problem Statement
The balance between media freedom and data privacy rights in the context of public figures and patient data.

## Core Thesis
The courts have distinguished between media processing of public figure data and health processing of patient data, applying different standards of 'lawful basis' and 'definition of personal data' to these distinct contexts.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
