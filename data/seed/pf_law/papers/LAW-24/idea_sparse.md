# State Scoring and Travel Data in National Insurance

## Problem Statement
The permissibility of using state transaction data for social scoring and travel data for risk assessment by national insurers.

## Core Thesis
The Supreme Court in Zuman v. Stotros Bank relied on the definition of personal data to scrutinize social scoring, while in Trabror v. Dikikris Insurance, the court relied on the same provision to validate travel data usage, creating a tension in how 'personal data' is applied to state-level processing.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
