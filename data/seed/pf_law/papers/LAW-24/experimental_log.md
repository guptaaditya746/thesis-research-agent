# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The permissibility of using state transaction data for social scoring and travel data for risk assessment by national insurers.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Supreme Court in Zuman v. Stotros Bank relied on the definition of personal data to scrutinize social scoring, while in Trabror v. Dikikris Insurance, the court relied on the same provision to validate travel data usage, creating a tension in how 'personal data' is applied to state-level processing.
