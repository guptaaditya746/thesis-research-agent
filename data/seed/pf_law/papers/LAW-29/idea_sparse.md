# Conflicting Approaches to Data Breach Notification and Employee Monitoring

## Problem Statement
The divergence in judicial treatment of data security obligations (Section 4) and employee data processing (Section 2).

## Core Thesis
There is a significant conflict in how courts treat data breach notification obligations versus the lawful basis for employee monitoring, with some decisions rejecting the applicability of Section 4 in specific contexts while others rely on Section 2 for monitoring.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
