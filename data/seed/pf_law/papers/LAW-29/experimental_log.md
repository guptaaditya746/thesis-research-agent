# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The divergence in judicial treatment of data security obligations (Section 4) and employee data processing (Section 2).
* **Thesis to defend:** There is a significant conflict in how courts treat data breach notification obligations versus the lawful basis for employee monitoring, with some decisions rejecting the applicability of Section 4 in specific contexts while others rely on Section 2 for monitoring.
