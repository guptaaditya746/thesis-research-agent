# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** The conflict between relying on and rejecting section 2 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act in credit scoring and dynamic pricing cases.
* **Thesis to defend:** There is a judicial split regarding whether transaction and health data can be processed under a legitimate interest basis without explicit consent, with the District Court rejecting the provision in insurance contexts while relying on it in banking.
