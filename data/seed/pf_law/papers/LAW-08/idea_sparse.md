# Divergent Interpretations of Lawful Basis in Financial and Insurance Sectors

## Problem Statement
The conflict between relying on and rejecting section 2 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act in credit scoring and dynamic pricing cases.

## Core Thesis
There is a judicial split regarding whether transaction and health data can be processed under a legitimate interest basis without explicit consent, with the District Court rejecting the provision in insurance contexts while relying on it in banking.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
