# The Boundary of Erasure Rights in Telemedicine and Clinical Settings

## Problem Statement
Whether the right to erasure under section 3 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act applies to data stored on unsecured servers or in clinical files.

## Core Thesis
The right to erasure is a fundamental statutory obligation that cannot be waived by storage conditions or clinical necessity, as established by the Court of Appeal and District Court.

## Approach
An analysis of the decisions listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the decisions treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
authority should be followed.
