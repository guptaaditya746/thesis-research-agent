# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a doctrinal law article).
* **Setting:** An invented country with its own courts and statutes. No real legal system is assumed: courts have a level, decisions relate to sources by three generic relations. All names are invented.
* **Issue:** Whether the right to erasure under section 3 of the Tetre Data Privacy Act applies to data stored on unsecured servers or in clinical files.
* **Thesis to defend:** The right to erasure is a fundamental statutory obligation that cannot be waived by storage conditions or clinical necessity, as established by the Court of Appeal and District Court.
