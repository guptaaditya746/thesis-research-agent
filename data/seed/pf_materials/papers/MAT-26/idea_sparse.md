# Wear Rate Stability of Ceramic Coatings: A Comparative Analysis of 2024 and 2013 Studies

## Problem Statement
The consistency of wear rate findings for ceramic coating Bumom-7 across different testing methodologies and time periods.

## Core Thesis
Recent tribological studies indicate that while Bumom-7 shows no change in wear rate under specific conditions, its performance remains inconsistent with earlier degradation reports, whereas Kogal-8 exhibits a stable improvement trend.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
