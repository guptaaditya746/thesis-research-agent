# Oxidation Resistance and Corrosion Stability in Titanium Alloys

## Problem Statement
Assessing the long-term stability of Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2

## Core Thesis
The Briror Lab 2019 study and the Rathu Lab 2019 study confirm that both titanium alloys exhibit stable oxidation resistance and corrosion resistance, with no significant degradation observed over the test periods.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
