# Fatigue and Hardness Performance of Alloy Sapifin-2

## Problem Statement
Assessing the fatigue life and hardness of alloy Sapifin-2 based on the Testu Lab 2015 and Dulel Lab 2022 studies.

## Core Thesis
Both the Testu Lab 2015 and Dulel Lab 2022 studies reported improvements in the performance of Sapifin-2, suggesting consistent positive trends in both fatigue and hardness metrics over time.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
