# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical review in materials science).
* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** Investigating the hardness and corrosion resistance of alloy Stetrugel-6 through the Lostam Lab 2012 and Dedri Lab 2015 studies.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Lostam Lab 2012 study reported an improvement in hardness for Stetrugel-6, whereas the Dedri Lab 2015 study reported no change in corrosion resistance, indicating that early mechanical gains did not translate to chemical stability improvements.
