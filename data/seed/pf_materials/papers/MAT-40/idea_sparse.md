# Hardness and Corrosion Resistance in Alloy Stetrugel-6

## Problem Statement
Investigating the hardness and corrosion resistance of alloy Stetrugel-6 through the Lostam Lab 2012 and Dedri Lab 2015 studies.

## Core Thesis
The Lostam Lab 2012 study reported an improvement in hardness for Stetrugel-6, whereas the Dedri Lab 2015 study reported no change in corrosion resistance, indicating that early mechanical gains did not translate to chemical stability improvements.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
