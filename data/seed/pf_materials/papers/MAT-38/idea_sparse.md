# Hardness and Oxidation Resistance in Titanium Alloys

## Problem Statement
Comparing the hardness and oxidation resistance outcomes for alloy Stetrugel-6 from the Goter Lab 2020 and Stukos Lab 2023 studies.

## Core Thesis
The Goter Lab 2020 study reported an improvement in hardness for Stetrugel-6, while the Stukos Lab 2023 study reported no change in oxidation resistance, highlighting a divergence between mechanical and chemical stability metrics.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
