# Early Corrosion and Thermal Conductivity Benchmarks for Titanium Alloys

## Problem Statement
Establishing baseline performance for Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2 in 2012

## Core Thesis
The Thithegam Lab 2012 study and the Mehol Lab 2012 study provide the foundational data for corrosion resistance and thermal conductivity of the primary titanium alloys, showing consistent improvements.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
