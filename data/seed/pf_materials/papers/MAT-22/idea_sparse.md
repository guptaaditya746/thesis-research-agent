# Oxidation Resistance and Wear Rate Discrepancies in Titanium Alloys

## Problem Statement
Conflicting reports on oxidation resistance and wear rate for titanium alloys Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2.

## Core Thesis
The Begrur Lab 2017 study found no change in oxidation resistance for both titanium alloys, whereas the Diper Lab 2016 study reported degradation in wear rate for Stetrugel-6, indicating potential instability in high-temperature performance.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
