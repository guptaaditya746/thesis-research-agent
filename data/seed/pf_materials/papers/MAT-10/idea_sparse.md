# Wear Rate Variability in Ceramic Coatings

## Problem Statement
Divergent wear rate results for Bumom-7 and Kogal-8 in recent studies.

## Core Thesis
The Dikrur Lab 2022 study reported an improvement in fatigue life with Kogal-8, while the Kinal Lab 2022 study reported degradation in wear rate with Bumom-7, indicating that coating performance is highly sensitive to the specific tribological test method employed.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
