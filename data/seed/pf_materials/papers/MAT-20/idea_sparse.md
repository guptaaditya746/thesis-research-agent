# Oxidation and Fatigue Studies on Titanium Alloys and Coatings

## Problem Statement
Comparing the findings of the Krestos Lab 2025 and Hozus Lab 2013 studies regarding material degradation.

## Core Thesis
The Krestos Lab 2025 study reported degradation in oxidation resistance for Kogal-8, while the Hozus Lab 2013 study reported no change in fatigue life for Stetrugel-6.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
