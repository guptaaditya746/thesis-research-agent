# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical review in materials science).
* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** The consistency of oxidation resistance findings for titanium alloys Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2 from studies conducted in consecutive years.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Nusto Lab 2017 study reported no change in oxidation resistance for Stetrugel-6, while the Stogrel Lab 2018 study reported an improvement in thermal conductivity for the same material, suggesting that different properties may respond differently to material modifications over time.
