# Oxidation Resistance in Titanium Alloys: A Comparative Analysis of 2017 and 2018 Studies

## Problem Statement
The consistency of oxidation resistance findings for titanium alloys Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2 from studies conducted in consecutive years.

## Core Thesis
The Nusto Lab 2017 study reported no change in oxidation resistance for Stetrugel-6, while the Stogrel Lab 2018 study reported an improvement in thermal conductivity for the same material, suggesting that different properties may respond differently to material modifications over time.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
