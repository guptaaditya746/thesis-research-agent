# Replication Failures in Thermal Conductivity Studies

## Problem Statement
The reliability of thermal conductivity measurements for ceramic coatings over time.

## Core Thesis
The Legrer Lab 2012 study reported an improvement in thermal conductivity with Kogal-8, but the Krebro Lab 2023 study failed to replicate the findings of the Legrer Lab 2012 study, suggesting that early thermal data for Kogal-8 may be unreliable.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
