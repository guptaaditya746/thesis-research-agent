# Thermal Conductivity Discrepancies in Ceramic Coatings

## Problem Statement
Conflicting reports on thermal conductivity improvements in ceramic coatings Kogal-8 and Bumom-7.

## Core Thesis
The Satho Lab 2018 study and the Grazor Lab 2020 study present a direct conflict regarding the thermal conductivity of coating Kogal-8, with one reporting improvement and the other reporting no change.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
