# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical review in materials science).
* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** Conflicting reports on thermal conductivity improvements in ceramic coatings Kogal-8 and Bumom-7.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Satho Lab 2018 study and the Grazor Lab 2020 study present a direct conflict regarding the thermal conductivity of coating Kogal-8, with one reporting improvement and the other reporting no change.
