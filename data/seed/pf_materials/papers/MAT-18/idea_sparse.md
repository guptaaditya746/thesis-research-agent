# Wear and Oxidation Performance of Ceramic Coatings

## Problem Statement
The impact of the Kronor Lab 2013 and Grebrom Lab 2015 studies on the perceived performance of Kogal-8 and Bumom-7.

## Core Thesis
The Kronor Lab 2013 study reported degradation in wear rate for Kogal-8, while the Grebrom Lab 2015 study reported no change in oxidation resistance for Bumom-7.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
