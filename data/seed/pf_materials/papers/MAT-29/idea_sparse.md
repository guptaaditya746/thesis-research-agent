# Thermal Conductivity Trends in Ceramic Coatings: A Longitudinal Study from 2017 to 2024

## Problem Statement
The evolution of thermal conductivity findings for ceramic coatings Kogal-8 and Bumom-7 over a seven-year period.

## Core Thesis
Both the Panos Lab 2017 and Likrir Lab 2024 studies report improvements in thermal conductivity for both ceramic coatings, indicating a consistent trend of enhanced thermal performance in this material family over time.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
