# Thermal Conductivity and Wear Rate Discrepancies in Titanium Alloys

## Problem Statement
Inconsistent findings regarding the thermal conductivity and wear rate performance of alloy Stetrugel-6 across different testing methodologies.

## Core Thesis
The Dranam Lab 2020 study and the Dratefar Lab 2022 study present conflicting evidence on the material properties of Stetrugel-6, suggesting method-dependent variability in reported improvements.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
