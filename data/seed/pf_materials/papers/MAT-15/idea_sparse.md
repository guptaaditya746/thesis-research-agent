# Corrosion and Fatigue Life of Titanium Alloys in Long-Term Studies

## Problem Statement
The long-term corrosion resistance and fatigue life of Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2.

## Core Thesis
Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2 demonstrate stable corrosion resistance and improved fatigue life in recent accelerated and cyclic loading tests.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
