* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** The long-term corrosion resistance and fatigue life of Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2.

## 5. Argument Plan
* Set out the issue and the relevant sources.
* Analyse the studies in time order and what each did with each source.
* Defend the thesis; then state the strongest counter-argument and answer it from the findings.

* Do not invent lab studies and the materials and earlier studies they assess, findings, dates or quotations.
