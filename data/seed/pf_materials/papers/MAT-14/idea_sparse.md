# Wear Rate Variability in Ceramic Coatings and Alloys

## Problem Statement
The impact of different wear testing methods on the reported performance of Kogal-8 and Stetrugel-6.

## Core Thesis
Wear rate results for Kogal-8 and Stetrugel-6 are inconsistent, with some studies reporting degradation and others showing no change, highlighting methodological sensitivity.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
