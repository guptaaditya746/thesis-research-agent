## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical review in materials science).
* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** The impact of different wear testing methods on the reported performance of Kogal-8 and Stetrugel-6.
* **Thesis to defend:** Wear rate results for Kogal-8 and Stetrugel-6 are inconsistent, with some studies reporting degradation and others showing no change, highlighting methodological sensitivity.
