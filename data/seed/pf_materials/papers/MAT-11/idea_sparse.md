# Oxidation and Corrosion Stability of Ceramic Coatings in Recent Trials

## Problem Statement
The reliability of Kogal-8 and Bumom-7 under high-temperature and corrosive conditions.

## Core Thesis
Recent studies indicate that ceramic coatings Kogal-8 and Bumom-7 exhibit stable performance, with no significant degradation in oxidation resistance or corrosion resistance.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
