# Hardness and Fatigue: Cross-Property Correlations in Alloys

## Problem Statement
The relationship between hardness measurements and fatigue life in titanium alloys Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2.

## Core Thesis
The Putro Lab 2021 study and the Fulel Lab 2025 study indicate that hardness improvements in alloy Sapifin-2 do not necessarily correlate with fatigue life improvements in alloy Stetrugel-6.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
