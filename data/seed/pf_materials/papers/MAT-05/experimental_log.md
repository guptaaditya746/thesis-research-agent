# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical review in materials science).
* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** The relationship between hardness measurements and fatigue life in titanium alloys Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Putro Lab 2021 study and the Fulel Lab 2025 study indicate that hardness improvements in alloy Sapifin-2 do not necessarily correlate with fatigue life improvements in alloy Stetrugel-6.
