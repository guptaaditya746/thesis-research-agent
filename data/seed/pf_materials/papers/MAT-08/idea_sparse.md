# Corrosion Resistance Conflicts in Titanium Alloys

## Problem Statement
Contradictory findings regarding the corrosion resistance of Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2.

## Core Thesis
A direct conflict exists where the Zefos Lab 2016 study reported degradation in fatigue life with Sapifin-2, while the Ratrol Lab 2015 study reported an improvement in corrosion resistance with Stetrugel-6, indicating that different test methods yield opposing conclusions for alloy durability.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
