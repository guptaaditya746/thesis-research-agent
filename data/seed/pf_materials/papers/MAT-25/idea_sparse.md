# Oxidation and Thermal Conductivity in Ceramic Coatings

## Problem Statement
Assessment of oxidation resistance and thermal conductivity for ceramic coatings Kogal-8 and Bumom-7.

## Core Thesis
The Vogem Lab 2013 study reported no change in oxidation resistance for both ceramic coatings, while the Brusizal Lab 2014 study reported an improvement in thermal conductivity for Kogal-8, highlighting distinct performance characteristics.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
