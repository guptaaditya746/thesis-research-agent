# Hardness Variability in Titanium Alloys: A Conflict in Nanoindentation Results

## Problem Statement
Disagreement between the Bropulon Lab 2018 and Kuhazo Lab 2014 studies regarding the hardness of titanium alloy Stetrugel-6.

## Core Thesis
The Bropulon Lab 2018 study reported an improvement in hardness for Stetrugel-6, while the Kuhazo Lab 2014 study reported no change, creating a direct conflict in the assessment of the alloy's mechanical properties.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
