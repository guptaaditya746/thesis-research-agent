# Fatigue Life Variability in Titanium Alloys

## Problem Statement
The impact of ultrasonic versus rotating beam fatigue testing on the perceived durability of titanium alloys.

## Core Thesis
The Tofere Lab 2025 study and the Rebru Lab 2017 study show that fatigue life outcomes for alloy Stetrugel-6 are method-dependent, with one reporting improvement and the other degradation.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
