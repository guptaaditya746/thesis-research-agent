# Fatigue Life and Wear Rate Stability in Ceramic Coatings

## Problem Statement
Evaluating the stability of coating Bumom-7 under fatigue and wear conditions, with a focus on the Dresta Lab 2014 and Megrekron Lab 2021 studies.

## Core Thesis
While the Dresta Lab 2014 study reported degradation in wear rate for Bumom-7, the Megrekron Lab 2021 study found no change in fatigue life, indicating that wear mechanisms do not necessarily correlate with fatigue performance.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
