# Fatigue Life and Oxidation Resistance in Advanced Materials

## Problem Statement
Evaluating the fatigue life of Sapifin-2 and oxidation resistance of Stetrugel-6 based on Furetho Lab 2023 and Hozethe Lab 2021 data.

## Core Thesis
The Furetho Lab 2023 study reported an improvement in fatigue life for Sapifin-2, and the Hozethe Lab 2021 study reported an improvement in oxidation resistance for Stetrugel-6.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
