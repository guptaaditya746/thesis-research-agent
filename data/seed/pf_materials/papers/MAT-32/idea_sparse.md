# Divergent Wear and Hardness Findings in Ceramic Coatings

## Problem Statement
Reconciling conflicting reports on the wear rate of Kogal-8 and Bumom-7

## Core Thesis
While the Pagadol Lab 2016 study reported no change in hardness for the ceramic coatings, the Brihoher Lab 2016 study reported significant degradation in their wear rate, suggesting a trade-off between hardness and wear performance.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
