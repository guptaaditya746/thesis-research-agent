# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical review in materials science).
* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** Reconciling conflicting reports on the wear rate of Kogal-8 and Bumom-7
* **Thesis to defend:** While the Pagadol Lab 2016 study reported no change in hardness for the ceramic coatings, the Brihoher Lab 2016 study reported significant degradation in their wear rate, suggesting a trade-off between hardness and wear performance.
