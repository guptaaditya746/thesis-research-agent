# Hardness Assessment of Titanium Alloys via Indentation Methods

## Problem Statement
Consistency of hardness measurements for Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2 across different indentation techniques.

## Core Thesis
Both Stetrugel-6 and Sapifin-2 show improved hardness in recent nanoindentation and scratch tests, suggesting a robust mechanical response.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
