* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical review in materials science).
* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** Inconsistencies in reported property changes for ceramic coatings and titanium alloys across different testing methodologies.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Brudes Lab 2024 study and the Thagrogral Lab 2012 study report conflicting outcomes for their respective materials, highlighting the sensitivity of corrosion and wear metrics to specific test conditions.
