# Divergent Corrosion and Wear Findings in Recent Material Studies

## Problem Statement
Inconsistencies in reported property changes for ceramic coatings and titanium alloys across different testing methodologies.

## Core Thesis
The Brudes Lab 2024 study and the Thagrogral Lab 2012 study report conflicting outcomes for their respective materials, highlighting the sensitivity of corrosion and wear metrics to specific test conditions.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
