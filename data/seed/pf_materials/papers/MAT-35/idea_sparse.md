# Corrosion and Oxidation: Early Findings for Ceramic Coatings

## Problem Statement
Initial assessment of Kogal-8 and Bumom-7 in corrosive and high-temperature environments

## Core Thesis
The Volar Lab 2013 study and the Trebun Lab 2014 study indicate that ceramic coatings Kogal-8 and Bumom-7 show improved corrosion resistance and oxidation resistance, respectively, making them suitable for harsh environments.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
