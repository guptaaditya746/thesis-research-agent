# Corrosion Resistance Discrepancies in Titanium Alloys

## Problem Statement
Conflicting reports on the corrosion resistance of Stetrugel-6 from the Zugreden Lab 2018 and Bopor Lab 2021 studies.

## Core Thesis
The Zugreden Lab 2018 study reported an improvement in corrosion resistance for Stetrugel-6, whereas the Bopor Lab 2021 study reported no change for the same material.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
experimental evidence should be followed.
