# Argument Log

## 1. Setting
* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical review in materials science).
* **Setting:** Invented materials (alloys, coatings, compounds) studied in invented lab studies. Properties and test methods are real concepts; material names, labs and findings are invented.
* **Issue:** Conflicting reports on the corrosion resistance of Stetrugel-6 from the Zugreden Lab 2018 and Bopor Lab 2021 studies.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Zugreden Lab 2018 study reported an improvement in corrosion resistance for Stetrugel-6, whereas the Bopor Lab 2021 study reported no change for the same material.
