# Brenoxalin as first treatment in newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes

## Problem Statement
For adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug, it is not known what effect Brenoxalin has on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Brenoxalin taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change depends on the dose.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug: Brenoxalin at 20 mg and 40 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 24.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
