# Veloranide with metformin in type 2 diabetes: a 24-week trial

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effect Veloranide has on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in body weight from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Veloranide taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change depends on the dose.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin: Veloranide at 25 mg and 50 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 12 and week 24.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
