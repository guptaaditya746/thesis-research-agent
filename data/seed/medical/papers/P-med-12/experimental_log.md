# Experimental Log

## 1. Experimental Setup
* **Design:** randomised controlled trial, parallel groups, Quelmastin against sitagliptin.
* **Patients:** adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin.
* **Arms:** Quelmastin 100 mg daily; sitagliptin.
* **Time points:** week 24 and week 52.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** change in HbA1c from baseline in percentage points and change in body weight from baseline in kg.
* **Statistic:** every value is the mean difference vs sitagliptin (negative = lower than sitagliptin).
* **Baselines Compared:** sitagliptin.
