# Quelmastin versus sitagliptin added to metformin: a 52-week head-to-head trial

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effect Quelmastin has on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in body weight from baseline, compared with sitagliptin.

## Core Hypothesis
Quelmastin taken once daily changes these measures more than sitagliptin, and the change grows over time.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin: Quelmastin at 100 mg daily against sitagliptin, with each measure taken at week 24 and week 52.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs sitagliptin for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
