# Quendarin in type 2 diabetes with moderate chronic kidney disease

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes and moderate chronic kidney disease on metformin, it is not known what effect Quendarin has on the change in HbA1c from baseline, change in body weight from baseline and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Quendarin taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change depends on the dose.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes and moderate chronic kidney disease on metformin: Quendarin at 10 mg and 20 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 12 and week 24.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
