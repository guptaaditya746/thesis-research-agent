# Ordenvik with metformin: a 52-week multicentre trial

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effect Ordenvik has on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in body weight from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Ordenvik taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the change grows over time.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin: Ordenvik at 25 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 24 and week 52.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
