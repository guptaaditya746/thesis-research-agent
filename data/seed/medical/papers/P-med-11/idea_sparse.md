# Quendarin, Quelmastin and Mirabexan added to metformin: a three-drug placebo-controlled trial

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effects Quendarin (QND-631), Quelmastin (QLM-331) and Mirabexan (MRB-613) have on the change in HbA1c from baseline, change in body weight from baseline and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Quendarin (QND-631), Quelmastin (QLM-331) and Mirabexan (MRB-613) taken once daily change these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change differs between the drugs.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin: Quendarin (QND-631) at 10 mg daily, Quelmastin (QLM-331) at 100 mg daily and Mirabexan (MRB-613) at 15 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 26.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
