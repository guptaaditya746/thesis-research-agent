# Sorvelin with metformin in type 2 diabetes: a multinational 26-week trial

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effect Sorvelin has on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Sorvelin taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the change grows over time.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin: Sorvelin at 10 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 26.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
