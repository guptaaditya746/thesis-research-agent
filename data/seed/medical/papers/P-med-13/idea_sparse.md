# Dose range of Quelmastin added to metformin in type 2 diabetes

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effect Quelmastin has on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Quelmastin taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change depends on the dose.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin: Quelmastin at 25 mg, 50 mg, 100 mg and 200 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 24.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
