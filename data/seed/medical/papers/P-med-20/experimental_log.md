# Experimental Log

## 1. Experimental Setup
* **Design:** randomised controlled trial, parallel groups, ORD-399 and Veloranide against placebo.
* **Patients:** adults aged 65 years or older with type 2 diabetes on metformin.
* **Arms:** ORD-399 25 mg daily, Veloranide 25 mg daily and Veloranide 100 mg daily; placebo.
* **Time points:** week 24.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** change in HbA1c from baseline in percentage points and change in body weight from baseline in kg.
* **Statistic:** every value is the mean difference vs placebo (negative = lower than placebo).
* **Baselines Compared:** placebo.
