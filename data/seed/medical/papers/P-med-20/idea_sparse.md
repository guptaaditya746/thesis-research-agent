# ORD-399 and Veloranide in older adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin

## Problem Statement
For adults aged 65 years or older with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effects ORD-399 and Veloranide have on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in body weight from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
ORD-399 and Veloranide taken once daily change these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change differs between the drugs.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults aged 65 years or older with type 2 diabetes on metformin: ORD-399 at 25 mg daily and Veloranide at 25 mg and 100 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 24.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
