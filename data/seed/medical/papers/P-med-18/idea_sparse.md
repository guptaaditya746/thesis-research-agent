# Ordenvik (ORD-399) as first treatment in newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes: 48-week results

## Problem Statement
For adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug, it is not known what effect Ordenvik (ORD-399) has on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Ordenvik (ORD-399) taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the change grows over time.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug: Ordenvik (ORD-399) at 25 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 4, week 8, week 16, week 24 and week 48.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
