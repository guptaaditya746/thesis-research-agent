# Experimental Log

## 1. Experimental Setup
* **Design:** randomised controlled trial, parallel groups, MRB-613 against placebo.
* **Patients:** adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin.
* **Arms:** MRB-613 15 mg daily and MRB-613 30 mg daily; placebo.
* **Time points:** week 12 and week 24.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** change in HbA1c from baseline in percentage points, change in systolic blood pressure from baseline in mmHg and change in LDL cholesterol from baseline in mmol/L.
* **Statistic:** every value is the mean difference vs placebo (negative = lower than placebo).
* **Baselines Compared:** placebo.
