# Experimental Log

## 1. Experimental Setup
* **Design:** randomised controlled trial, parallel groups, Saventrol against placebo.
* **Patients:** adults with type 2 diabetes and obesity on metformin.
* **Arms:** Saventrol 10 mg daily, Saventrol 20 mg daily and Saventrol 40 mg daily; placebo.
* **Time points:** week 12 and week 26.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** change in body weight from baseline in kg, change in HbA1c from baseline in percentage points and change in systolic blood pressure from baseline in mmHg.
* **Statistic:** every value is the mean difference vs placebo (negative = lower than placebo).
* **Baselines Compared:** placebo.
