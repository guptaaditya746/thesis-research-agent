# Experimental Log

## 1. Setting
* **Paper type:** a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised trials.
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Question:** The conflicting results between the DREBRABES-4 and NAZOTO-9 trials regarding the efficacy of FGF23-targeted agents in secondary hyperparathyroidism.
* **Main conclusion to support:** While the DREBRABES-4 trial demonstrated a benefit of potrocet in reducing phosphate levels, the subsequent NAZOTO-9 trial found no effect of katrogrestinib on vascular calcification, suggesting that different mechanisms of FGF23 inhibition may yield disparate clinical outcomes in CKD patients.
