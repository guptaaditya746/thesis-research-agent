# Divergent Efficacy of FGF23-Targeted Therapies in Secondary Hyperparathyroidism: A Comparative Analysis of Early Trials

## Problem Statement
The conflicting results between the DREBRABES-4 and NAZOTO-9 trials regarding the efficacy of FGF23-targeted agents in secondary hyperparathyroidism.

## Core Thesis
While the DREBRABES-4 trial demonstrated a benefit of potrocet in reducing phosphate levels, the subsequent NAZOTO-9 trial found no effect of katrogrestinib on vascular calcification, suggesting that different mechanisms of FGF23 inhibition may yield disparate clinical outcomes in CKD patients.

## Approach
A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised trials of the trials listed in the log.

## Expected Contribution
An answer to the question: The conflicting results between the DREBRABES-4 and NAZOTO-9 trials
regarding the efficacy of FGF23-targeted agents in secondary hyperparathyroidism.
