# Divergent Efficacy Signals for CDK4/6 Inhibitors in Metastatic Breast Cancer: A Comparative Analysis of SIPEM-7 and LEFOKOL-5

## Problem Statement
The consistency of progression-free survival benefits across different generations of CDK4/6 inhibitors in HR-positive metastatic breast cancer.

## Core Thesis
While the SIPEM-7 trial established a clear benefit for lifostinib, the subsequent LEFOKOL-5 trial reported a benefit for dagostinib, creating a conflict in the interpretation of efficacy for the drug class when compared against earlier neutral or contradictory findings in the broader literature.

## Approach
A structured evidence review for clinicians of the trials listed in the log.

## Expected Contribution
An answer to the question: The consistency of progression-free survival benefits across different
generations of CDK4/6 inhibitors in HR-positive metastatic breast cancer.
