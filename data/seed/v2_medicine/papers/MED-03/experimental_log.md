# Experimental Log

## 1. Setting
* **Paper type:** a structured evidence review for clinicians.
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Question:** The consistency of progression-free survival benefits across different generations of CDK4/6 inhibitors in HR-positive metastatic breast cancer.
* **Main conclusion to support:** While the SIPEM-7 trial established a clear benefit for lifostinib, the subsequent LEFOKOL-5 trial reported a benefit for dagostinib, creating a conflict in the interpretation of efficacy for the drug class when compared against earlier neutral or contradictory findings in the broader literature.
