# Conflicting Efficacy Signals for Third-Generation EGFR TKIs: LEGEGRUM-3 vs. GRENAM-7

## Problem Statement
The LEGEGRUM-3 trial (2020) reported a benefit for the third-generation TKI krethebrutinib, while the subsequent GRENAM-7 trial (2022) reported no effect for the second-generation TKI nukokrontinib, creating a discrepancy in the perceived value of advanced EGFR TKIs.

## Core Thesis
The LEGEGRUM-3 trial found a benefit of krethebrutinib, whereas the GRENAM-7 trial found no effect of nukokrontinib, indicating that the efficacy of EGFR TKIs is highly dependent on the specific agent and trial context.
