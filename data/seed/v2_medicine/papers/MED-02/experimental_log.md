# Experimental Log

## 1. Setting
* **Paper type:** a brief meta-analysis report.
* **Setting:** Invented treatments tested in invented clinical trials. Diseases, outcome measures and trial designs are real concepts; drug names, trial names, sponsors and results are invented.
* **Question:** The LEGEGRUM-3 trial (2020) reported a benefit for the third-generation TKI krethebrutinib, while the subsequent GRENAM-7 trial (2022) reported no effect for the second-generation TKI nukokrontinib, creating a discrepancy in the perceived value of advanced EGFR TKIs.
* **Main conclusion to support:** The LEGEGRUM-3 trial found a benefit of krethebrutinib, whereas the GRENAM-7 trial found no effect of nukokrontinib, indicating that the efficacy of EGFR TKIs is highly dependent on the specific agent and trial context.
