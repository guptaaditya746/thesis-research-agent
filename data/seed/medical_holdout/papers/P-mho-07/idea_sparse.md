# Tembrexil as first treatment in newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes: 60-week results

## Problem Statement
For adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug, it is not known what effect Tembrexil has on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in body weight from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Tembrexil taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the change grows over time.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug: Tembrexil at 30 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 6, week 12, week 24, week 36 and week 60.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
