# Experimental Log

## 1. Experimental Setup
* **Design:** randomised controlled trial, parallel groups, Tembrexil against placebo.
* **Patients:** adults aged 70 years or older with type 2 diabetes on metformin.
* **Arms:** Tembrexil 45 mg daily; placebo.
* **Time points:** week 24.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** change in HbA1c from baseline in percentage points and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline in mmol/L.
* **Statistic:** every value is the mean difference vs placebo (negative = lower than placebo).
* **Baselines Compared:** placebo.
