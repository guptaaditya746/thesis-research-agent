# Saventrol in type 2 diabetes with obesity: two-year follow-up

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes and obesity on metformin, it is not known what effect Saventrol has on the change in body weight from baseline and change in HbA1c from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Saventrol taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change depends on the dose.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes and obesity on metformin: Saventrol at 20 mg and 40 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 52 and week 104.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
