# PMT-774 added to metformin: effects on blood pressure and LDL cholesterol

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effect PMT-774 has on the change in systolic blood pressure from baseline and change in LDL cholesterol from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
PMT-774 taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the change grows over time.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin: PMT-774 at 6 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 12 and week 24.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
