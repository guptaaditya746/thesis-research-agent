# Experimental Log

## 1. Experimental Setup
* **Design:** randomised controlled trial, parallel groups, Tavorindel (TVR-676) and Parvelinor against placebo.
* **Patients:** adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug.
* **Arms:** Tavorindel (TVR-676) 10 mg daily and Parvelinor 50 mg daily; placebo.
* **Time points:** week 26.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** change in HbA1c from baseline in percentage points and change in body weight from baseline in kg.
* **Statistic:** every value is the mean difference vs placebo (negative = lower than placebo).
* **Baselines Compared:** placebo.
