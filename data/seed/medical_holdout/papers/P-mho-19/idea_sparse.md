# Tavorindel (TVR-676) and Parvelinor as first treatment in newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes

## Problem Statement
For adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug, it is not known what effects Tavorindel (TVR-676) and Parvelinor have on the change in HbA1c from baseline and change in body weight from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Tavorindel (TVR-676) and Parvelinor taken once daily change these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change differs between the drugs.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes, no prior drug: Tavorindel (TVR-676) at 10 mg daily and Parvelinor at 50 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 26.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
