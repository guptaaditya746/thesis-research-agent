# Saventrol for glucose and body weight in type 2 diabetes with obesity

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes and obesity on metformin, it is not known what effect Saventrol has on the change in body weight from baseline, change in HbA1c from baseline and change in systolic blood pressure from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Saventrol taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change depends on the dose.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes and obesity on metformin: Saventrol at 10 mg, 20 mg and 40 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 12 and week 26.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
