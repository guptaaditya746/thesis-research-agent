# Tavorindel added to basal insulin in type 2 diabetes: a 26-week trial

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on basal insulin, it is not known what effect Tavorindel has on the change in HbA1c from baseline, change in body weight from baseline and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Tavorindel taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change depends on the dose.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on basal insulin: Tavorindel at 5 mg, 10 mg and 20 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 13 and week 26.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
