# Experimental Log

## 1. Experimental Setup
* **Design:** randomised controlled trial, parallel groups, Saventrol against placebo.
* **Patients:** adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin.
* **Arms:** Saventrol 5 mg daily, Saventrol 10 mg daily, Saventrol 20 mg daily, Saventrol 40 mg daily and Saventrol 80 mg daily; placebo.
* **Time points:** week 24.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** change in body weight from baseline in kg and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline in mmol/L.
* **Statistic:** every value is the mean difference vs placebo (negative = lower than placebo).
* **Baselines Compared:** placebo.
