# Tembrexil, Dulmarexa and Saventrol added to metformin: a three-arm placebo-controlled trial

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin, it is not known what effects Tembrexil (TMB-399), Dulmarexa (DLM-868) and Saventrol (SVN-171) have on the change in HbA1c from baseline, change in body weight from baseline and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Tembrexil (TMB-399), Dulmarexa (DLM-868) and Saventrol (SVN-171) taken once daily change these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change differs between the drugs.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin: Tembrexil (TMB-399) at 30 mg daily, Dulmarexa (DLM-868) at 200 mg daily and Saventrol (SVN-171) at 20 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 26.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
