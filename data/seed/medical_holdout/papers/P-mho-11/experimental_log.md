# Experimental Log

## 1. Experimental Setup
* **Design:** randomised controlled trial, parallel groups, Tembrexil (TMB-399), Dulmarexa (DLM-868) and Saventrol (SVN-171) against placebo.
* **Patients:** adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin.
* **Arms:** Tembrexil (TMB-399) 30 mg daily, Dulmarexa (DLM-868) 200 mg daily and Saventrol (SVN-171) 20 mg daily; placebo.
* **Time points:** week 26.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** change in HbA1c from baseline in percentage points, change in body weight from baseline in kg and change in fasting plasma glucose from baseline in mmol/L.
* **Statistic:** every value is the mean difference vs placebo (negative = lower than placebo).
* **Baselines Compared:** placebo.
