# Pomitral for blood pressure and cholesterol in type 2 diabetes with high blood pressure

## Problem Statement
For adults with type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure on metformin, it is not known what effect Pomitral has on the change in systolic blood pressure from baseline, change in LDL cholesterol from baseline and change in HbA1c from baseline, compared with placebo.

## Core Hypothesis
Pomitral taken once daily changes these measures more than placebo, and the size of the change depends on the dose.

## Proposed Methodology (High-Level Technical Approach)
We propose a randomised controlled trial in adults with type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure on metformin: Pomitral at 3 mg and 6 mg daily against placebo, with each measure taken at week 12 and week 24.

## Expected Contribution
The mean difference vs placebo for each measure, dose and time point, so readers can choose a dose.
