# Revisiting Theme Probability Scoring: A Replication Study on Topic Classification

## Problem Statement
Does the Gredapos prompting method consistently improve topic classification performance, or are earlier gains artifacts of small sample sizes?

## Core Thesis
The initial reported gain from Gredapos prompting in the Netras 2021 study is not robust; a full-scale re-evaluation in the Brastom 2023 study shows no significant improvement, suggesting the method's effectiveness is context-dependent or negligible.

## Approach
A re-analysis of published evaluation results of the studies listed in the log.
