# Reproducibility and Variability in Neural Keyword Extraction: A Comparative Study

## Problem Statement
Inconsistencies in the reported effectiveness of neural keyword extraction methods across different models and evaluation settings.

## Core Thesis
The performance of neural keyword extraction methods is unstable, with recent studies failing to replicate earlier gains and reporting no significant improvement over baselines.

## Approach
A position paper backed by pooled evidence of the studies listed in the log.

## Expected Contribution
An answer to the question: Inconsistencies in the reported effectiveness of neural keyword
extraction methods across different models and evaluation settings.
