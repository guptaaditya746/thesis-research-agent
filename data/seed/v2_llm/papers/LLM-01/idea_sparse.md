# Divergent Outcomes in Morphological Tagging and Translation Replication

## Problem Statement
The reliability of part-of-speech prompting in low-resource translation and the consistency of zero-shot tagging performance across different model architectures.

## Core Thesis
While the Vofal 2024 study demonstrates that the Thoradram-13B model achieves a gain in part-of-speech tagging accuracy using the Kabaso prompting method, the Donar 2025 study fails to replicate the earlier positive findings of the Nodrana 2022 study regarding the same method's impact on translation quality.

## Approach
An empirical comparison of reported results of the studies listed in the log.

## Expected Contribution
An answer to the question: The reliability of part-of-speech prompting in low-resource translation
and the consistency of zero-shot tagging performance across different model architectures.
