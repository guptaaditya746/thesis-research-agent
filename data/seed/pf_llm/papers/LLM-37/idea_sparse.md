# Adapter Efficacy in Fine-Tuned Arithmetic: A Comparative Analysis

## Problem Statement
Does the Tedroso adapter method provide consistent benefits in fine-tuned arithmetic reasoning compared to prompting strategies?

## Core Thesis
The Tedroso adapter method demonstrates superior stability in fine-tuned settings for arithmetic tasks, whereas prompting methods show inconsistent or negative effects.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
