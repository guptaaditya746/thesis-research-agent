# Conflicting Evidence on Prompting and Decoding in Zero-Shot QA

## Problem Statement
The contradictory findings regarding the effectiveness of prompting and decoding methods in zero-shot multi-hop question answering.

## Core Thesis
There is a direct conflict in the literature regarding the utility of Kabaso prompting and Ragrer decoding in zero-shot QA, with some studies reporting gains and others reporting no gain or drops, highlighting the instability of these methods in this setting.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
