# The Fragility of Early Code Generation Claims: A Replication Crisis

## Problem Statement
Why do recent studies fail to replicate the positive results reported in the foundational Bruke 2019 study on code generation?

## Core Thesis
The apparent gains from decoding methods in early code generation studies are artifacts of specific evaluation settings that do not generalize to later, more rigorous zero-shot evaluations.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
