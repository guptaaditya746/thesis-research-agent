# Divergent Outcomes for Retrieval and Prompting in Large-Scale Zero-Shot Evaluation

## Problem Statement
The reliability of Thoradram retrieval and Thetran prompting on the Nodrana-70B model across different benchmarks and settings.

## Core Thesis
Thoradram retrieval yields inconsistent results on the Vofal-Math benchmark, while Thetran prompting shows no gain on the Mupadem-QA benchmark in zero-shot settings.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
