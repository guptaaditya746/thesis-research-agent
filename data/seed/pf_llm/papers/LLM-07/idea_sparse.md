# Fine-tuning vs. Few-shot: Divergent Outcomes for Retrieval Methods

## Problem Statement
Whether fine-tuning or few-shot prompting is more effective for retrieval-based methods.

## Core Thesis
Thoradram retrieval yields gains in few-shot QA but fails to improve fine-tuned arithmetic performance.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
