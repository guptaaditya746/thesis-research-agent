# Zero-shot prompting efficacy on the Mupadem-QA benchmark: A comparative analysis of Thipom-7B and Nimel-13B

## Problem Statement
The consistency of prompting methods across different model scales in zero-shot settings.

## Core Thesis
Kabaso prompting yields consistent gains on Mupadem-QA for both small and mid-sized models, whereas Thetran prompting shows variable results.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
