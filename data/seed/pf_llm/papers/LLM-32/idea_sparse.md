# Decoding Strategies for Fine-Tuned Small Models: Gains and Drops

## Problem Statement
How do different decoding methods (Ragrer vs Gofabran) affect the performance of the Thipom-7B model on the Donar-Code benchmark when fine-tuned?

## Core Thesis
The Ragrer decoding method causes a performance drop in fine-tuned settings, whereas the Gofabran decoding method provides a gain.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
