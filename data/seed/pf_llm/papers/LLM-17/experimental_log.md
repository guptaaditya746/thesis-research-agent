* **Genre:** argument paper (a critical survey of language model research).

Tasks, metrics and evaluation settings (zero-shot, few-shot, fine-tuned) are real concepts; model names, method names, benchmark names, authors and results are invented.
* **Issue:** The reproducibility of early few-shot findings on Thipom-7B and the efficacy of Tedroso adapter on Nodrana-70B.
* **Thesis to defend:** The Fistem 2021 study failed to replicate the Brulum 2020 findings, and the Bredul 2021 study reported a gain from Tedroso adapter on the Donar-Code benchmark.
