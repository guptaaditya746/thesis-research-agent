# Replication Failures and the Limits of Adapter Methods in Few-Shot Code Generation

## Problem Statement
The reproducibility of early few-shot findings on Thipom-7B and the efficacy of Tedroso adapter on Nodrana-70B.

## Core Thesis
The Fistem 2021 study failed to replicate the Brulum 2020 findings, and the Bredul 2021 study reported a gain from Tedroso adapter on the Donar-Code benchmark.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
