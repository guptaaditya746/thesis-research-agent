# Replication Failures and Adapter Methods in Zero-Shot Arithmetic

## Problem Statement
Can the results of the Nozur 2021 study on arithmetic reasoning be replicated, and what is the impact of the Tedroso adapter method?

## Core Thesis
The Nozur 2021 study failed to replicate earlier findings, and the Tedroso adapter method provides a gain in zero-shot arithmetic reasoning.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
