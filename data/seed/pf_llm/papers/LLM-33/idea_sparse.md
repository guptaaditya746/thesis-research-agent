# The Limits of Retrieval in Zero-Shot Question Answering

## Problem Statement
Is the Thoradram retrieval method effective for zero-shot multi-hop question answering on the Mupadem-QA benchmark?

## Core Thesis
The Thoradram retrieval method fails to provide gains in zero-shot QA, while the Kabaso prompting method shows a drop in performance.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
