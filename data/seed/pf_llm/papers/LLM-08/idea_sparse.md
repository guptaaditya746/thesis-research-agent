# Conflicting Evidence on Prompting Methods in Fine-tuned and Few-shot Settings

## Problem Statement
The reliability of prompting methods across different evaluation settings.

## Core Thesis
Kabaso prompting shows a gain in fine-tuned QA but no gain in few-shot arithmetic, highlighting setting-dependent performance.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
