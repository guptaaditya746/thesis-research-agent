# Prompting Efficacy in Few-Shot vs Zero-Shot Multi-Hop QA

## Problem Statement
How prompting methods perform differently when moving from few-shot to zero-shot settings on multi-hop question answering.

## Core Thesis
Kabaso prompting shows a gain in few-shot settings but fails to provide a gain in zero-shot settings for the same benchmark, suggesting a dependency on demonstration examples.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
