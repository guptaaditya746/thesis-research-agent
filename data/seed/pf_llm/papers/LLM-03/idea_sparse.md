# Adapter Fine-Tuning vs. Prompting in Multi-Hop QA

## Problem Statement
The comparative effectiveness of fine-tuning adapters versus prompting in complex question answering.

## Core Thesis
Adapter-based fine-tuning (Tedroso) offers superior performance gains in multi-hop QA compared to prompting methods, which often fail to replicate earlier positive results.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
