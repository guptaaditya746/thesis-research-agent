# Zero-Shot Stability of Gofabran Decoding and Thoradram Retrieval

## Problem Statement
The performance of Gofabran decoding and Thoradram retrieval in zero-shot settings on Thipom-7B and Nimel-13B.

## Core Thesis
Gofabran decoding shows no gain in zero-shot settings on both Thipom-7B and Nimel-13B, while Thoradram retrieval also fails to show gains in these specific zero-shot evaluations.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
