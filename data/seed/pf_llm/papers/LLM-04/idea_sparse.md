# Conflicting Evidence on Retrieval-Augmented Decoding

## Problem Statement
The stability of retrieval and decoding method impacts across different evaluation settings.

## Core Thesis
There is a direct conflict in the literature regarding the impact of retrieval methods, with one study reporting a gain and another reporting no gain for the same method on the same benchmark.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
