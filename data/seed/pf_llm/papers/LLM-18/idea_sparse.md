# Conflicting Evidence for Ragrer Decoding in Fine-Tuned and Few-Shot Settings

## Problem Statement
Disagreement between recent fine-tuned and historical few-shot studies regarding the impact of Ragrer decoding on Thipom-7B.

## Core Thesis
The Dugrudis 2026 study reported a drop with Ragrer decoding, while the Sakem 2020 study reported a gain from Ragrer decoding, creating a direct conflict in the literature.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
