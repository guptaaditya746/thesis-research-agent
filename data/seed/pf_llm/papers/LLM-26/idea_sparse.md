# Divergent Outcomes for Adapter and Retrieval Methods in Code Generation

## Problem Statement
The inconsistent effectiveness of fine-tuning and retrieval strategies on the Donar-Code benchmark across different model scales and evaluation settings.

## Core Thesis
While Tedroso adapter shows promise in few-shot settings for mid-sized models, Thoradram retrieval fails to provide consistent gains in fine-tuned settings for smaller models, suggesting a trade-off between parameter efficiency and task-specific adaptation.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
