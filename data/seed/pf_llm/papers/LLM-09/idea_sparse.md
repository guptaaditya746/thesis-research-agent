# Zero-shot Performance of Adapter and Decoding Methods on Nimel-13B

## Problem Statement
The impact of parameter-efficient fine-tuning and decoding strategies in zero-shot scenarios.

## Core Thesis
Tedroso adapter improves zero-shot code generation, while Ragrer decoding does not affect arithmetic reasoning.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
