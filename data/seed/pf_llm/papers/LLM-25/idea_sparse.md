# Adapter Methods and Replication Challenges in Few-Shot Evaluation

## Problem Statement
The role of adapter methods in few-shot settings and the difficulty in replicating earlier zero-shot findings.

## Core Thesis
The Tedroso adapter method provides consistent gains in few-shot settings, while the Brulum 2020 study's findings on prompting are not replicated by the Steferis 2022 study.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
