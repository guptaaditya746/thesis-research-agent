# The Replication Crisis in Large Model Evaluation: A Case Study of Nodrana-70B

## Problem Statement
The failure of subsequent studies to replicate the initial findings of the Grodrokris 2021 study on the Nodrana-70B model.

## Core Thesis
The Grodrokris 2021 study's findings were not replicated by the Steferis 2022 and Brastom 2021 studies, and the Stutohas 2023 study reported no gain from Thetran prompting on the Vofal-Math benchmark.

## Approach
An analysis of the studies listed in the log, read against the sources they deal with.

## Expected Contribution
A clear account of how the studies treated these sources, and an argued view on which line of
empirical evidence should be followed.
